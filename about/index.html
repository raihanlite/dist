About

StudyKoro helps Bangladeshi students study abroad — with clear, honest, practical guidance in simple language.

What we do

Studying abroad is one of the biggest decisions a student from Bangladesh will make. It costs lakhs of taka, takes months of preparation, and the rules change every year. StudyKoro exists to make that planning possible without an agent: which country fits your budget, which scholarships you can actually apply for, how admissions and visas work, and what everything costs.

We cover:

  • Countries — the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Japan, China, Malaysia and more: tuition, living costs, work rights and post-study options.
  • Scholarships — MEXT, DAAD, Chevening, Australia Awards, CSC, Commonwealth and others: what they cover, who can apply, and when to apply.
  • Admissions — IELTS and TOEFL, statements of purpose, application timelines and GPA.
  • Student visas — fees, documents, financial proof and interviews, step by step.
  • Costs — honest first-year budgets and a free cost estimator.

How we work

Every fee, deadline and requirement on this site is checked against an official source — the embassy, the government, or the scholarship body — before it is published. When a rule is changing or unclear, we say so instead of guessing. When we cannot verify a number, we leave it out. Fees and rules change often, so every guide carries a "last checked" date and tells you to confirm the current figure on the official source before you pay anything.

What we are not

StudyKoro is not an agency and not a consultancy. We do not process applications, guarantee admissions or visas, or take payment for placements. The guides here help you apply yourself — directly to universities, scholarship bodies and embassies.

Who runs StudyKoro

StudyKoro is an independent project from Bangladesh, run by students and graduates who went through the same process. If you spot an error or an outdated figure, tell us — corrections make the site better for the next student.
