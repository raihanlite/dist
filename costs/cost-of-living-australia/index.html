Cost of Living in Australia for Bangladeshi Students

24 Nov 2026 · 7 min read · Last checked 24 Nov 2026

Australia is the most expensive of the eight countries on paper, but the range is wide: Adelaide costs about AUD $1,430–$2,560 a month, while Sydney costs AUD $1,690–$3,030. This guide breaks down the real monthly cost of living in Australia for international students: rent, food, transport, bills and the one-off costs of arriving. It is part of the cost of living abroad hub, and the figures here feed the full comparison across eight countries.

Accommodation

Rent is the cost that moves your budget most, taking about 60% of it. A private room in a shared house typically costs AUD $180–$400 a week. Sydney rooms run $280–$420 a week and studios $600–$850, while Melbourne rooms run $230–$350 a week and studios $450–$650. Purpose-built student accommodation is simpler to arrange from Dhaka but usually costs more than a share house. Expect to pay a rental bond (usually four weeks' rent) before you move in. Sharing a house with other students, living a little further from the city centre, and choosing Adelaide or Brisbane over Sydney are the three biggest rent savers. For the full picture on universities, visas and work rules, read the Australia country guide and the Australia student visa guide.

Food

A week's groceries for one person typically costs AUD $80–$150, depending on what you buy and where you shop. Cooking at home keeps food near $300–$450 a month; eating out often pushes it past $600–$900. A cafe lunch costs $15–$25, a mid-range restaurant dinner $30–$60, and a flat white $5–$7. Halal meat, rice, lentils and spices are easy to find in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane, and South Asian grocery shops often beat supermarket prices on those staples. Cooking in bulk with housemates and shopping at ALDI or Asian grocers keeps this line under control.

Transport

Transport costs depend heavily on which state you study in, because concession rules for international students differ in every state. In Brisbane, every trip costs 50 cents, so transport can fall under AUD $30 a month. In Sydney, most full-fee international students cannot get concession fares, so regular travel costs around $150–$220 a month. In Melbourne, public transport is half price for everyone, with some universities offering an International Student Travel Pass for a further discount. Confirm your eligibility with your university and your state's transport authority before you budget. Compare the full first-year bill, tuition included, in the cost of studying abroad comparison.

One-off arrival costs

Budget for these before you fly. A rental bond (four weeks' rent, roughly AUD $1,000–$1,700), the flight from Dhaka, and basic setup items for your room add roughly AUD $2,000–$3,500 on top of your first month's living costs. The student visa (subclass 500) costs AUD $2,500, and Overseas Student Health Cover is paid upfront for the whole visa, usually several hundred dollars a year. The proof-of-funds figure (AUD $29,710 a year) is the minimum you must show for the visa, not necessarily what you will spend: Sydney students spend above it, Adelaide students can spend below it.

How to keep costs down

Live in Adelaide or Brisbane rather than Sydney if your course allows it: the rent gap alone is hundreds a month. Share a house, cook at home, and learn your state's concession rules on day one. Students can work up to 48 hours a fortnight during term, which typically covers food and transport. Buy a second-hand bicycle for short trips, and check whether your university's accommodation service can place you in a share house before you arrive, so you are not paying for a hotel or hostel while you search. Finally, run your own numbers with the study-abroad cost estimator, using your university's actual costs instead of national averages.

Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need per month to live in Australia as a student?

Most international students spend AUD $1,450–$3,330 a month, excluding tuition. Adelaide sits near $1,430–$2,560; Sydney sits near $1,690–$3,030.

Is AUD $2,000 a month enough in Australia?

In Adelaide, Brisbane or Melbourne, yes, if you share a house and cook at home. In Sydney it is tight: a room alone can cost $1,200–$1,800 a month.

How much money must I show for an Australia student visa?

AUD $29,710 a year for living costs (about $2,476 a month), in force since 10 May 2024, plus tuition and travel costs. That is the amount you must show, not necessarily what you will spend.

What is the cheapest student city in Australia?

Adelaide is the cheapest big student city, at about AUD $1,430–$2,560 a month, because rents there are lower than in Sydney or Melbourne.

Can part-time work cover my living costs in Australia?

Partly. Up to 48 hours a fortnight during term typically covers food and transport. Rent needs savings, family support or a scholarship.
