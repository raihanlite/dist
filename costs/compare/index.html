Cost of Studying Abroad from Bangladesh Compared — USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Japan, China, Malaysia

19 Nov 2026 · 8 min read · Last checked 19 Nov 2026

Tuition is only part of the cost. The real first-year bill is tuition plus living costs, the visa, health insurance, and flights — and the cheapest tuition can hide the most expensive visa. This guide compares eight destinations on the same basis, with the same five costs, so you can see the full picture before you choose. Every figure is either an official number or a clearly labelled estimate; use the cost estimator to run your own numbers with your university's actual fees.

How to read this table

Tuition is a range, not a price

Tuition varies more within a country than between countries. A US state university can cost $20,000 while a private one costs $60,000; a UK university outside London can be £15,000 while a London business school is £30,000. Always use the exact fee on your offer letter, not a national average. The cost estimator lets you type in your real tuition and see the total change.

Living costs: official proof vs real spending

For the UK, Canada, Australia and Germany, the living figure is the official amount the visa requires you to show — it is a minimum, not a budget. Real spending in London, Sydney or Toronto is usually higher. For the USA, Japan, China and Malaysia there is no official national figure: the USA uses the amount on your I-20, and the others are estimates. Budget above the minimum, not at it.

The visa can be the second-biggest cost

Australia's visa charge (AUD $2,500) is more than ten times Canada's (CAD $235). The UK's headline fee (£558) looks small until you add the health surcharge (£776 a year, paid upfront for the whole visa). Germany's €75 visa is the cheapest of the eight. Always count the visa plus the health surcharge plus biometrics together — never the application fee alone.

Budgeting tips

1. Start with the offer letter, not a ranking

Your university's fee and your city decide the real number. Two students in the same country can have first-year totals ৳20 lakh apart. Use the estimator with your actual offer.

2. Keep a buffer above the visa minimum

Visa officers check exact amounts — a shortfall of £1 or CAD $1 means refusal. Hold 5–10% more than the required figure, and hold it for the full required period (28 days for the UK; six months of statements for Canada).

3. Do not fund the plan with part-time work

Part-time work covers some living costs once you are there, but no visa officer will accept "I will work" as proof of funds, and work is capped everywhere (20 hours a week in the UK, 24 in Canada, 48 per fortnight in Australia). Plan as if you earn nothing in your first semester.

4. Watch the exchange rate

The taka has weakened through 2026 — the US dollar was around ৳124.50 on 19 November 2026, and the pound around ৳163.50. A 5% move in the rate adds lakhs to a multi-year degree. If your family holds savings in taka, budget for the rate at the time you pay, not the rate when you started planning.

5. Compare the whole degree, not the first year

A one-year UK master's looks expensive per year but costs one year of living. A three-year bachelor's in the USA or Australia costs three first years. Multiply the annual total by the length of the course before you compare.

6. Apply for scholarships first

Full scholarships close 8–12 months before the course starts. If you shortlist universities first and check scholarships later, you will miss the deadlines. Check the scholarship guides at the same time as the country guides.
