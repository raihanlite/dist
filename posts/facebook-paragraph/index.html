Facebook Paragraph for Class 6,7,8,9,10, HSC

January 20, 2026 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find a Facebook paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Facebook Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Facebook is a social networking service that connects people across the world. Users can create a profile, add friends, share photographs and videos, join groups, follow news pages, and send messages. It helps relatives and friends who live far apart stay in touch, and it helps students learn new things through educational pages and groups. Small businesses also use Facebook to show their products and reach customers.

However, Facebook has some dangers. False news spreads quickly, and strangers can misuse personal information. Spending too much time scrolling can waste study time and disturb sleep. Users should keep their privacy settings strong, accept friend requests carefully, and never share passwords or personal details with strangers. They should check a news item before sharing it and use polite language in comments. Facebook is useful when it is used for learning and communication, but it becomes harmful when it replaces study, sleep, and real friendship.

Facebook Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Facebook is a social networking service that connects people across the world. A user can create a profile, add friends, share photographs and videos, join groups, follow news pages, and send messages. It helps relatives and friends who live far apart stay in touch, and it helps students learn new things through educational pages and groups. Teachers share lessons and notices, and small businesses use Facebook to show their products and reach customers without opening a shop.

But Facebook also has serious dangers. False news spreads quickly, and people may believe wrong information. Strangers can misuse personal details and photographs, and some people use fake accounts to cheat others. Too much scrolling wastes study time, disturbs sleep, and makes students less interested in books and outdoor games. Arguments in comments can also create quarrels among friends.

So Facebook must be used with care. Users should keep their privacy settings strong, accept friend requests only from people they know, and never share passwords, addresses, or personal details with strangers. They should check a news item before sharing it, report harassment and suspicious links, and use polite language in comments. Setting a daily time limit for Facebook is also wise. Facebook is useful when it serves learning and communication, but it becomes harmful when it replaces study, sleep, and real friendship. Parents can also help by talking with their children about what they see online and by setting family rules for screen time. A short daily limit keeps Facebook a tool, not a master.

Facebook Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Facebook is a social networking service that connects people across the world. A user can create a profile, add friends, share photographs and videos, join groups, follow news pages, and send messages. It helps relatives and friends who live far apart stay in touch, and it helps students learn new things through educational pages and groups. Teachers share lessons, notices, and study materials, while small businesses use Facebook to show their products and reach customers without opening a shop. During natural disasters and emergencies, it also helps people share information quickly and ask for help.

But Facebook also has serious dangers. False news spreads quickly, and people may believe wrong information and share it again. Strangers can misuse personal details and photographs, and some people use fake accounts to cheat others or to bully them. Too much scrolling wastes study time, disturbs sleep, and makes students less interested in books, games, and family life. Arguments in comments can create quarrels among friends, and comparing one's own life with the happy pictures of others can create sadness and jealousy.

So Facebook must be used with care and self-control. Users should keep their privacy settings strong, accept friend requests only from people they know, and never share passwords, addresses, or personal details with strangers. They should check a news item before sharing it, ask permission before posting another person's photograph, and report harassment and suspicious links. Setting a daily time limit for Facebook is also wise, and parents should know how their children use it. Facebook is a useful tool when it serves learning and communication, but it becomes harmful when it replaces study, sleep, and real friendship. Schools can teach digital literacy so that students learn to check sources and protect their privacy from an early age. This is a modern life skill.

Bangla Meaning of Facebook Paragraph

ফেসবুক অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

ফেসবুক একটি সামাজিক যোগাযোগ মাধ্যম, যা সারা বিশ্বের মানুষকে একে অপরের সঙ্গে যুক্ত করে। এতে প্রোফাইল তৈরি করে বন্ধু যোগ করা যায়, ছবি ও ভিডিও শেয়ার করা যায়, গ্রুপে যোগ দেওয়া যায় এবং বার্তা পাঠানো যায়। দূরে থাকা আত্মীয় ও বন্ধুদের সঙ্গে যোগাযোগ রাখতে এবং শিক্ষামূলক পেজ থেকে নতুন কিছু শিখতে এটি সাহায্য করে। ছোট ব্যবসায়ীরাও তাদের পণ্য দেখাতে ফেসবুক ব্যবহার করে।

তবে ফেসবুকের কিছু বিপদও আছে। ভুয়া খবর দ্রুত ছড়িয়ে পড়ে এবং অপরিচিত মানুষ ব্যক্তিগত তথ্যের অপব্যবহার করতে পারে। বেশি সময় স্ক্রল করলে পড়াশোনার সময় নষ্ট হয় এবং ঘুমের ব্যাঘাত ঘটে। তাই গোপনীয়তার সেটিংস শক্ত রাখা, অপরিচিতদের বন্ধুর অনুরোধ গ্রহণ না করা এবং পাসওয়ার্ড বা ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য কাউকে না দেওয়া উচিত। খবর শেয়ার করার আগে তা যাচাই করা এবং মন্তব্যে ভদ্র ভাষা ব্যবহার করা উচিত। শেখা ও যোগাযোগের কাজে ব্যবহার করলে ফেসবুক উপকারী, কিন্তু পড়াশোনা, ঘুম ও আসল বন্ধুত্বের জায়গা দখল করলে তা ক্ষতিকর হয়ে ওঠে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the main points — uses, dangers, and safe use — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Keep one paragraph in one block. Do not break it into many small paragraphs unless the question asks for it.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words first.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
