The Ant and the Grasshopper Completing Story

October 12, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

This is the completing story of the ant and the grasshopper, written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The given beginning is: "Once there lived an ant and a grasshopper in a field. The ant was hard-working, but the grasshopper loved to sing and play all day." Read the complete story below, then learn the moral, the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end. The story shows why we should prepare in good times.

The Ant and the Grasshopper (Complete Story)

Once there lived an ant and a grasshopper in a field. The ant was hard-working, but the grasshopper loved to sing and play all day. Throughout the warm months, the ant carried grains to a dry store beneath a tree. It worked a little every day, and it did not refuse rest or happiness; it simply prepared before the weather changed.

Nearby, the grasshopper spent the days singing and resting in the sun. One day, the ant invited him to gather food before winter. The grasshopper laughed and replied that there was plenty of time. "Why should I work in this fine weather?" he said. "Summer will last for many more days."

The ant said nothing and continued its work. It stored wheat, rice and seeds in its safe store. Sometimes it stopped to rest in the shade, but it never forgot the coming winter. The grasshopper, on the other hand, sang from morning to evening. It believed that food would always be easy to find.

One afternoon, the grasshopper asked the ant why it worked so hard when the sun was shining. The ant answered that summer food would not last forever, and a wise worker prepares for the season that is coming. The grasshopper only laughed and sang a louder song. It danced in the grass and told the ant to enjoy the summer while it lasted.

Soon the warm days ended. Cold rain covered the fields, insects disappeared, and the grasshopper found nothing to eat. Hungry and shivering, it went to the ant's shelter and asked for help.

The ant shared a small meal but explained that kindness could not replace planning. "I warned you in summer," it said gently. "You must gather food whenever spring returns and balance enjoyment with responsibility."

The grasshopper understood that it had confused leisure with a life without duties. It thanked the ant and promised to change. When warm weather came again, it worked beside the ant each morning and sang only after storing enough food. The two friends remained helpful to one another, and the grasshopper never forgot the lesson of that cold winter. The ant smiled and welcomed its friend, because a good neighbour helps, but a good worker also remembers the lesson.

Moral

Prepare during good times, and do not sacrifice necessary work for immediate pleasure.

Bangla Meaning of the Story

গল্পটির বাংলা অর্থ

এক মাঠে একটি পিঁপড়া ও একটি ফড়িং থাকত। পিঁপড়াটি ছিল পরিশ্রমী, আর ফড়িংটি সারাদিন গান গেয়ে ও খেলে বেড়াতে ভালোবাসত। গরমের দিনগুলোতে পিঁপড়া প্রতিদিন একটু একটু করে শস্য জমা করত, যাতে শীতকালে খাবারের অভাব না হয়।

ফড়িং পিঁপড়ার পরামর্শ শুনল না; সে বলল, অনেক সময় আছে। কিন্তু শীত এলে মাঠে আর খাবার পাওয়া গেল না। ক্ষুধার্ত ও ঠান্ডায় কাঁপতে কাঁপতে ফড়িং পিঁপড়ার কাছে সাহায্য চাইল। পিঁপড়া তাকে অল্প খাবার দিল, তবে বুঝিয়ে দিল যে দয়া পরিকল্পনার বিকল্প হতে পারে না। ফড়িং তার ভুল বুঝল। পরের বছর থেকে সে সকালে পিঁপড়ার পাশে কাজ করত এবং যথেষ্ট খাবার জমা করার পরেই গান গাইত।

এক বিকেলে ফড়িং পিঁপড়াকে জিজ্ঞেস করল, রোদ ঝলমলে দিনে সে এত কষ্ট করে কেন। পিঁপড়া বলল, গ্রীষ্মের খাবার চিরদিন থাকবে না; বুদ্ধিমান কর্মী আগামী ঋতুর জন্য প্রস্তুতি নেয়। ফড়িং শুধু হাসল এবং আরও জোরে গান গাইল। সে ঘাসের উপর নাচল এবং পিঁপড়াকে গ্রীষ্ম উপভোগ করতে বলল।

Exam Tips

  • Begin the story with the given beginning exactly as it is written in the question, then continue it in your own words.
  • Keep the events in order: the ant works in summer, the grasshopper sings, winter comes, the grasshopper asks for help, and the grasshopper changes its habit.
  • Write in the past tense, because the story happened in the past.
  • Do not change the main idea of the story. The grasshopper must suffer in winter because it did not prepare in summer.
  • End the story with the moral in a separate short paragraph, as shown above.
  • Show the contrast clearly: the ant works while the grasshopper sings, and the grasshopper suffers while the ant is safe in winter.
