Our National Flag Paragraph for Class 6, 7, 8, SSC and HSC

September 19, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find our national flag paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Our National Flag Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A national flag is the sign of a country's identity and independence. Our national flag is a green rectangle with a red circle placed towards the hoist. The green field stands for the lush land of Bangladesh, its youth and vitality. The red circle stands for the rising sun over Bengal and the blood of the martyrs who died for our freedom.

Our present flag was adopted on 17 January 1972. It was designed by the artist Quamrul Hassan. An earlier wartime flag carried a map of Bangladesh inside the red circle, but the map was later removed to make the flag simple and clear.

The flag was first raised in an earlier form at Dhaka University on 2 March 1971. Today it flies on Independence Day, Victory Day, in schools and on government buildings. We should keep it clean, handle it with respect and never let it touch the ground.

Our National Flag Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A national flag is the sign of a country's identity and independence. It is not only a piece of cloth; it carries the history, hopes and sacrifices of a nation. Our national flag is a green rectangle with a red circle placed slightly towards the hoist, so that the circle looks centred when the flag flies in the wind. The green field stands for the lush green land of Bangladesh, its youth and vitality. The red circle stands for the rising sun over Bengal and for the blood of the martyrs who died for our freedom.

The flag has a short but proud history. An earlier version, with a map of Bangladesh inside the red circle, was first raised at Dhaka University on 2 March 1971, before the Liberation War began. After independence, the artist Quamrul Hassan refined the design and removed the map, because a map was hard to draw correctly on a small flag. The present flag was officially adopted on 17 January 1972. Its length and width follow the ratio of 10 to 6, and the red circle has a radius of one-fifth of the length.

Today our flag flies on Independence Day and Victory Day, in schools, colleges and government offices, and at international events. It reminds us of the sacrifices of 1971 and of our duty to build a better country. Students should learn to handle the flag with care, keep it clean, and never let it touch the ground. Respect for the flag is respect for the people and history it represents.

Our National Flag Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A national flag is the sign of a country's identity and independence. It is not only a piece of cloth; it carries the history, hopes and sacrifices of a nation. Our national flag is a green rectangle with a red circle placed slightly towards the hoist, so that the circle looks centred when the flag flies in the wind. The green field stands for the lush green land of Bangladesh, its youth and vitality. The red circle stands for the rising sun over Bengal and for the blood of the martyrs who died for our freedom in 1971.

The flag has a short but proud history. An earlier version, with a golden map of Bangladesh inside the red circle, was designed by student leaders and first raised at Dhaka University on 2 March 1971, before the Liberation War began. After independence, the artist Quamrul Hassan refined the design and removed the map, because a map was hard to draw correctly on a small flag and looked different from each side. The present flag was officially adopted on 17 January 1972. Its length and width follow the ratio of 10 to 6, and the red circle has a radius of one-fifth of the length.

Today our flag flies on Independence Day and Victory Day, in schools, colleges and government offices, and at international events where Bangladesh is represented. It reminds us of the sacrifices of 1971 and of our duty to build an honest and hardworking country. Students should learn to handle the flag with care, keep it clean, and never let it touch the ground. On national days, we should stand respectfully when the flag is hoisted and the national anthem is sung. Respect for the flag is respect for the people, the land and the history it represents.

Bangla Meaning of Our National Flag Paragraph

আমাদের জাতীয় পতাকার বাংলা অর্থ

জাতীয় পতাকা একটি দেশের পরিচয় ও স্বাধীনতার প্রতীক। আমাদের জাতীয় পতাকা সবুজ আয়তক্ষেত্রের ওপর লাল বৃত্তবিশিষ্ট। সবুজ রং বাংলাদেশের সবুজ-শ্যামল ভূমি ও তারুণ্যের প্রতীক। লাল বৃত্ত উদীয়মান সূর্য ও স্বাধীনতার জন্য শহীদদের রক্তের প্রতীক।

১৯৭১ সালের ২ মার্চ ঢাকা বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ে পতাকার আগের রূপ প্রথম উত্তোলন করা হয়। বর্তমান পতাকাটি ১৯৭২ সালের ১৭ জানুয়ারি গৃহীত হয়। শিল্পী কামরুল হাসান এর বর্তমান রূপ দিয়েছেন। যুদ্ধকালীন পতাকায় লাল বৃত্তের ভেতরে বাংলাদেশের মানচিত্র ছিল, পরে মানচিত্রটি বাদ দেওয়া হয়।

আজ পতাকাটি স্বাধীনতা দিবস, বিজয় দিবস, স্কুল ও সরকারি ভবনে ওড়ে। পতাকাটি পরিষ্কার রাখা, যত্নে ধরা এবং কখনো মাটিতে পড়তে না দেওয়া আমাদের কর্তব্য। পতাকাকে সম্মান করা মানে আমাদের ইতিহাস ও মানুষকে সম্মান করা।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Memorise the key facts together: adopted on 17 January 1972, designed by Quamrul Hassan, ratio 10 to 6, first raised on 2 March 1971.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — design, meaning of the colours, history, and respect — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
