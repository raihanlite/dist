How to Write an Application: Format and Examples

April 28, 2026 by Study Koro · 12 min read

Application writing is an important part of English writing in Bangladesh. Students may need to write an application to the headmaster, principal, department head, or another authority for different reasons.

For example, you may need to write an application for leave, a testimonial, a transfer certificate, permission for a study tour, or another school or college matter. University students and job seekers also write formal applications for academic and professional purposes.

The good news is that most formal applications follow a simple structure. Once you understand the format, you can write an application for almost any situation.

This guide explains the basic application writing format and provides examples for school, college, university, and job applications.

What Is an Application?

An application is a formal written request made to a person or authority.

For example:

  • A student writes to the headmaster for leave.
  • A student writes to the principal for a testimonial.
  • A university student writes to the examination office for a transcript.
  • A student writes to an authority for permission to join a study tour.
  • A job seeker writes to an employer to apply for a position.

The purpose of an application should be clear from the beginning. Avoid unnecessary information and explain exactly what you are requesting.

Basic Application Writing Format

A formal application usually contains these parts:

  1. Date
  2. Name and designation of the recipient
  3. Institution or organisation
  4. Address
  5. Subject
  6. Salutation
  7. Main body
  8. Closing
  9. Name and identifying information of the applicant

A simple structure looks like this:

Date

The [Designation] [Institution Name] [Address]

Subject: Application for [purpose].

Sir/Madam,

First paragraph: Introduce yourself and explain why you are writing.

Second paragraph: Give the necessary details about your situation.

Final paragraph: Clearly state what you are requesting.

Yours faithfully/obediently, [Your Name] [Class/Department] [Roll/ID]

The exact format can vary between institutions, so students should follow any specific instructions given by their school, college, university, or employer.

1. Application for Leave

A leave application is written when you need permission to remain absent from school, college, or another institution.

Example: Leave Application for Illness

15 January 2026

The Headmaster Ideal High School Motijheel, Dhaka

Subject: Application for leave of absence.

Sir,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I am a student of Class IX, Section B, of your school. I have been suffering from a high fever for the last three days, and the doctor has advised me to take rest.

For this reason, I will not be able to attend school from 15 January to 18 January 2026. I therefore request you to kindly grant me leave for these four days.

I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours obediently, Md. Rakib Hasan Class IX, Section B Roll No. 17

What to include in a leave application

Make sure you mention:

  • The reason for your absence
  • The dates of your leave
  • Your class or department
  • Your roll or student ID
  • Any supporting document if the institution requires one

Do not write a long explanation. Give only the information the authority needs.

2. Application for a Testimonial

A testimonial is an official document that may provide information about a student's academic record, conduct, or period of study at an institution.

Students may need a testimonial after completing SSC or HSC or when applying for further education or other purposes.

Example: Testimonial Application

10 February 2026

The Principal Dhaka College New Market, Dhaka

Subject: Application for a testimonial.

Sir,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I passed the HSC examination in 2025 from your college under the Dhaka Education Board. I am now preparing to apply for higher studies and require a testimonial from the college.

I therefore request you to kindly issue me a testimonial at your earliest convenience. I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours obediently, Sumaiya Akter HSC 2025, Science Group Roll No. 124356

Tip for students

If the institution has a specific testimonial form, use that form instead of writing a separate application. Some Bangladeshi institutions provide their own testimonial forms with fields for student information and academic details.

3. Application for an Academic Transcript

University students may need an academic transcript when applying for higher studies, scholarships, employment, or other official purposes.

The procedure is different from one university to another. Some universities require a specific form, fee payment, clearance, or supporting documents.

Example

22 March 2026

The Controller of Examinations University of Dhaka Dhaka-1000

Subject: Application for issuance of academic transcript.

Sir,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I completed my Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science and Engineering from your university in 2025. I now require an official academic transcript for my application for higher studies abroad.

I have completed the required payment and attached the necessary documents with this application.

I therefore request you to kindly issue two copies of my official academic transcript.

Yours faithfully, Mohammad Tanvir Ahmed Registration No. 2021-715-001 Session: 2020–2021 Department of CSE

Important

Before submitting a university application, check your university's current instructions. Do not assume that every university uses the same process.

4. Application for a Transfer Certificate

A transfer certificate application is generally written when a student needs official permission or documentation to leave one institution and continue studies elsewhere.

Example

20 April 2026

The Principal ABC College Dhaka

Subject: Application for a transfer certificate.

Sir,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I am a student of Class XII of your college. My family is moving to another city, and I need to continue my studies there.

I therefore request you to kindly issue me a transfer certificate so that I can complete the necessary admission formalities at another institution.

I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours obediently, Rahim Ahmed Class XII Roll No. 25 Science Group

5. Application for Permission to Join a Study Tour

Students may need permission from their school or college authority before joining an educational tour or study tour.

Example

5 May 2026

The Headmaster ABC High School Dhaka

Subject: Application for permission to join a study tour.

Sir,

With due respect, we, the students of Class X, would like to arrange an educational study tour to the National Museum in Dhaka. Such a visit would give us an opportunity to learn more about the history and culture of Bangladesh outside the classroom.

We therefore request you to kindly grant us permission to arrange the study tour under the guidance of our teachers.

We shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours obediently, The students of Class X ABC High School Dhaka

Tip

For a group application, write on behalf of the students and clearly mention the proposed destination, purpose, and supervision arrangements when relevant.

6. Job Application Letter

A job application is different from a school application. Instead of asking a school authority for permission or a document, you are introducing yourself to an employer and explaining why you are suitable for a particular position.

A modern job application should be concise and specific.

Example

5 April 2026

The Head of Human Resources ABC Bank Limited Gulshan, Dhaka

Subject: Application for the position of Management Trainee Officer.

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to apply for the position of Management Trainee Officer advertised on your company's website.

I recently completed my BBA in Finance from North South University with a CGPA of 3.78. During my undergraduate studies, I completed a three-month internship with a commercial bank, where I gained practical experience in credit analysis and customer service.

I believe my academic background, internship experience, and communication skills would allow me to contribute effectively to your organisation. My CV and relevant academic documents are attached for your consideration.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my qualifications in an interview.

Yours faithfully, Nusrat Jahan nusrat.jahan@example.com +880 1XXX-XXXXXX

Job application tip

Do not copy the same application for every job.

Mention the actual position, organisation, and skills that are relevant to the job. Your application should support your CV rather than repeat everything in it.

7. Application for a Character Certificate

A character certificate or similar official document may be requested for admission, employment, or other purposes.

The exact name and procedure can vary by institution.

Example

12 June 2026

The Headmaster ABC High School Dhaka

Subject: Application for a character certificate.

Sir,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I was a student of your school and passed the SSC examination in 2026. I now require a character certificate for my further studies.

I therefore request you to kindly issue me a character certificate mentioning my conduct and period of study at the institution.

I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours obediently, Sadia Islam SSC 2026 Roll No. 123456

How to Write an Application Step by Step

If you have never written a formal application before, follow these steps.

Step 1: Identify the person you are writing to

Find out who should receive the application.

For example:

  • The Headmaster
  • The Principal
  • The Head of the Department
  • The Controller of Examinations
  • The Human Resources Manager

Use the correct designation.

Step 2: Write a clear subject

The subject should tell the reader what you want.

Good examples:

  • Application for leave of absence
  • Application for a testimonial
  • Application for issuance of an academic transcript
  • Application for a transfer certificate
  • Application for permission to join a study tour

Do not write a long subject.

Step 3: Introduce yourself

Tell the reader who you are.

For example:

I am a student of Class X, Section A, of your school.

A university student could write:

I am a student of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

Step 4: Explain the reason

Give the important facts related to your request.

For a leave application, mention the reason and dates.

For a testimonial, explain why you need it.

For a transcript, explain what you need and, if relevant, what the transcript is for.

Step 5: Make your request clearly

Do not make the reader guess what you want.

For example:

I therefore request you to kindly grant me leave for three days.

Step 6: End politely

Use an appropriate formal closing.

For example:

I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Then add your name and relevant identification details.

Common Mistakes in Application Writing

Students often make simple mistakes that can make an application look careless.

1. Forgetting the subject

The subject helps the reader understand the purpose of the application quickly.

2. Writing too much

A formal application should contain the information necessary to understand and process the request. Avoid unrelated stories.

3. Using informal language

Avoid expressions such as:

  • Hi Sir
  • What's up
  • I wanna request
  • Please help me out
  • Thanks a lot

Use polite and formal language instead.

4. Using unnecessary difficult words

You do not need complicated English to write a good application.

For example:

I would like to request you to kindly grant me leave.

is clear and sufficient.

A simple sentence is usually better than a long sentence filled with difficult words.

5. Forgetting personal details

For school and college applications, include the details needed to identify you, such as:

  • Name
  • Class
  • Section
  • Roll number
  • Registration number
  • Department
  • Student ID

Only include information that is relevant to the application.

6. Using the wrong closing

Choose the closing according to the situation and the style required by your institution.

For traditional school applications, “Yours obediently” is commonly used. For professional applications, “Yours faithfully” or “Yours sincerely” may be more appropriate depending on how you address the recipient.

Yours Obediently, Yours Faithfully, or Yours Sincerely?

These expressions are often confusing for students.

Yours obediently

This is commonly found in traditional school and college applications addressed to teachers or institutional authorities.

Yours faithfully

This is commonly used in formal correspondence when you do not address the recipient by name, especially in British-style business writing.

Example:

Dear Sir/Madam, Yours faithfully,

Yours sincerely

This is commonly used when you address the recipient by name.

Example:

Dear Mr Karim, Yours sincerely,

However, institutions may have their own preferred format. If your school or university gives you a prescribed format, follow it.

Application Writing Format for SSC and HSC Students

For SSC and HSC students, application writing is also an important part of English writing practice.

Common topics include:

  • Application for leave
  • Application for a testimonial
  • Application for a transfer certificate
  • Application for a seat in the school hostel
  • Application for permission to arrange a study tour
  • Application for setting up a canteen
  • Application for improving library facilities
  • Application for arranging a cultural programme

For examination practice, first understand the format. Then practise writing different applications instead of memorising one answer word for word.

A memorised application may not fit a new question exactly.

Application Writing vs. Formal Letter Writing

These two types of writing are related, but they have different purposes.

An application is usually a formal request made to an authority.

A formal letter can have a wider purpose, such as making a complaint, requesting information, giving notice, or communicating with an organisation.

For example:

Application: Application to the principal for leave.

Formal letter: Letter to the editor about traffic congestion in Dhaka.

Knowing the purpose helps you choose the right format.

Quick Application Writing Template

You can use this template when you need to write a new application.

[Date]

The [Designation] [Institution Name] [Address]

Subject: Application for [purpose].

Sir/Madam,

With due respect, I would like to inform you that I am [your position/class/department].

[Explain the reason for your application and provide the important details.]

I therefore request you to kindly [clearly state what you want].

I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours faithfully/obediently, [Your Name] [Class/Department] [Roll/Student ID]

Use this as a framework, not as a paragraph to memorise. Change the details and sentences according to the situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the format of an application in Bangladesh?

A formal application generally includes the date, recipient's designation and institution, subject, salutation, body, closing, and the applicant's identifying details. The exact format may vary depending on the institution.

How do I start an application?

Start by identifying the recipient and writing a clear subject. In the first paragraph, introduce yourself and explain the reason for writing.

How long should a formal application be?

For most school and college applications, one or two short paragraphs are enough. Include the necessary information without adding unrelated details.

Should I write an application by hand?

Follow the instructions of the institution or organisation. Some schools may prefer handwritten applications, while universities and employers may require typed or online submissions.

What paper size should I use?

If the institution has not specified a paper size, A4 is a practical choice for a typed formal document. However, always follow the instructions provided by the receiving institution.

Can I use the same application format for every topic?

You can use the same basic structure, but the content should change according to your purpose. A leave application, testimonial application, university transcript request, and job application should not contain the same body text.

Should I memorise application examples for exams?

It is better to understand the format and practise different topics. Memorising complete applications can make it difficult to answer questions that use a different situation.

Final Tips for Writing a Good Application

Before submitting your application, check these points:

  • Is the recipient correct?
  • Is the subject clear?
  • Did you explain the reason?
  • Did you make your request clear?
  • Did you include your class, roll, ID, or department where necessary?
  • Did you use polite and formal language?
  • Did you check spelling and grammar?
  • Did you follow any instructions given by the institution?
  • Did you remove unnecessary information?

A good application does not need complicated English. It needs a clear purpose, correct information, polite language, and a suitable format.

Once you understand the basic structure, you can write applications for school, college, university, and professional situations with much more confidence.
