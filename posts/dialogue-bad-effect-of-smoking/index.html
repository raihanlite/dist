Dialogue Bad Effect of Smoking

August 10, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 5 min read

This dialogue is about the bad effects of smoking. It is written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The exam question is usually: "Write a dialogue between two friends about the bad effects of smoking." Read the dialogue below, then learn the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end. The dialogue uses simple English, so you can learn it easily and rewrite it in your own words in the examination.

Dialogue: Bad Effect of Smoking

Rafi: My older cousin has started smoking because he thinks it makes him look mature.

Joya: Tobacco does not prove maturity. It contains addictive substances and exposes the body to harmful chemicals.

Rafi: What damage can smoking cause?

Joya: It raises the risk of lung disease, heart problems, stroke, and several cancers. Smoke also harms people nearby, especially children and pregnant women.

Rafi: My cousin says he smokes only a few cigarettes a day, so it cannot be dangerous.

Joya: Even a small amount is harmful. There is no safe level of smoking, and the damage grows with every year of use.

Rafi: Smoking also wastes money, does it not?

Joya: Yes. Money spent on cigarettes could support food, books, or savings. A smoker also pays more later for treatment.

Rafi: Some people say they can stop whenever they wish.

Joya: Dependence makes quitting difficult, but support from a doctor, family, or cessation programme can help. Avoiding triggers and replacing the habit with exercise or another activity is useful.

Rafi: What is a trigger?

Joya: A trigger is something that makes a person want to smoke — for example, stress, tea with friends, or a break after a meal. If a person knows his triggers, he can prepare for them.

Rafi: We should not tease someone who is trying to quit.

Joya: Right. Encouragement is more helpful than shame. Young people should learn the facts before advertising or peer pressure influences them.

Rafi: Why do young people start smoking at all?

Joya: Often because of friends, films, or advertisements that make smoking look attractive. Some start from curiosity and then cannot stop.

Rafi: Can schools help to stop this?

Joya: Yes. Teachers can explain the dangers, and schools can keep their surroundings free from tobacco. Parents should also talk openly with their children.

Rafi: My cousin coughs every morning now. Is that a sign of damage?

Joya: It can be. A smoker's cough is a warning. He should see a doctor and stop smoking before the damage becomes serious.

Rafi: I will share reliable health information with my cousin and ask whether he wants help.

Joya: Good. A calm and honest conversation can do more than a lecture. If he decides to quit, we can support him every step of the way.

Bangla Meaning of the Dialogue

সংলাপটির বাংলা অর্থ

রাফির বড় চাচাতো ভাই ধূমপান শুরু করেছে, কারণ সে মনে করে এতে তাকে পরিণত দেখায়। জয়া বলে, তামাক পরিণত হওয়ার প্রমাণ নয়। এতে আসক্তি সৃষ্টিকারী পদার্থ থাকে এবং শরীর ক্ষতিকর রাসায়নিকের সংস্পর্শে আসে।

ধূমপান ফুসফুসের রোগ, হৃদরোগ, স্ট্রোক ও নানা ধরনের ক্যান্সারের ঝুঁকি বাড়ায়। ধোঁয়া আশপাশের মানুষের, বিশেষ করে শিশুদের ক্ষতি করে। অল্প ধূমপানও নিরাপদ নয়। সিগারেটের টাকা খাবার, বই বা সঞ্চয়ে খরচ করা যেত। আসক্তির কারণে ছাড়া কঠিন হলেও ডাক্তার, পরিবার বা ধূমপান ছাড়ার কর্মসূচির সাহায্যে তা সম্ভব। চাপ বা আড্ডার মতো কারণগুলো চিনে রাখলে সেগুলোর জন্য প্রস্তুতি নেওয়া যায়। শেষে রাফি তার ভাইয়ের সঙ্গে শান্তভাবে কথা বলে সাহায্য করার সিদ্ধান্ত নেয়।

জয়া আরও বলে, অনেক তরুণ বন্ধুদের দেখাদেখি, সিনেমা বা বিজ্ঞাপনের প্রভাবে ধূমপান শুরু করে। কেউ কেউ কৌতূহল থেকে শুরু করে, তারপর আর ছাড়তে পারে না। শিক্ষকরা বিপদগুলো বুঝিয়ে বলতে পারেন, আর স্কুলের আশপাশ তামাকমুক্ত রাখা যায়। বাবা-মায়েরও সন্তানদের সঙ্গে খোলামেলা কথা বলা উচিত। সকালে কাশি ধূমপায়ীর জন্য একটি সতর্ক সংকেত; ক্ষতি গুরুতর হওয়ার আগেই ডাক্তার দেখানো ও ধূমপান ছাড়া উচিত।

Exam Tips

  • Write the dialogue as a conversation, not as a paragraph. Put the speaker's name and a colon before each line.
  • Keep the language simple and natural, the way two friends really talk about a family problem.
  • Include clear bad effects of smoking — health damage, harm to others, and wasted money — and also mention how a smoker can quit.
  • End with a kind and positive decision, such as sharing health information and offering help, instead of only blaming the smoker.
  • Learn the important words from the table so that you can write the dialogue in your own sentences in the exam.
  • Use the facts from the dialogue in your own sentences. Do not write medical claims you are not sure about — the health effects mentioned here are enough for the exam.
