Discipline Composition

August 27, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 5 min read

On this page you will find a discipline composition with an outline, the full composition, a Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips. It is written in simple English for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students, so you can learn the main points easily and write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.

Outline

  • What discipline is: following rules and doing duties on time
  • Discipline in the daily life of a student
  • Discipline at home, at school, at work and in public life
  • Life without discipline: talent wasted
  • How to build discipline step by step: small habits and a realistic daily routine
  • Conclusion: discipline is the key to success

Discipline Composition

Discipline means following good rules and doing our duties at the proper time. It does not mean fear or punishment; it means controlling ourselves so that a small comfort today does not destroy a big goal tomorrow. A disciplined person wakes up on time, keeps his things in order, finishes his work on time, and respects the rules of his family, school and society. Discipline is not a burden. It is a habit that makes life orderly, peaceful and successful. Just as a train cannot run without rails, a person cannot reach his goal without discipline.

Discipline is most important in the life of a student. A disciplined student attends classes regularly, listens to his teachers, studies every day, and submits his homework on time. He keeps his books and notes tidy, prepares his lessons before class, and never copies in the examination. He respects his teachers and classmates, speaks politely, and follows the rules of his school. Such a student earns the love of his teachers and the trust of his friends, and he moves steadily towards success.

Discipline is also needed outside school. At home, it means helping parents, keeping things in their place, and eating and sleeping on time. At work, it means arriving on time and finishing duties with care. In public life, it means obeying traffic rules, standing in a queue, keeping the roads and parks clean, and keeping promises. Discipline also teaches us to respect the rights of others. When people follow such rules, society becomes peaceful and safe. A country cannot progress if its people do not follow discipline.

Without discipline, even talent is wasted. A clever student may fail because he studies only at the last moment. A strong player may lose because he does not practise regularly. Discipline turns small daily efforts into great skill, and it builds self-respect, because a person learns that he can depend on his own decisions. But discipline should be realistic: we must keep time for sleep, play, family and rest, or it will become a burden. With patience and daily practice, discipline becomes a habit, and that habit is the key to success in life. Let us practise discipline every day, from small things, and build a better future.

Bangla Meaning

শৃঙ্খলার বাংলা অর্থ

শৃঙ্খলা মানে ভালো নিয়ম মেনে চলা এবং সময়মতো নিজের কর্তব্য পালন করা। শৃঙ্খলা মানে ভয় বা শাস্তি নয়; এর মানে নিজেকে নিয়ন্ত্রণ করা, যাতে আজকের ছোট আরাম আগামী দিনের বড় লক্ষ্য নষ্ট না করে। শৃঙ্খলাপরায়ণ মানুষ সময়মতো ঘুম থেকে ওঠে, জিনিসপত্র গুছিয়ে রাখে, সময়মতো কাজ শেষ করে এবং পরিবার, স্কুল ও সমাজের নিয়ম মেনে চলে।

ছাত্রজীবনে শৃঙ্খলা সবচেয়ে বেশি দরকার। শৃঙ্খলাপরায়ণ ছাত্র নিয়মিত ক্লাসে যায়, প্রতিদিন পড়ে, সময়মতো বাড়ির কাজ জমা দেয় এবং পরীক্ষায় কখনো নকল করে না। বাড়িতে, কর্মক্ষেত্রে ও জনজীবনেও শৃঙ্খলা দরকার—যেমন ট্রাফিক নিয়ম মানা, লাইনে দাঁড়ানো, রাস্তা পরিষ্কার রাখা ও কথা রাখা।

শৃঙ্খলা ছাড়া প্রতিভাও নষ্ট হয়ে যায়। ছোট ছোট প্রতিদিনের চেষ্টাই শৃঙ্খলার মাধ্যমে বড় দক্ষতায় পরিণত হয়। তবে শৃঙ্খলা হতে হবে বাস্তবসম্মত—ঘুম, খেলা, পরিবার ও বিশ্রামের জন্যও সময় রাখতে হবে। ধৈর্য ও নিয়মিত অভ্যাসে শৃঙ্খলা জীবনের সাফল্যের চাবিকাঠি হয়ে ওঠে। প্রতিদিন ছোট ছোট নিয়ম মেনে চললেই শৃঙ্খলা অভ্যাসে পরিণত হয়।

Exam Tips

  • Learn the outline first — what discipline is, student life, home and public life, life without it — then write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.
  • Do not memorise the whole composition. Remember the main points and write them in your own simple, correct sentences.
  • Use short paragraphs: one for the meaning of discipline, one for student life, one for public life, and one short paragraph for the conclusion.
  • Keep your spelling, grammar and handwriting neat and correct. A few correct sentences are better than many wrong ones.
  • Revise the composition once again the night before the exam and read the Bangla meaning to remember the ideas clearly.
