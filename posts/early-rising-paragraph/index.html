Early Rising paragraph

March 19, 2026 by Study Koro · 6 min read

On this page you will find an early rising paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Early Rising Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Early rising means getting up from bed early in the morning. It is a very good habit. There is a famous saying, "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise." An early riser can enjoy the fresh air of the morning and the beauty of nature. The morning is calm and quiet, so a student can study with full attention.

A person who rises early gets more time for his work. He can take exercise, say his prayers, and prepare his lessons before school. He can finish his work in time and never feels hurried. On the other hand, a late riser wakes up late, misses the fresh morning, and cannot finish his work properly. He becomes lazy and falls behind in life.

Early rising is good for health, mind, and study. So every student should try to form this good habit from an early age.

Early Rising Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Early rising means getting up from bed early in the morning. It is one of the best habits a person can form. There is a famous saying, "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise." This saying is true. The morning is the best part of the day. The air is fresh, nature is calm and beautiful.

An early riser gets many benefits. First, he can enjoy the fresh morning air and take exercise or go for a walk. This keeps his body healthy and his mind fresh. Second, the morning is quiet, so a student can study with full concentration. Lessons learned in the morning are remembered easily. Third, an early riser gets more time for his work. He can say his prayers, prepare his lessons, and plan his day without hurry. He can finish his work in time and reach school punctually.

On the other hand, a late riser loses all these benefits. He wakes up late, feels lazy, and cannot finish his work in time. He often misses his lessons and falls behind in his studies. He becomes dull and inactive.

However, early rising is useful only when a person also goes to bed early and gets enough sleep. A student who stays awake late at night and wakes up early will feel tired, not fresh. So we should form the habit of sleeping early and rising early. It is a simple habit, but it can make our life healthy, disciplined, and successful.

Early Rising Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Early rising means the habit of getting up from bed early in the morning. It is one of the best habits in human life. There is a well-known saying, "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise." This old saying is still true today, because the habit of rising early brings many benefits to our body, mind, and work.

The morning is the most beautiful and peaceful part of the day. At that time, nature looks fresh, the air is pure, and everything is calm and quiet. He can go for a walk or take exercise, which keeps his body strong and his mind fresh for the whole day.

Early rising is especially useful for students. The morning hours are quiet, and there is no noise to disturb the mind. So a student can study with full concentration and remember his lessons easily. He can revise his lessons, prepare his homework, and plan his day before going to school. Punctuality and discipline grow from this habit.

A late riser, on the other hand, loses all these advantages. He wakes up late, feels lazy, and rushes through his work. He often misses his lessons, reaches school late, and falls behind in his studies. His health also suffers because he misses the fresh air and exercise of the morning.

But early rising is helpful only when it is joined with early sleeping. A person who stays awake till late at night and then wakes up early will be tired and dull. So we should go to bed early, sleep well, and rise early. If we form this habit from our student life, we can enjoy a healthy, disciplined, and successful life. This habit also builds confidence and a strong character.

Bangla Meaning of Early Rising Paragraph

ভোরে ওঠা অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

ভোরে ওঠা মানে সকালবেলা তাড়াতাড়ি ঘুম থেকে ওঠা। এটি একটি খুব ভালো অভ্যাস। একটি বিখ্যাত প্রবাদ আছে, "তাড়াতাড়ি ঘুমাতে যাওয়া এবং তাড়াতাড়ি ওঠা মানুষকে সুস্থ, ধনী ও জ্ঞানী করে।" ভোরে ওঠা মানুষ সকালের নির্মল বাতাস ও প্রকৃতির সৌন্দর্য উপভোগ করতে পারে। সকাল শান্ত ও নীরব থাকে, তাই শিক্ষার্থী পূর্ণ মনোযোগে পড়াশোনা করতে পারে।

যে ব্যক্তি ভোরে ওঠে, সে কাজের জন্য বেশি সময় পায়। সে ব্যায়াম করতে পারে, প্রার্থনা করতে পারে এবং স্কুলের আগে পাঠ তৈরি করতে পারে। সে সময়মতো কাজ শেষ করতে পারে এবং কখনো তাড়াহুড়ো অনুভব করে না। অন্যদিকে, দেরিতে ওঠা ব্যক্তি দেরিতে ঘুম থেকে ওঠে, সকালের নির্মল পরিবেশ হারায় এবং কাজ ঠিকমতো শেষ করতে পারে না। সে অলস হয়ে যায় এবং জীবনে পিছিয়ে পড়ে।

ভোরে ওঠা স্বাস্থ্য, মন ও পড়াশোনার জন্য ভালো। তাই প্রত্যেক শিক্ষার্থীর ছোটবেলা থেকেই এই ভালো অভ্যাস গড়ে তোলা উচিত।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the structure first — meaning, the famous saying, benefits for health and study, the harm of late rising, and the need for enough sleep — then write it in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Remember the main points and connect them with simple linking words like first, second, and on the other hand.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
