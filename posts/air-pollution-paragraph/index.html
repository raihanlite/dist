Air Pollution Paragraph

April 22, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find an air pollution paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Air Pollution Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Air pollution means the air becomes dirty and unsafe to breathe. It happens when smoke, dust, poisonous gases and tiny harmful particles mix with the air. Smoke from vehicles, factories and brick kilns, dust from construction work, and the burning of rubbish are the main causes of air pollution in our country.

Polluted air is very harmful to our health. It irritates our eyes and throat, and it can cause asthma, cough and heart disease. Children, old people and those who work outdoors suffer the most. Air pollution also damages crops, buildings and animals, and it makes the climate warmer.

We must work together to keep the air clean. The government should enforce laws, control smoke from factories and vehicles, and improve public transport. We should not burn rubbish, and we should plant more trees and use clean energy. Clean air is necessary for a healthy life.

Air Pollution Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Air pollution means the air around us becomes dirty and unsafe to breathe. It happens when smoke, dust, poisonous gases and tiny harmful particles mix with the air in large amounts. In Bangladesh, the main causes are smoke from motor vehicles, factories and brick kilns, dust from construction work and broken roads, and the burning of rubbish and dry leaves. In crowded cities, the problem becomes worse when the wind is weak and the smoke cannot blow away.

Polluted air is very harmful to our health. It irritates our eyes, nose and throat, and it can cause cough, asthma, lung disease and heart disease. Children, old people and people who work outdoors, such as rickshaw-pullers and construction workers, suffer the most. Air pollution also harms crops, animals and buildings. It makes the climate warmer by increasing harmful gases in the atmosphere. When the air is very polluted, even healthy people feel tired and uncomfortable.

We must all work together to keep the air clean. The government should enforce laws against smoke, improve public transport so that fewer private cars are needed, control dust at construction sites and encourage clean energy like solar power. We as citizens should not burn rubbish, should keep our vehicles in good condition, and should plant trees, which clean the air naturally. Schools can teach children about the dangers of polluted air. Clean air is not a luxury; it is necessary for the health and life of everyone.

Air Pollution Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Air pollution means the air around us becomes dirty and unsafe to breathe. It occurs when smoke, dust, poisonous gases and tiny harmful particles enter the atmosphere in harmful amounts. In Bangladesh, the main sources are exhaust from motor vehicles, smoke from factories and brick kilns, dust from construction work and broken roads, and the burning of rubbish, dry leaves and crop waste. In crowded cities, the problem becomes serious in the dry winter months, when little rain washes the dust away. Air pollution is now a great threat to public health in our country.

Breathing polluted air is very harmful to the human body. It irritates the eyes, nose and throat, and it can cause cough, asthma, bronchitis, lung disease and heart disease. Children, old people and outdoor workers suffer the most, because they breathe polluted air for many hours every day. Air pollution also damages crops, weakens animals, blackens buildings and harms the climate by adding greenhouse gases to the atmosphere. On very polluted days, even healthy people feel tired. Treating pollution-related diseases is also a heavy burden on poor families.

Preventing air pollution needs both government action and personal responsibility. The authorities should enforce emission rules strictly, improve bus and train services so fewer private cars are needed, control dust at construction sites, and promote clean energy such as solar power. Citizens should also play their part: we should not burn rubbish or leaves, we should keep vehicles in good condition, we should walk or use public transport for short journeys, and we should plant trees, which clean the air naturally. Schools and the media can teach people about the dangers of polluted air. Clean air is the right of every citizen, and protecting it is a shared duty, because everyone depends on the air we breathe.

Bangla Meaning of Air Pollution Paragraph

বায়ু দূষণের বাংলা অর্থ

বায়ু দূষণ মানে আমাদের চারপাশের বাতাস নোংরা ও শ্বাস নেওয়ার অনুপযুক্ত হয়ে যাওয়া। যানবাহন, কারখানা ও ইটভাটার ধোঁয়া, নির্মাণকাজের ধুলো এবং আবর্জনা পোড়ানোর ধোঁয়া বাতাসে মিশে বায়ু দূষণ ঘটায়। দূষিত বাতাস চোখ ও গলা জ্বালা করে এবং হাঁপানি, কাশি, ফুসফুস ও হৃদরোগের কারণ হতে পারে। শিশু, বৃদ্ধ ও বাইরে কাজ করা মানুষ সবচেয়ে বেশি ক্ষতিগ্রস্ত হয়। বায়ু দূষণ ফসল, পশুপাখি ও ভবনেরও ক্ষতি করে এবং জলবায়ু উষ্ণ করে। বাতাস পরিষ্কার রাখতে সরকারকে আইন প্রয়োগ করতে হবে, গণপরিবহন উন্নত করতে হবে এবং পরিচ্ছন্ন জ্বালানি ব্যবহারে উৎসাহ দিতে হবে। আমাদের আবর্জনা পোড়ানো বন্ধ করে বেশি করে গাছ লাগাতে হবে। পরিচ্ছন্ন বাতাস সুস্থ জীবনের জন্য অপরিহার্য।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the main points — causes, health effects, and solutions — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word; practise writing it once from memory.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block and revise it once the night before the exam.
