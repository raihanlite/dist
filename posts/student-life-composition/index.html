Student life Composition for Class 5, 6, 7, 8, SSC – Easy

August 27, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find a student life composition with an outline, the full composition, a Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips. It is written in simple English for Class 5 to 10 and SSC students, so you can learn the main points easily and write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.

Outline

  • What student life is and why it is the best time of life
  • The first duty of a student: sincere study and good habits
  • Character, discipline and respect for teachers and parents
  • Games, sports, health and co-curricular activities
  • Problems of student life and how to face them
  • Conclusion: good habits in student life build a bright and happy future

Student Life Composition

Student life is the best period of a person's life. It is the time to learn, to build character, and to prepare for the future. A student does not study only to pass examinations; he studies to gain knowledge, to form good habits, and to become a useful citizen. The lessons and habits we learn in student life stay with us for our whole life. That is why student life is often called the seed time of life, because the seeds we sow now will grow into the fruits of our future.

The first duty of a student is sincere study. He should attend classes regularly, listen to his teachers carefully, and ask questions when he does not understand a lesson. He should study every day, finish his homework on time, and revise his lessons before they are forgotten. A good student keeps his books and notes tidy, reads books outside his textbooks, and tries to understand his lessons instead of memorising them without meaning. He should also learn from his mistakes and never copy in an examination. Regular study, not study at the last moment, brings real success.

Student life is not only about books. A student should also build a good character. He should respect his parents and teachers, love his classmates, speak the truth, and keep his promises. He should be disciplined and punctual, and he should take part in games, sports and cultural activities. Games keep the body healthy and the mind fresh, and they teach teamwork and fair play. A student may also join social work, which teaches kindness and responsibility towards others.

Student life also has problems. Many students feel pressure about examinations, and some waste their time on the phone or in bad company. A student should face these problems with courage. He should keep a daily routine, sleep well, eat healthy food, and ask his teachers or parents for help when he faces a difficulty. If a student uses his time well, studies with sincerity, and keeps a good character, he can build a bright future for himself and for his country. In short, student life is the golden time of life, and we should use it wisely.

Bangla Meaning

ছাত্রজীবনের বাংলা অর্থ

ছাত্রজীবন মানুষের জীবনের শ্রেষ্ঠ সময়। এই সময়ে আমরা জ্ঞান অর্জন করি, চরিত্র গঠন করি এবং ভবিষ্যতের জন্য নিজেকে তৈরি করি। ছাত্র শুধু পরীক্ষায় পাস করার জন্য পড়ে না; সে জ্ঞান লাভের জন্য, ভালো অভ্যাস গড়ার জন্য এবং একজন ভালো নাগরিক হওয়ার জন্য পড়ে। ছাত্রজীবনে শেখা পাঠ ও অভ্যাস সারা জীবন আমাদের সঙ্গে থাকে।

একজন ভালো ছাত্রের প্রথম কর্তব্য আন্তরিকভাবে পড়াশোনা করা। সে নিয়মিত ক্লাসে যায়, শিক্ষকের কথা মনোযোগ দিয়ে শোনে, প্রতিদিন পড়ে এবং সময়মতো বাড়ির কাজ শেষ করে। সে বাবা-মা ও শিক্ষককে সম্মান করে, সত্য কথা বলে এবং নিয়ম মেনে চলে। খেলাধুলা শরীর সুস্থ রাখে এবং মন সতেজ করে।

ছাত্রজীবনে নানা সমস্যাও আসে। পরীক্ষার চাপ, মোবাইলে সময় নষ্ট করা ও খারাপ সঙ্গ ছাত্রদের ক্ষতি করে। তাই ছাত্রকে দৈনন্দিন রুটিন মেনে চলতে হবে, ভালোভাবে ঘুমাতে হবে এবং প্রয়োজনে শিক্ষক বা বাবা-মায়ের সাহায্য নিতে হবে। সময়ের সঠিক ব্যবহার ও ভালো চরিত্রই ছাত্রজীবনকে সোনালি করে তোলে। ভালো ছাত্র হওয়ার জন্য পড়াশোনার পাশাপাশি ভালো চরিত্রও দরকার।

Exam Tips

  • Learn the outline first — study, character, games and health, problems — then write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.
  • Do not memorise the whole composition. Remember the main points and write them in your own simple, correct sentences.
  • Use clear short paragraphs: one for study, one for character and games, one for problems, and one for the conclusion.
  • Keep your spelling, grammar and handwriting neat and correct. A few correct sentences are better than many wrong ones.
  • Revise the composition once again the night before the exam and read the Bangla meaning to remember the ideas clearly.
