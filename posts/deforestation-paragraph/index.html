Deforestation Paragraph

February 15, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find a deforestation paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Deforestation Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Deforestation means cutting down trees in large numbers and clearing forests. Trees are very useful to us. They give us oxygen, wood, fruits, and shade. They also give shelter to birds and animals. But people are cutting down trees for wood, fuel, furniture, and land for houses, roads, and farms.

Deforestation causes great harm to our environment. Trees also bring rain and keep the soil rich and fertile for farming. When trees are cut down, the amount of carbon dioxide in the air increases, and the earth becomes warmer. Rainfall becomes irregular, and the soil loses its fertility. Floods and droughts become more common. Many birds and animals lose their homes and die.

We must stop deforestation to save our earth. We should plant more trees and take care of them. If we cut down one tree, we should plant two trees. Let us all plant trees and protect our forests.

Deforestation Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Deforestation means the cutting down of trees in large numbers and the clearing of forests. Forests are one of the greatest gifts of nature. Trees give us oxygen to breathe, absorb carbon dioxide from the air, and keep the earth cool. They give us wood, fruits, flowers, and medicine. They also give shelter to birds and animals and protect the soil from erosion. But people are destroying forests for their own needs.

There are many causes of deforestation. Our population is growing fast, so more land is needed for houses, roads, schools, and farms. Trees are cut down for timber, firewood, furniture, and paper. Some dishonest people cut down valuable trees illegally and sell them for money. In the hilly areas, forests are also cleared for shifting cultivation.

The effects of deforestation are very harmful. When trees are cut down, carbon dioxide increases in the air, and the world becomes warmer. Rainfall becomes irregular, and the fertile soil is washed away by rain. As a result, floods, droughts, and cyclones become more frequent. Many birds and animals lose their homes, and some species are in danger of extinction. In Bangladesh, the Sundarbans, our great mangrove forest, is also under threat.

To prevent deforestation, we must plant more trees and protect our forests. The government should enforce strict laws against illegal logging and start tree plantation programmes. Every student should plant at least one tree every year and take care of it.

Deforestation Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Deforestation means the large-scale cutting down of trees and the clearing of forests. Forests are one of the most precious gifts of nature to mankind. Trees give us oxygen to breathe and absorb the harmful carbon dioxide from the air. They give us timber, fuel, fruits, flowers, and medicine. They bring rainfall, keep the soil fertile, and give shelter to countless birds, animals, and insects.

But people are destroying forests at an alarming rate. The main cause is the rapid growth of population. More people need more houses, roads, schools, and farmland, so forests are cleared to make room for them. Trees are cut down for timber, firewood, furniture, and paper. Some dishonest people cut down valuable trees illegally and sell the timber for quick money. In the hilly districts, forests are also cleared for shifting cultivation.

The effects of deforestation are very serious. When trees are cut down, carbon dioxide increases in the atmosphere, and the earth becomes warmer. Without the roots of trees to hold it, the fertile top soil is washed away by rain, and the land becomes barren. Floods, droughts, and cyclones become more frequent. Many species of birds and animals lose their homes, and some are in danger of extinction. In Bangladesh, the Sundarbans, the largest mangrove forest in the world, protects our coast from cyclones, but it is also under threat from deforestation.

To save our environment, we must stop deforestation and plant more trees. The government should enforce strict laws against illegal logging, expand social forestry, and start a tree plantation movement in which every student plants at least one tree every year. We should also use wood carefully and raise public awareness about the value of forests. If we protect our forests today, we can leave a green and safe Bangladesh for the coming generations.

Bangla Meaning of Deforestation Paragraph

বন উজাড় অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

বন উজাড় মানে প্রচুর সংখ্যায় গাছ কেটে ফেলা এবং বন ধ্বংস করা। গাছ আমাদের জন্য খুবই উপকারী। এগুলো আমাদের অক্সিজেন, কাঠ, ফল ও ছায়া দেয়। এগুলো পাখি ও পশুদের আশ্রয়ও দেয়। কিন্তু মানুষ কাঠ, জ্বালানি, আসবাবপত্র এবং বাড়ি, রাস্তা ও খামারের জমির জন্য গাছ কাটছে।

বন উজাড় আমাদের পরিবেশের বড় ক্ষতি করে। গাছ কাটা হলে বাতাসে কার্বন ডাই-অক্সাইডের পরিমাণ বেড়ে যায় এবং পৃথিবী আরও গরম হয়ে ওঠে। বৃষ্টিপাত অনিয়মিত হয় এবং মাটি তার উর্বরতা হারায়। বন্যা ও খরা আরও বেশি ঘটে। অনেক পাখি ও পশু তাদের বাসস্থান হারিয়ে মারা যায়।

পৃথিবীকে বাঁচাতে আমাদের বন উজাড় বন্ধ করতে হবে। আমাদের আরও বেশি গাছ লাগাতে হবে এবং সেগুলোর যত্ন নিতে হবে। একটি গাছ কাটলে আমাদের দুটি গাছ লাগানো উচিত। আসুন আমরা সবাই গাছ লাগাই এবং আমাদের বন রক্ষা করি।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the structure first — meaning, causes, effects on climate and wildlife, the Sundarbans, and solutions — then write it in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Remember the main points and connect them with simple linking words like first, as a result, and finally.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
