Drug Addiction Paragraph for Class 6-12, SSC, HSC

August 01, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find drug addiction paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Drug Addiction Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Drug addiction means a person cannot stop using a harmful drug even when it damages health, study and family life. It often begins with curiosity or pressure from friends. What starts as a small experiment can slowly become a strong dependence. The drug slowly takes control of the person's life.

An addicted person may lose interest in study, work and family duties. Drugs damage the body and the mind, waste money and break relationships. Addiction can also lead to accidents and crime, and it brings shame and pain to the whole family.

Prevention is better than cure. Education, sports, creative activities and caring families can keep young people away from drugs. Anyone who is addicted should get help from a doctor or a treatment centre. Hiding the problem only makes it worse. We should say no to drugs firmly, but treat affected people with kindness so they can recover.

Drug Addiction Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Drug addiction means a person cannot stop using a harmful drug even when it damages health, study and family life. It often begins with curiosity or pressure from friends. What starts as a small experiment can slowly become a strong dependence. The drug slowly takes control of the person's life, and the person needs more and more of it to feel normal. Young people are the most at risk, because they want to try new things and they want to be accepted by their friends.

An addicted person may lose interest in study, work and family duties. Drugs damage the body and the mind, waste money and break relationships. The person may steal or borrow money to buy drugs. Addiction can also lead to accidents and crime, and it brings shame and pain to the whole family. Many addicts drop out of school and cannot find a good job later in life. In the end, the addict loses health, money, respect and hope.

Prevention is better than cure. Education, sports, creative activities and caring families can keep young people away from drugs. Parents should talk honestly with their children and notice sudden changes in behaviour. Anyone who is addicted should get help from a doctor, a counsellor or a treatment centre. Hiding the problem only makes it worse. We should say no to drugs firmly, but treat affected people with kindness so they can recover. The government should also stop illegal drug trade and make treatment easy to reach.

Drug Addiction Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Drug addiction means a person cannot stop using a harmful drug even when it damages health, study and family life. It often begins with curiosity or pressure from friends. What starts as a small experiment can slowly become a strong dependence. The drug slowly takes control of the person's life, and the person needs more and more of it to feel normal. Young people are the most at risk, because they want to try new things and they want to be accepted by their friends. A single wrong decision can destroy years of study and the dreams of a family.

An addicted person may lose interest in study, work and family duties. Drugs damage the body and the mind, waste money and break relationships. The person may steal or borrow money to buy drugs. Addiction can also lead to accidents and crime, and it brings shame and pain to the whole family. Many addicts drop out of school and cannot find a good job later in life. Families of addicts often spend their savings on treatment and suffer worry day and night. In the end, the addict loses health, money, respect and hope.

Prevention is better than cure. Education, sports, creative activities and caring families can keep young people away from drugs. Parents should talk honestly with their children and notice sudden changes in behaviour. Anyone who is addicted should get help from a doctor, a counsellor or a treatment centre. Hiding the problem only makes it worse. We should say no to drugs firmly, but treat affected people with kindness so they can recover. The government should also stop illegal drug trade and make treatment easy to reach. A drug-free life is a healthy and happy life, and every young person deserves that chance.

Bangla Meaning of Drug Addiction Paragraph

মাদকাসক্তি অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

মাদকাসক্তি মানে ক্ষতিকর মাদক ছাড়তে না পারা, যদিও তা স্বাস্থ্য, পড়াশোনা ও পারিবারিক জীবন নষ্ট করে। এটি প্রায়ই কৌতূহল বা বন্ধুদের চাপে শুরু হয়।

মাদকাসক্ত ব্যক্তি পড়াশোনা, কাজ ও পরিবারের প্রতি আগ্রহ হারিয়ে ফেলে। মাদক শরীর ও মন নষ্ট করে, অর্থ অপচয় করে এবং সম্পর্ক ভেঙে দেয়।

প্রতিরোধই উত্তম। শিক্ষা, খেলাধুলা, সৃজনশীল কাজ ও যত্নশীল পরিবার তরুণদের মাদক থেকে দূরে রাখতে পারে। আসক্ত ব্যক্তির ডাক্তার বা চিকিৎসাকেন্দ্রের সাহায্য নেওয়া উচিত। মাদককে দৃঢ়ভাবে না বলা উচিত, তবে আক্রান্ত ব্যক্তির সঙ্গে সহানুভূতির আচরণ করা উচিত, যাতে সে সুস্থ হতে পারে। সমস্যা লুকিয়ে রাখলে তা আরও খারাপ হয়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — how addiction begins, its effects, and prevention and treatment — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Write about prevention and kindness together: say no to drugs, but treat affected people with sympathy.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
