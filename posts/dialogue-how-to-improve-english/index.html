Dialogue How to improve English

November 01, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read
DH

A practical plan for better English

Sumi: I understand English lessons but hesitate when I have to speak. How can I improve?

Nadia: Practise a little every day. Read a short article aloud, note unfamiliar words, and use each new word in your own sentence.

Sumi: I often translate every sentence from Bengali before speaking.

Nadia: Try thinking in simple English instead. Mistakes are natural; the important thing is to communicate and learn from corrections.

Sumi: Should I study grammar first?

Nadia: Learn grammar through examples. Keep a notebook of patterns, write a short paragraph each day, and ask your teacher to mark repeated errors.

Sumi: What about listening?

Nadia: Listen to clear educational programmes or conversations. Pause, repeat a sentence, and notice pronunciation. A partner can practise questions and answers with you.

Sumi: That sounds manageable. I will set aside thirty minutes daily instead of studying only before an examination.

Nadia: Regular practice is the key. Language grows through use, not through memorising long word lists alone.
