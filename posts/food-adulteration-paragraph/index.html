Food Adulteration paragraph for Class 6,7,8, HSC, SSC

August 08, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find food adulteration paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Food Adulteration Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Food adulteration means mixing inferior, contaminated or harmful substances into food, or using unsafe chemicals to make food look better or last longer. Milk, spices, oil, fruit, fish, sweets and packaged food can all be affected. Greedy sellers do it to earn more money. It is a serious crime against public health.

Eating adulterated food may cause stomach pain, vomiting and diarrhoea at once, and it can damage organs after repeated use. Children, old people and sick people face the greatest danger. Adulteration is therefore not a small business offence; it threatens the health of the whole nation.

Consumers should buy from reliable shops, check packaging and dates, wash fruit and vegetables, and be careful about unnatural colours or very low prices. We should also report suspicious food to the authorities. Authorities must test food, inspect shops and punish offenders. Honest sellers and informed buyers can help build a safer food market.

Food Adulteration Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Food adulteration means mixing inferior, contaminated or harmful substances into food, or using unsafe chemicals to make food look better or last longer. Milk, spices, oil, fruit, fish, sweets and packaged food can all be affected. Greedy sellers do it to earn more money. Formalin, artificial colours and cheap oil are some of the substances dishonest sellers use. It is a serious crime against public health, not a small business trick.

Eating adulterated food may cause stomach pain, vomiting and diarrhoea at once, and it can damage organs after repeated use. Children, old people and sick people face the greatest danger. Some chemicals used in food can cause serious illness over time. A mother who feeds her child adulterated milk does not know she is harming the child she loves. Adulteration is therefore not a small business offence; it threatens the health of the whole nation. When people lose trust in food, honest sellers also suffer.

Consumers should buy from reliable shops, check packaging and dates, wash fruit and vegetables, and be careful about unnatural colours or very low prices. We should also report suspicious food to the authorities. Authorities must test food, inspect shops and punish offenders. Honest sellers and informed buyers can help build a safer food market and protect every family. Schools and the media should teach people how to spot adulterated food. The government should give strong punishment, because a small fine does not stop a greedy seller.

Food Adulteration Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Food adulteration means mixing inferior, contaminated or harmful substances into food, or using unsafe chemicals to make food look better or last longer. Milk, spices, oil, fruit, fish, sweets and packaged food can all be affected. Greedy sellers do it to earn more money. Formalin, artificial colours and cheap oil are some of the substances dishonest sellers use. It is a serious crime against public health, not a small business trick. In a country where most people buy food from open markets, this danger touches almost every home.

Eating adulterated food may cause stomach pain, vomiting and diarrhoea at once, and it can damage organs after repeated use. Children, old people and sick people face the greatest danger. Some chemicals used in food can cause serious illness over time. A mother who feeds her child adulterated milk does not know she is harming the child she loves. Adulteration is therefore not a small business offence; it threatens the health of the whole nation. When people lose trust in food, honest sellers also suffer. No profit is worth a child's health. Food is a basic need, so cheating with food is cheating with life itself.

Consumers should buy from reliable shops, check packaging and dates, wash fruit and vegetables, and be careful about unnatural colours or very low prices. We should also report suspicious food to the authorities. Authorities must test food, inspect shops and punish offenders. Honest sellers and informed buyers can help build a safer food market and protect every family. Schools and the media should teach people how to spot adulterated food. The government should give strong punishment, because a small fine does not stop a greedy seller. If buyers stay alert and the law stays strict, adulteration can be reduced greatly.

Bangla Meaning of Food Adulteration Paragraph

খাদ্যে ভেজাল অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

খাদ্যে ভেজাল মানে খাবারে নিম্নমানের, দূষিত বা ক্ষতিকর পদার্থ মেশানো, অথবা খাবার ভালো দেখাতে বা বেশি দিন টিকিয়ে রাখতে অনিরাপদ রাসায়নিক ব্যবহার করা।

ভেজাল খাবার খেলে পেটব্যথা, বমি ও ডায়রিয়া হতে পারে, আর বারবার খেলে শরীরের অঙ্গ ক্ষতিগ্রস্ত হতে পারে। শিশু, বৃদ্ধ ও অসুস্থ মানুষ সবচেয়ে বেশি ঝুঁকিতে থাকে।

নির্ভরযোগ্য দোকান থেকে কেনা, প্যাকেজিং ও মেয়াদ যাচাই করা, ফল ও সবজি ধুয়ে খাওয়া এবং অস্বাভাবিক রং বা খুব কম দাম দেখে সতর্ক থাকা উচিত। কর্তৃপক্ষের উচিত খাবার পরীক্ষা করা ও অপরাধীদের শাস্তি দেওয়া। সৎ বিক্রেতা ও সচেতন ক্রেতা মিলে নিরাপদ খাদ্যবাজার গড়তে পারে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — what adulteration is, its dangers, and what buyers and authorities should do — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Cover both sides of the solution: careful buyers and strict punishment for sellers.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
