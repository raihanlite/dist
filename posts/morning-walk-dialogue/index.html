Morning Walk Dialogue for Class 6,7,8, SSC, HSC

October 31, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 5 min read

This dialogue is about the benefits of a morning walk. It is written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The exam question is usually: "Write a dialogue between two friends about the benefits of a morning walk." Read the dialogue below, then learn the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end. The dialogue uses simple English, so you can learn it easily and rewrite it in your own words in the examination.

Dialogue: Morning Walk

Rony: You look energetic today. Did you exercise?

Lima: I took a brisk walk before breakfast. The cool air made it easy to continue.

Rony: What benefits do you notice from a morning walk?

Lima: Walking improves fitness, supports the heart, refreshes the mind, and helps me begin my studies with concentration.

Rony: Do we need special equipment for a morning walk?

Lima: Comfortable shoes and a safe route are enough. We should start gently, keep a steady pace, and drink water after the walk.

Rony: I usually wake late and feel tired all day. I never get time for a walk.

Lima: Try going to bed on time and walking for twenty minutes at first. You do not need to walk for hours to feel the benefit.

Rony: Is it safe to walk alone in the early morning?

Lima: It is safer to walk with a friend or a family member. Choose a road away from heavy traffic and avoid dark, lonely places.

Rony: What else can we do during a morning walk?

Lima: We can enjoy the fresh air, the birds and the rising sun. Some people listen to the news or revise a lesson in their mind while walking.

Rony: That sounds pleasant. I have been wasting my mornings in bed for a long time.

Lima: A morning walk is a simple habit, but it changes the whole day. The body becomes active, the mind becomes calm, and study becomes easier.

Rony: How long should a morning walk be?

Lima: Twenty to thirty minutes is enough for a student. We should not tire ourselves before school.

Rony: What should we wear for a morning walk?

Lima: Light, comfortable clothes and shoes that fit well.

Rony: Does a morning walk help with study?

Lima: Yes. After a walk, the mind is fresh and the memory works better. Many students find that lessons learned in the morning stay in the mind longer.

Rony: I have heard that morning air is cleaner than the air later in the day.

Lima: That is true. There is less dust and smoke in the early morning, and the air feels fresh after the night.

Rony: I will join you tomorrow, provided we choose a safe road away from traffic.

Lima: Good. We will meet at six in the morning near the school gate. Bring a water bottle and wear comfortable shoes.

Bangla Meaning of the Dialogue

সংলাপটির বাংলা অর্থ

রনি দেখে লিমা আজ খুব চনমনে। লিমা বলে, সে নাশতার আগে দ্রুত হেঁটেছে। ঠান্ডা বাতাসে হাঁটতে ভালো লেগেছে। সকালের হাঁটা শরীর ভালো রাখে, হৃদয়ের জন্য উপকারী, মনকে সতেজ করে এবং পড়াশোনায় মনোযোগ বাড়ায়।

সকালের হাঁটার জন্য বিশেষ কিছু লাগে না — আরামদায়ক জুতা আর নিরাপদ রাস্তাই যথেষ্ট। ধীরে শুরু করে নিয়মিত গতিতে হাঁটতে হয় এবং পরে পানি পান করতে হয়। রনি দেরিতে ঘুম থেকে ওঠে বলে সারাদিন ক্লান্ত থাকে। লিমা তাকে সময়মতো ঘুমাতে এবং প্রথমে বিশ মিনিট হাঁটতে বলে। বন্ধুর সঙ্গে হাঁটলে নিরাপদ হয়। শেষে রনি পরদিন সকালে লিমার সঙ্গে হাঁটতে যাওয়ার সিদ্ধান্ত নেয়।

লিমা আরও বলে, বিশ থেকে ত্রিশ মিনিট হাঁটাই একজন ছাত্রের জন্য যথেষ্ট। হাঁটার জন্য হালকা আরামদায়ক পোশাক ও ভালো জুতা দরকার। শীতকালে গরম সোয়েটার পরা উচিত। হাঁটার পর মন সতেজ থাকে এবং স্মৃতিশক্তি ভালো কাজ করে। সকালে শেখা পাঠ মনে থাকে বেশি। সকালের বাতাস দিনের পরের সময়ের চেয়ে পরিষ্কার থাকে, কারণ তখন ধুলো ও ধোঁয়া কম থাকে।

Exam Tips

  • Write the dialogue as a conversation, not as a paragraph. Put the speaker's name and a colon before each line.
  • Keep the language simple and natural, the way two friends really talk in the morning.
  • Include clear benefits of a morning walk — health, fresh air, a calm mind and better study — instead of writing only general sentences.
  • Give the dialogue a clear ending, such as a plan to walk together the next morning.
  • Learn the important words from the table so that you can write the dialogue in your own sentences in the exam.
  • Practise the dialogue with a classmate before the exam so that the questions and answers sound natural.
  • In the examination, write the dialogue in the present tense, because the two friends are talking about a daily habit.
