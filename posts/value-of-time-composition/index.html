Value of Time Composition for Class 6,7,8,SSC,HSC

August 27, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find a value of time composition with an outline, the full composition, a Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips. It is written in simple English for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students, so you can learn the main points and write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.

Outline

  • What time is and why it cannot be bought back
  • How wise people use time: planning, a daily routine and clear priorities
  • How time is wasted: laziness, delay and useless habits
  • The results of using time well and wasting it
  • Why students must learn the value of time early
  • Conclusion: time is life, so use every moment well

Value of Time Composition

Time is the most valuable thing in our life. We can earn money again if we lose it, and we can recover our health with care and treatment, but a moment that has passed can never come back. Time moves forward all the time, and it waits for nobody. That is why wise people say that time is more precious than gold. Every person gets the same twenty-four hours in a day, but some use those hours well and rise in life, while others waste them and fall behind.

A wise person uses time with a plan. He fixes his daily work, gives study, rest, play and family their proper time, and begins his work on time. A student should make a daily routine, study every subject a little every day, and finish his homework before the last moment. When a big task comes, he should divide it into small parts and finish one part each day. In this way, no work becomes too heavy, and the mind stays free from worry. Punctuality is also a part of using time well, because a punctual person earns the trust of others.

On the other hand, many people waste time without knowing it. They sit idle, keep putting off their work for tomorrow, and spend hours on useless chatting and scrolling on the phone. This habit is called procrastination, and it is the greatest enemy of success. When work is left for the last moment, it becomes a burden, and the result is poor. A student who wastes his time in class and at home cannot do well in the examination, however clever he may be. Lost time brings regret, but regret cannot bring back the lost hours.

So we should value time from today. We should set small goals, keep a routine, and do our daily work in time. We should also keep time for rest and play, because a tired mind cannot work well. A person who uses his time well can learn more, do more, and live a happier life. Let us remember that time is life itself. If we waste time, we waste our life. If we use time well, we can build a bright future for ourselves and for our country.

Bangla Meaning

সময়ের মূল্যের বাংলা অর্থ

সময় আমাদের জীবনের সবচেয়ে মূল্যবান সম্পদ। হারানো টাকা আবার আয় করা যায়, অসুস্থ শরীর যত্নে ভালো হতে পারে, কিন্তু যে মুহূর্ত একবার চলে যায় তা আর কখনো ফিরে আসে না। সময় কারো জন্য অপেক্ষা করে না। তাই জ্ঞানী মানুষ সময়কে সোনার চেয়েও দামি মনে করেন।

বুদ্ধিমান মানুষ পরিকল্পনা করে সময় ব্যবহার করেন। তিনি প্রতিদিনের কাজের একটি রুটিন তৈরি করেন, পড়াশোনা, বিশ্রাম, খেলা ও পরিবারকে সঠিক সময় দেন এবং কাজ সময়মতো শুরু করেন। বড় কাজকে ছোট ছোট ভাগে ভাগ করে প্রতিদিন একটু একটু করে শেষ করলে কোনো কাজই ভারী মনে হয় না।

অন্যদিকে অনেকে অলসতা ও কাজ ফেলে রাখার অভ্যাসে সময় নষ্ট করে। এতে কাজ জমে যায়, মন চিন্তায় ভরে যায় এবং পরীক্ষায় ফল খারাপ হয়। তাই আমাদের আজ থেকেই সময়ের মূল্য বুঝতে হবে। সময়মতো কাজ করলে আমরা বেশি শিখতে পারব, বেশি কাজ করতে পারব এবং উজ্জ্বল ভবিষ্যৎ গড়তে পারব।

Exam Tips

  • Learn the outline first — what time is, how to use it, how it is wasted, and the conclusion — then write the composition in your own words in the examination hall.
  • Do not memorise the whole composition. Remember the main points and write them in your own simple, correct sentences.
  • Use short paragraphs: one for the value of time, one for using it well, one for wasting it, and one for the conclusion.
  • Keep your spelling and grammar correct. A few correct sentences are better than many wrong ones.
  • Revise the composition once the night before the exam and read the Bangla meaning to remember the ideas clearly.
