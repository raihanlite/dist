A Thirsty Crow completing story For Class 6,7,8, SSC and HSC

October 24, 2024 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

This is the completing story of a thirsty crow, written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The given beginning is: "Once, on a hot summer day, a crow was very thirsty. It flew here and there in search of water but found none." Read the complete story below, then learn the moral, the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end. The story teaches us that intelligence and patience can solve a problem that strength cannot.

A Thirsty Crow (Complete Story)

Once, on a hot summer day, a crow was very thirsty. It flew here and there in search of water, but it found none. The sun was hot, the fields were dry, and every pond the crow knew had dried up. At last, tired and hopeless, it sat on the branch of a tree and looked around once more.

Then it saw a jar standing in a corner of a yard. The crow flew down quickly and looked inside. A little water lay at the bottom of the jar, but the jar was tall and narrow. The crow pushed its beak inside, but it could not reach the water. It tried to tip the jar over, yet the vessel was too heavy. It pushed the jar with its wings and its feet, but the jar did not move.

The crow felt very sad. It had found water at last, but the water was useless. It looked around the yard for another source and found none. For a moment, it thought of flying away and giving up. Then it noticed some small pebbles lying near the jar.

A new idea came into its mind. It picked up a pebble with its beak and dropped it into the jar. Then it brought another pebble, and another. The work was slow, and the crow was tired, but it did not stop. The water rose little by little, and the crow worked with more hope.

The other birds watched from the trees and wondered what the crow was doing. Some of them laughed at the slow work. But the crow paid no attention to them. It knew that dropping pebbles was the only way to bring the water within reach. Each pebble made a small sound as it fell, and each sound gave the crow a little more hope.

After a long time, the water reached the rim of the jar. At last the crow drank the cool water to its heart's content. Its wings felt light again, and it flew away happily, calling out to the other birds as it went. The solution was not quick, but patient observation had turned a difficult problem into a manageable task. When the first plan fails, a person can examine the materials at hand, think step by step, and continue working rather than complain.

Moral

Intelligence, patience, and perseverance can overcome obstacles.

Bangla Meaning of the Story

গল্পটির বাংলা অর্থ

এক গ্রীষ্মের গরম দিনে একটি কাকের খুব পিপাসা পেল। সে পানির খোঁজে এদিক-ওদিক উড়ল, কিন্তু কোথাও পানি পেল না। রোদ ছিল প্রচণ্ড, মাঠ ছিল শুকনো, আর কাকের চেনা পুকুরগুলোও শুকিয়ে গিয়েছিল।

অবশেষে সে একটি উঠানের কোণে একটি কলসি দেখতে পেল। কলসির তলায় অল্প পানি ছিল, কিন্তু কলসিটি ছিল উঁচু ও সরু। কাক তার ঠোঁট ঢুকিয়েও পানির নাগাল পেল না। কলসিটি উল্টানোর চেষ্টা করল, কিন্তু তা খুব ভারী ছিল। তখন সে কাছাকাছি ছোট ছোট নুড়ি পাথর দেখতে পেল। একটি একটি করে পাথর কলসিতে ফেলতে লাগল। ধীরে ধীরে পানি উপরে উঠে এলো। শেষে কাক তৃপ্তি করে পানি পান করল এবং খুশি মনে উড়ে গেল।

অন্য পাখিরা গাছ থেকে দেখে ভাবছিল কাক কী করছে। কেউ কেউ তার ধীর কাজ দেখে হাসছিল, কিন্তু কাক সেদিকে মনোযোগ দেয়নি। সে জানত, পাথর ফেলাই পানিকে নাগালের মধ্যে আনার একমাত্র উপায়। প্রতিটি পাথর পড়ার শব্দ তাকে আরও আশা দিচ্ছিল। ধীরে ধীরে চেষ্টা করলে কঠিন কাজও সহজ হয় — এই গল্পের শিক্ষা এটাই।

Exam Tips

  • Begin the story with the given beginning exactly as it is written in the question, then continue it in your own words.
  • Keep the events in order: the crow's thirst, the jar, the failed attempts, the pebbles, and the success at the end.
  • Write in the past tense, because the story happened in the past.
  • Do not change the main idea of the story. The crow must solve the problem with its intelligence and patience.
  • End the story with the moral in a separate short paragraph, as shown above.
  • Keep the story short and clear. Do not add new characters or new problems that are not in the original story.
