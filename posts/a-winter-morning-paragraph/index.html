A Winter Morning paragraph

May 07, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find a winter morning paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

A Winter Morning Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A winter morning is a quiet, beautiful morning of the cold season. Before sunrise, everything is covered with thick fog. Trees, houses and roads look blurred in the fog. The air is cold and sharp, so people wrap themselves in warm clothes, shawls and blankets.

When the sun rises, golden light removes the fog. Dew drops shine on the grass like pearls, and birds begin to sing in the trees. Farmers go to the fields, and children hurry to school after warming themselves by the fire. Tea stalls become busy because a hot cup of tea gives comfort in the cold.

A winter morning is pleasant for those who can enjoy it. Date juice, pitha and warm sunshine are the joys of the season. But poor people suffer because they have no warm clothes or shelter. We should help them with food and blankets. A winter morning teaches us both beauty and kindness.

A Winter Morning Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A winter morning is one of the most beautiful sights of the cold season in Bangladesh. It comes slowly, wrapped in thick fog. Before sunrise, trees, houses and roads look blurred, and the far end of the village road disappears in the mist. The air is cold and sharp, and people wrap themselves in sweaters, shawls and blankets. Nature seems asleep, and even the birds remain silent until the sun appears.

The scene changes when the sun rises. Golden light slowly melts the fog, dew drops shine on the grass like pearls, and birds begin to sing in the trees. Farmers lead their cattle to the fields, fishermen go to the rivers, and children hurry to school after warming their hands by a small fire. Tea stalls become busy places because a hot cup of tea gives workers comfort before the day begins. In the villages, people enjoy date juice, pitha and fresh vegetables, which are special foods of winter.

A winter morning is pleasant for those who can enjoy it at leisure, sitting in the warm sunshine. But it is hard for the poor. Many poor people have no warm clothes, no blanket and no proper shelter, so they shiver through the cold morning. Old people and children suffer the most. We should remember them and help them with blankets, warm clothes and food. A winter morning is thus a mixture of beauty and hardship. It delights our eyes, but it also teaches us sympathy and kindness towards those who suffer in the cold.

A Winter Morning Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A winter morning is one of the most beautiful and memorable sights of the cold season in Bangladesh. It arrives slowly, wrapped in thick fog. Before sunrise, trees, rooftops and roads look blurred, and the far end of the village road disappears in the mist. The air is cold and sharp, so people wrap themselves in sweaters, shawls and blankets. Nature seems to sleep, and even the birds remain silent until the first light appears. Everything looks calm and quiet in the fog.

The scene changes when the sun rises. Golden light slowly melts the fog, dew drops shine on the grass like pearls, and birds begin to sing from the branches. Farmers lead their cattle to the fields, fishermen go to the rivers, and children hurry to school after warming their hands by a small fire. Tea stalls become busy, because a hot cup of tea comforts workers before the day begins. In the villages, people enjoy date juice, pitha and warm sunshine, the special joys of winter. A winter morning is fresh, bright and full of life once the fog is gone.

However, a winter morning is not pleasant for everyone. It is a season of suffering for the poor. Many poor people have no warm clothes, no blanket and no proper shelter, so they shiver through the cold morning. Old people and little children suffer the most, and many fall sick. Rich people enjoy the warm sunshine, but the poor cannot enjoy it at all. We should remember them and share our blankets, warm clothes and food with them. A winter morning is thus a mixture of beauty and hardship. It delights our eyes, but it also teaches us that comfort should not make us forget the suffering of others.

Bangla Meaning of A Winter Morning Paragraph

শীতের সকালের বাংলা অর্থ

শীতের সকাল শীতকালের একটি শান্ত ও সুন্দর সকাল। সূর্য ওঠার আগে সবকিছু ঘন কুয়াশায় ঢাকা থাকে। গাছপালা, ঘরবাড়ি ও রাস্তা ঝাপসা দেখায় এবং রাস্তার দূরের প্রান্ত দেখা যায় না। বাতাস ঠান্ডা ও তীক্ষ্ণ হয়, তাই মানুষ গরম কাপড়, শাল ও কম্বল জড়িয়ে নেয়। সূর্য উঠলে সোনালি আলো ধীরে ধীরে কুয়াশা সরিয়ে দেয়, ঘাসের ওপর শিশির মুক্তার মতো জ্বলজ্বল করে এবং পাখি গান গাইতে শুরু করে। কৃষক গরু নিয়ে মাঠে যায়, শিশুরা আগুনে হাত গরম করে স্কুলে ছোটে। খেজুরের রস, পিঠা, তাজা সবজি ও রোদ শীতের আনন্দ। কিন্তু গরিব মানুষের গরম কাপড় ও আশ্রয় নেই বলে তারা কষ্ট পায়। আমাদের কম্বল ও খাবার দিয়ে তাদের সাহায্য করা উচিত। শীতের সকাল আমাদের সৌন্দর্য ও দয়ার শিক্ষা দেয়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the main points — fog before sunrise, the change after sunrise, joys of winter, suffering of the poor — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word; practise writing it once from memory.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block and revise it once the night before the exam.
