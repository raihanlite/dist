Mobile Phone paragraph

September 03, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find mobile phone paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Mobile Phone Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A mobile phone is a portable device used for calls, messages, photography, internet access, navigation, learning and emergency help. Today almost every person carries one in a pocket. Students can use educational apps, dictionaries, recorded lessons and online libraries. Families can stay connected even when members live far apart.

But a phone becomes harmful when it controls the user. Excessive gaming and social media can reduce sleep, attention, physical activity and face-to-face conversation. Many students check their phones hundreds of times a day without noticing it. Distracted driving, unsafe sharing of photos, scams and cyberbullying are other serious risks.

We should keep phones away during study and meals, protect personal information and follow school and road rules. Parents should guide young users and set time limits. We should use the phone as a tool, not let the phone use us. A phone is useful equipment when guided by self-control.

Mobile Phone Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A mobile phone is a portable device used for calls, messages, photography, internet access, navigation, learning and emergency help. Today almost every person carries one in a pocket. Students can use educational apps, dictionaries, recorded lessons and online libraries. Families can stay connected even when members live far apart. A farmer can check market prices, a student can attend a class, and a doctor can advise a patient — all through a phone. In an emergency, a phone can call for help within seconds.

But a phone becomes harmful when it controls the user. Excessive gaming and social media can reduce sleep, attention, physical activity and face-to-face conversation. Many students check their phones hundreds of times a day without noticing it. Notifications steal our attention again and again, so deep study becomes almost impossible. Distracted driving, unsafe sharing of photos, scams and cyberbullying are other serious risks. A phone in the hand of a driver can cause a terrible accident in a single moment.

We should keep phones away during study and meals, protect personal information and follow school and road rules. Parents should guide young users and set time limits. Schools should teach students how to use phones safely. Fixed phone-free hours at home, such as meal times and one hour before sleep, help the whole family. We should use the phone as a tool, not let the phone use us. A phone is useful equipment when guided by self-control, and it becomes a curse when self-control is lost.

Mobile Phone Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A mobile phone is a portable device used for calls, messages, photography, internet access, navigation, learning and emergency help. Today almost every person carries one in a pocket. Students can use educational apps, dictionaries, recorded lessons and online libraries. Families can stay connected even when members live far apart. A farmer can check market prices, a student can attend a class, and a doctor can advise a patient — all through a phone. In an emergency, a phone can call for help within seconds. It has changed the way we talk, shop, learn and travel, and life without it now seems difficult.

But a phone becomes harmful when it controls the user. Excessive gaming and social media can reduce sleep, attention, physical activity and face-to-face conversation. Many students check their phones hundreds of times a day without noticing it. Notifications steal our attention again and again, so deep study becomes almost impossible. Distracted driving, unsafe sharing of photos, scams and cyberbullying are other serious risks. A phone in the hand of a driver can cause a terrible accident in a single moment. Young people should never meet an online stranger alone or share their home address.

We should keep phones away during study and meals, protect personal information and follow school and road rules. Parents should guide young users and set time limits. Schools should teach students how to use phones safely. Fixed phone-free hours at home, such as meal times and one hour before sleep, help the whole family. We should use the phone as a tool, not let the phone use us. A phone is useful equipment when guided by self-control, and it becomes a curse when self-control is lost. In the end, the phone is neither good nor bad by itself; our habits decide what it becomes.

Bangla Meaning of Mobile Phone Paragraph

মোবাইল ফোন অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

মোবাইল ফোন একটি বহনযোগ্য যন্ত্র, যা কল, বার্তা, ছবি, ইন্টারনেট, শেখা ও জরুরি সাহায্যের কাজে ব্যবহৃত হয়। ছাত্ররা শিক্ষামূলক অ্যাপ, অভিধান ও অনলাইন পাঠাগার ব্যবহার করতে পারে।

তবে ফোন যখন ব্যবহারকারীকে নিয়ন্ত্রণ করে, তখন তা ক্ষতিকর হয়। অতিরিক্ত গেম ও সামাজিক মাধ্যম ঘুম, মনোযোগ ও শারীরিক কাজ কমিয়ে দেয়। বাবা-মায়ের উচিত ছোটদের ফোন ব্যবহারে সময়সীমা বেঁধে দেওয়া।

পড়াশোনা ও খাবারের সময় ফোন দূরে রাখা, ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য গোপন রাখা এবং স্কুল ও সড়কের নিয়ম মানা উচিত। আত্মনিয়ন্ত্রণ থাকলে ফোন উপকারী যন্ত্র, না থাকলে তা অভিশাপ। ফোনকে হাতিয়ার হিসেবে ব্যবহার করা উচিত, ফোনের দাস হওয়া নয়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — uses of a phone, its harms, and safe use — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Write about both sides: the benefits of mobile phones and their risks. A one-sided paragraph looks weak.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
