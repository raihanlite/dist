A Liar Cowboy story with moral for class 6,7,8, SSC & HSC

November 02, 2024 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read
AL

A warning that lost its power

A young shepherd watched sheep near a village and became bored with the quiet work. One afternoon he shouted that a wolf was attacking. Villagers ran to help, only to discover that he was laughing at them. He repeated the trick a few days later, and again the frightened people found no wolf.

Later, a real wolf entered the pasture. The shepherd cried for assistance with all his strength, but the villagers thought it was another joke and stayed away. The wolf scattered the flock before the shepherd could drive it off.

The boy returned sadly and admitted what he had done. The villagers explained that trust is easily broken but difficult to restore. A false alarm may seem amusing for a moment, yet repeated lies make even an honest warning sound false.

Moral: A person who makes a habit of lying cannot expect belief in a crisis.
