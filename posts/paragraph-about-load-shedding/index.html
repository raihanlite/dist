Load Shedding Paragraph

May 07, 2026 by Study Koro · 6 min read

On this page you will find a load shedding paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Load Shedding Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Load shedding means stopping the supply of electricity in an area for some time. It happens when the demand for electricity is more than the supply. Sometimes it also happens because of a fault in a power plant, a broken line, or a storm. In Bangladesh, load shedding is a common problem, and it causes much suffering to people.

When the electricity goes off, our daily life comes to a stop. Students cannot study at night, fans and lights stop working, and people suffer in the heat. Food in the refrigerator may go bad, water pumps stop, and shops and factories cannot work. Hospitals also face great trouble because many machines need electricity.

To reduce load shedding, we should use electricity carefully and not waste it. The government should build more power plants and use solar power. If we all save electricity, our life will become easier.

Load Shedding Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Load shedding means the planned stopping of electricity supply in an area for a certain time. It happens when the demand for electricity is greater than the supply. Sometimes it also happens because of a shortage of fuel, a fault in a power plant, damaged lines, or a storm. In Bangladesh, load shedding is a common problem. It disturbs our daily life and slows down the progress of the country.

When the power goes off, everything comes to a stop. Students cannot study at night, and it becomes very hard for them to prepare for examinations. Fans, lights, computers, and mobile phones stop working. People suffer greatly in the heat, and food kept in refrigerators may go bad. Water pumps stop, so people cannot get water easily. Shops, offices, and factories lose working hours, and production falls. During a long power cut, mobile networks may also fail, and students who depend on online classes lose their lessons. This wastes time and money for everyone. Hospitals face serious trouble because many patients depend on machines that run on electricity.

Load shedding can be reduced in many ways. The government should build more power plants, repair old lines, and stop illegal connections. Solar power and other renewable sources should be used more. We should also use electricity wisely. We should switch off lights and fans when we do not need them and use energy-saving bulbs. If the government and the people work together, load shedding can be reduced, and our life will become more comfortable.

Load Shedding Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Load shedding means the planned interruption of electricity supply in an area for a certain period. It takes place when the demand for electricity is greater than the supply. It may also happen because of a shortage of fuel, a fault in a power plant, damaged transmission lines, or a natural disaster like a storm. In Bangladesh, load shedding is a familiar problem. It causes great suffering to people and hampers the development of the country.

Load shedding disturbs every part of our life. Students suffer most because they cannot study at night, especially before examinations. Without light, they cannot read, and without fans, they cannot sleep well in the heat. As a result, their studies are badly hampered. In homes, food kept in refrigerators may spoil, water pumps stop working, and people cannot use computers, televisions, or mobile phones. In hospitals, the problem is even more serious, because the lives of many patients depend on electrical machines. Doctors and nurses face great difficulty during an operation when the power goes off.

Our economy also suffers from load shedding. Factories cannot run their machines, so production falls and workers lose their jobs or working hours. Small shops and businesses lose customers, and farmers cannot run irrigation pumps in time. Repeated power cuts can also damage electrical appliances when the current returns suddenly.

To solve this problem, the government should build more power plants, repair the old power lines, and prevent illegal connections. More use should be made of solar power and other renewable sources of energy. People should also use electricity carefully, switch off unnecessary lights and fans, and use energy-saving appliances. If the authorities publish a regular schedule, people can plan their work. With proper planning and responsible use, load shedding can be reduced, and the country can move forward.

Bangla Meaning of Load Shedding Paragraph

লোডশেডিং অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

লোডশেডিং মানে কোনো এলাকায় কিছু সময়ের জন্য বিদ্যুৎ সরবরাহ বন্ধ রাখা। বিদ্যুতের চাহিদা সরবরাহের চেয়ে বেশি হলে এটি ঘটে। কখনো কখনো বিদ্যুৎকেন্দ্রের ত্রুটি, লাইন নষ্ট হওয়া বা ঝড়ের কারণেও এটি হয়। বাংলাদেশে লোডশেডিং একটি সাধারণ সমস্যা এবং এটি মানুষের অনেক কষ্টের কারণ।

বিদ্যুৎ চলে গেলে আমাদের দৈনন্দিন জীবন থেমে যায়। শিক্ষার্থীরা রাতে পড়াশোনা করতে পারে না, পাখা ও বাতি বন্ধ হয়ে যায় এবং গরমে মানুষ কষ্ট পায়। ফ্রিজের খাবার নষ্ট হতে পারে, পানির পাম্প বন্ধ হয়ে যায় এবং দোকান ও কারখানা কাজ করতে পারে না। হাসপাতালেও বড় সমস্যা হয়, কারণ অনেক যন্ত্র বিদ্যুতে চলে।

লোডশেডিং কমাতে আমাদের বিদ্যুৎ সাবধানে ব্যবহার করা উচিত এবং অপচয় করা উচিত নয়। সরকারের উচিত আরও বিদ্যুৎকেন্দ্র তৈরি করা এবং সৌরশক্তি ব্যবহার করা। আমরা সবাই বিদ্যুৎ সাশ্রয় করলে আমাদের জীবন সহজ হবে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the structure first — meaning, causes, effects on students and hospitals, effects on the economy, and solutions — then write it in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Remember the main points and connect them with simple linking words like firstly, secondly, and finally.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
