A Street Hawker paragraph for Class 6,7,8, SSC, HSC

November 12, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find a Street Hawker paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

A Street Hawker Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A street hawker sells goods from a basket, cart, or temporary stand instead of a permanent shop. Hawkers may offer fruit, vegetables, snacks, clothes, toys, household items, or newspapers. Their flexible prices and convenient locations make them useful to busy customers, especially in crowded neighbourhoods.

Most hawkers work long hours in sun and rain and have little protection from eviction, illness, or loss of stock. Food sellers also face the responsibility of keeping products covered and using clean water. Unplanned stalls can narrow pavements and create waste, so fair rules are needed. Street hawkers contribute to the local economy, and orderly planning can support both their livelihood and the public's right to move safely. Customers should bargain fairly, avoid littering, and choose safe food, because a hawker's small income depends on honest trade. With fair rules, small trade can benefit everyone in the city.

A Street Hawker Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A street hawker sells goods from a basket, cart, or temporary stand instead of a permanent shop. Hawkers may offer fruit, vegetables, snacks, clothes, toys, household items, or newspapers. Their flexible prices and convenient locations make them useful to busy customers, especially in crowded neighbourhoods. A hawker brings goods to the doorstep of people who cannot easily go to a market, and for many poor families, hawking is the only way to earn a living.

Most hawkers work long hours in sun and rain and have little protection from eviction, illness, or loss of stock. They have no fixed income, and a rainy day or a sudden eviction can take away their earnings. Food sellers also face the responsibility of keeping products covered and using clean water, because unclean food can make people ill. Unplanned stalls can narrow pavements and create waste, so fair rules are needed.

Municipal authorities can provide designated vending spaces, waste bins, and hygiene guidance while protecting pedestrian paths. Customers should bargain fairly, avoid littering, and choose safe food. Street hawkers contribute to the local economy, and orderly planning can support both their livelihood and the public's right to move safely. Many hawkers are the only earning members of their families, and their daily income pays for food, rent, and their children's schooling. When a hawker falls ill, his family may go hungry, because there is no paid leave and no savings to fall back on.

A Street Hawker Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A street hawker sells goods from a basket, cart, or temporary stand instead of a permanent shop. Hawkers may offer fruit, vegetables, snacks, clothes, toys, household items, or newspapers. Their flexible prices and convenient locations make them useful to busy customers, especially in crowded neighbourhoods. A hawker brings goods to the doorstep of people who cannot easily go to a market, and for many poor families, hawking is the only way to earn a living. In this way, hawkers serve both buyers and sellers: customers get cheap goods near home, and the hawker earns enough to feed his family.

Most hawkers work long hours in sun and rain and have little protection from eviction, illness, or loss of stock. They have no fixed income, and a rainy day or a sudden eviction can take away their earnings. Many of them borrow money to buy goods, so a loss can push them into debt. Food sellers also face the responsibility of keeping products covered and using clean water, because unclean food can make people ill. Unplanned stalls can narrow pavements and create waste, so fair rules are needed.

Municipal authorities can provide designated vending spaces, waste bins, and hygiene guidance while protecting pedestrian paths. Customers should bargain fairly, avoid littering, and choose safe food. Street hawkers contribute to the local economy, and orderly planning can support both their livelihood and the public's right to move safely. A city should not treat hawkers only as a problem; with fair rules, their small trade can benefit everyone. Some cities have shown that when hawkers are given proper places and clean water, their business improves and the streets stay clean as well. A city should not treat hawkers only as a problem; with fair rules, their small trade can benefit everyone.

Bangla Meaning of A Street Hawker Paragraph

রাস্তার ফেরিওয়ালা অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

রাস্তার ফেরিওয়ালা স্থায়ী দোকানের বদলে ঝুড়ি, ঠেলাগাড়ি বা অস্থায়ী দোকান থেকে পণ্য বিক্রি করে। ফেরিওয়ালারা ফল, শাকসবজি, খাবার, কাপড়, খেলনা, গৃহস্থালি জিনিস বা সংবাদপত্র বিক্রি করতে পারে। তাদের নমনীয় দাম ও সুবিধাজনক জায়গা ব্যস্ত ক্রেতাদের জন্য উপকারী, বিশেষ করে ঘনবসতিপূর্ণ এলাকায়।

বেশিরভাগ ফেরিওয়ালা রোদ-বৃষ্টিতে দীর্ঘ সময় কাজ করে এবং উচ্ছেদ, অসুস্থতা বা মালামালের ক্ষতি থেকে তাদের তেমন সুরক্ষা নেই। খাবার বিক্রেতাদের পণ্য ঢেকে রাখা ও পরিষ্কার পানি ব্যবহারের দায়িত্বও রয়েছে। অপরিকল্পিত দোকান ফুটপাত সরু করে এবং বর্জ্য সৃষ্টি করে, তাই ন্যায্য নিয়ম প্রয়োজন। ফেরিওয়ালারা স্থানীয় অর্থনীতিতে অবদান রাখে এবং সুশৃঙ্খল পরিকল্পনা তাদের জীবিকা ও জনসাধারণের নিরাপদ চলাচল — দুটোই রক্ষা করতে পারে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — what a hawker sells, his hardships, and what fair rules should do — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Keep one paragraph in one block. Do not break it into many small paragraphs unless the question asks for it.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words first.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
