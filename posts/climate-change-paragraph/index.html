Climate Change Paragraph

January 03, 2026 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find a climate change paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version suits junior classes, while the longer versions suit SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Climate Change Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Climate change means a long-term change in the earth's weather patterns. The earth is slowly becoming warmer because heat-trapping gases are entering the atmosphere. Vehicles, factories and power stations release these gases, and cutting down forests makes the problem worse. A warmer planet brings irregular rain, longer heat waves, stronger storms and rising sea levels.

These changes are serious for Bangladesh. Floods and cyclones can damage homes and crops, while salt water may enter fields and drinking-water sources. Poor families suffer most because they have fewer resources to rebuild their lives. Farmers lose crops, and many people lose their homes and livelihoods.

We cannot solve this problem with one action. The government should protect forests, develop clean energy, improve warning systems and plan climate-resilient towns. We can save electricity, plant and protect trees, reduce waste, and use buses or bicycles when possible. Education is important because informed people make better choices. Careful decisions today can protect future generations.

Climate Change Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Climate change means a long-term change in the earth's weather patterns. The earth is slowly becoming warmer because heat-trapping gases are entering the atmosphere. Vehicles, factories and power stations release these gases, and cutting down forests makes the problem worse. A warmer planet brings irregular rain, longer heat waves, stronger storms and rising sea levels. Melting ice in cold regions also adds water to the oceans.

These changes are serious for Bangladesh, a low-lying country with many rivers and a long coastline. Floods and cyclones can damage homes, roads and crops, while salt water may enter fields and drinking-water sources. Poor families suffer most because they have fewer resources to rebuild their lives. Farmers lose crops, fishermen lose their catch, and many people lose their homes and livelihoods. When people lose their income, children may even have to leave school.

We cannot solve this problem with one action. The government should protect forests, develop clean energy, improve warning systems and plan climate-resilient towns. It should also build strong embankments and shelters in coastal areas. Citizens can save electricity, plant and protect trees, reduce waste, and use buses or bicycles when possible. Education is important because informed people make better choices. Careful decisions today can protect future generations.

Climate change is not a distant issue. It is already affecting our weather, our food and our homes. If we act together — government, citizens and students — we can reduce future damage and protect the lives and livelihoods of coming generations.

Climate Change Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Climate change means a long-term change in the earth's weather patterns. The earth is slowly becoming warmer because heat-trapping gases are entering the atmosphere. Vehicles, factories and power stations release these gases, and cutting down forests makes the problem worse. A warmer planet brings irregular rain, longer heat waves, stronger storms and rising sea levels. Melting ice in cold regions also adds water to the oceans. Scientists warn that if this warming continues, the effects will become more severe.

These changes are serious for Bangladesh, a low-lying country with many rivers and a long coastline. Floods and cyclones can damage homes, roads and crops, while salt water may enter fields and drinking-water sources. Poor families suffer most because they have fewer resources to rebuild their lives. Farmers lose crops, fishermen lose their catch, and many people lose their homes and livelihoods. When people lose their income, children may even have to leave school. The burden of climate change therefore falls hardest on those who contributed least to causing it.

We cannot solve this problem with one action. The government should protect forests, develop clean energy, improve warning systems and plan climate-resilient towns. It should also build strong embankments and shelters in coastal areas. Citizens can save electricity, plant and protect trees, reduce waste, and use buses or bicycles when possible. Students can also spread awareness in their families and communities. Education is important because informed people make better choices. Careful decisions today can protect future generations.

Climate change is not a distant issue. It is already affecting our weather, our food and our homes. If we act together — government, citizens and students — we can reduce future damage and protect the lives and livelihoods of coming generations. The earth is our only home, and saving it is the duty of every one of us.

Bangla Meaning of Climate Change Paragraph

জলবায়ু পরিবর্তনের বাংলা অর্থ

জলবায়ু পরিবর্তন মানে পৃথিবীর আবহাওয়ার দীর্ঘমেয়াদি পরিবর্তন। যানবাহন, কারখানা ও বিদ্যুৎকেন্দ্র থেকে তাপ আটকে রাখা গ্যাস বাতাসে মিশছে, আর বন কাটার ফলে সমস্যা আরও বাড়ছে। এতে পৃথিবী ধীরে ধীরে গরম হচ্ছে, বৃষ্টি অনিয়মিত হচ্ছে এবং ঝড় ও বন্যা বাড়ছে।

বাংলাদেশের জন্য এর পরিণতি খুবই ভয়াবহ। বন্যা ও ঘূর্ণিঝড় ঘরবাড়ি ও ফসল নষ্ট করে, আর লবণাক্ত পানি ক্ষেত ও খাবার পানির উৎসে ঢুকে পড়ে। গরিব পরিবার সবচেয়ে বেশি ক্ষতিগ্রস্ত হয়, কারণ তাদের ঘুরে দাঁড়ানোর সামর্থ্য কম।

একটি পদক্ষেপে এই সমস্যা সমাধান করা যাবে না। সরকারের উচিত বন রক্ষা করা, পরিষ্কার জ্বালানি ব্যবহার বাড়ানো ও সতর্কীকরণ ব্যবস্থা উন্নত করা। আমরা বিদ্যুৎ সাশ্রয় করতে পারি, গাছ লাগাতে ও রক্ষা করতে পারি এবং বাস বা সাইকেল ব্যবহার করতে পারি। আজকের সতর্ক সিদ্ধান্ত ভবিষ্যৎ প্রজন্মের জীবন রক্ষা করতে পারে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — causes, effects on Bangladesh, and solutions — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block. Do not break it into many small paragraphs unless the question asks for it.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
