Dengue Fever paragraph for Class 6,7,8, SSC, HSC

August 24, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find dengue fever paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Dengue Fever Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Dengue is a viral illness spread by the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes. These mosquitoes breed in small amounts of clean standing water in buckets, flowerpots, tyres, roof gutters and uncovered tanks. They bite mainly during the day, especially in the early morning and late afternoon.

A patient may develop sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea or a rash. Symptoms usually begin 4 to 10 days after the bite and last for 2 to 7 days. Most people recover in 1 to 2 weeks, but some develop severe dengue and need hospital care.

Warning signs of severe dengue include severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding gums, rapid breathing, tiredness and restlessness. Anyone with these signs should go to a hospital at once. Families can reduce risk by emptying containers, covering stored water and using mosquito nets. Community action is the strongest defence against dengue.

Dengue Fever Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Dengue is a viral illness spread by the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes. These mosquitoes breed in small amounts of clean standing water in buckets, flowerpots, tyres, roof gutters and uncovered tanks. They bite mainly during the day, especially in the early morning and late afternoon. Unlike many mosquitoes, the Aedes mosquito often lives near human homes, so the danger is close to us. It does not spread from person to person by touch.

A patient may develop sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea or a rash. Symptoms usually begin 4 to 10 days after the bite and last for 2 to 7 days. Most people recover in 1 to 2 weeks, but some develop severe dengue and need hospital care. People infected for a second time, as well as young children, pregnant women and old people, face a greater risk of severe dengue.

Warning signs of severe dengue often appear around 3 to 7 days after the illness begins, sometimes just as the fever falls. They include severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding gums, rapid breathing, tiredness and restlessness. Anyone with these signs should go to a hospital at once. A patient should rest, drink plenty of liquids and take paracetamol for pain, but should avoid aspirin and ibuprofen, which increase the risk of bleeding. Families can reduce risk by emptying containers, covering stored water and using mosquito nets. Community action is the strongest defence against dengue.

Dengue Fever Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Dengue is a viral illness spread by the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes. These mosquitoes breed in small amounts of clean standing water in buckets, flowerpots, tyres, roof gutters and uncovered tanks. They bite mainly during the day, especially in the early morning and late afternoon. Unlike many mosquitoes, the Aedes mosquito often lives near human homes, so the danger is close to us. It does not spread from person to person by touch. The rainy season is the most dangerous time, because rain fills the containers where mosquitoes breed.

A patient may develop sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea or a rash. Symptoms usually begin 4 to 10 days after the bite and last for 2 to 7 days. Most people recover in 1 to 2 weeks, but some develop severe dengue and need hospital care. People infected for a second time, as well as young children, pregnant women and old people, face a greater risk of severe dengue. High fever may reach 40 degrees Celsius, so the illness is sometimes called break-bone fever for the severe body pain it causes.

Warning signs of severe dengue often appear around 3 to 7 days after the illness begins, sometimes just as the fever falls. They include severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding gums, rapid breathing, tiredness and restlessness. Anyone with these signs should go to a hospital at once. A patient should rest, drink plenty of liquids and take paracetamol for pain, but should avoid aspirin and ibuprofen, which increase the risk of bleeding. Families can reduce risk by emptying containers, covering stored water and using mosquito nets. Community action is the strongest defence against dengue. Schools and local authorities should keep public areas clean, remove standing water and spread correct information.

Bangla Meaning of Dengue Fever Paragraph

ডেঙ্গু জ্বর অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

ডেঙ্গু একটি ভাইরাসজনিত রোগ, যা সংক্রমিত এডিস মশার কামড়ে ছড়ায়। এই মশা বালতি, ফুলের টব, টায়ার, ছাদের নালা ও খোলা ট্যাংকে জমে থাকা অল্প পানিতে বংশবৃদ্ধি করে। মশা মূলত দিনের বেলা কামড়ায়, তাই দিনে সতর্ক থাকা জরুরি।

আক্রান্ত ব্যক্তির হঠাৎ উচ্চ জ্বর, তীব্র মাথাব্যথা, চোখের পেছনে ব্যথা, পেশি ও গাঁটে ব্যথা, বমি ভাব বা ফুসকুড়ি হতে পারে। বেশিরভাগ মানুষ এক থেকে দুই সপ্তাহে সুস্থ হয়ে যায়।

তীব্র পেটব্যথা, বারবার বমি, মাড়ি থেকে রক্ত, দ্রুত শ্বাস ও অস্বাভাবিক ঘুমঘুম ভাব দেখা দিলে দ্রুত হাসপাতালে যেতে হবে। পাত্রের পানি ফেলে দেওয়া, জমা পানি ঢেকে রাখা ও মশারি ব্যবহার করলে ঝুঁকি কমে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — how dengue spreads, its symptoms, warning signs, and prevention — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Remember the warning signs: severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding gums and rapid breathing mean hospital care at once.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
