Padma Bridge paragraph for Class 6,7,8,SSC,HSC

October 07, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find a Padma Bridge paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Padma Bridge Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

The Padma Multipurpose Bridge is one of Bangladesh's most important transport projects. It crosses the broad Padma River and links Mawa in Munshiganj with Jajira in Shariatpur. In this way it gives the south-western districts a direct connection with Dhaka and the northern and eastern parts of the country.

The bridge was inaugurated on 25 June 2022 and opened to road traffic the next day. It is about 6.15 kilometres long and has two levels: the upper deck carries a four-lane road, while the lower deck is designed for a single railway track. Forty-two piers support forty-one steel spans. The bridge has reduced dependence on ferries and made travel more predictable. Farmers can send products to markets more quickly, and people in the connected districts can reach hospitals, schools, and workplaces with greater ease. Trains began crossing the bridge in October 2023, and the project was financed from Bangladesh's own public resources.

Padma Bridge Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

The Padma Multipurpose Bridge is one of Bangladesh's most important transport projects. It crosses the broad Padma River and links Mawa in Munshiganj with Jajira in Shariatpur. In this way it gives the south-western districts a direct connection with Dhaka and the northern and eastern parts of the country. Before the bridge, people of the south-west had to depend on ferries, and a journey could take many hours or stop completely in bad weather.

The bridge was inaugurated on 25 June 2022 and opened to road traffic the next day. It is about 6.15 kilometres long and has two levels: the upper deck carries a four-lane road, while the lower deck is designed for a single railway track. Forty-two piers support forty-one steel spans, each about 150 metres long. The project was financed from Bangladesh's own public resources and built by China Major Bridge Engineering Company. Trains began crossing the bridge in October 2023.

The bridge has reduced dependence on ferries and made travel more predictable. Farmers can send products to markets more quickly, businesses can move goods efficiently, and people in the connected districts can reach hospitals, schools, and workplaces with greater ease. Its value is not only structural; it represents engineering confidence and a long-term investment in regional development. Before the bridge, a journey from the south-west to Dhaka could take many hours, and in fog or storms the ferries stopped completely. Now the same journey takes far less time, and people can travel at any hour with confidence.

Padma Bridge Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

The Padma Multipurpose Bridge is one of Bangladesh's most important transport projects. It crosses the broad Padma River and links Mawa in Munshiganj with Jajira in Shariatpur. In this way it gives the south-western districts a direct connection with Dhaka and the northern and eastern parts of the country. Before the bridge, people of the south-west had to depend on ferries, and a journey could take many hours or stop completely in fog, storms, or strong currents. The bridge has ended that uncertainty and joined the two banks of the river by road for the first time.

The bridge was inaugurated on 25 June 2022 and opened to road traffic the next day. It is about 6.15 kilometres long and has two levels: the upper deck carries a four-lane road, while the lower deck is designed for a single railway track. Forty-two piers support forty-one steel spans, each about 150 metres long. Building a bridge across the Padma was a great engineering challenge, because the river is wide, deep, and changes its course. The project was financed from Bangladesh's own public resources and built by China Major Bridge Engineering Company. Trains began crossing the bridge in October 2023.

The bridge has reduced dependence on ferries and made travel more predictable. Farmers can send products to markets more quickly, businesses can move goods efficiently, and people in the connected districts can reach hospitals, schools, and workplaces with greater ease. Its value is not only structural; it represents engineering confidence and a long-term investment in regional development. For the people of the south-west, the Padma Bridge is not only a bridge but also a symbol of what the country can achieve. May this bridge inspire the next generation to dream big and to build even greater things for the country.

Bangla Meaning of Padma Bridge Paragraph

পদ্মা সেতু অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

পদ্মা বহুমুখী সেতু বাংলাদেশের অন্যতম গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পরিবহন প্রকল্প। এটি প্রশস্ত পদ্মা নদী পার হয়ে মুন্সীগঞ্জের মাওয়া ও শরীয়তপুরের জাজিরাকে যুক্ত করেছে। এর ফলে দক্ষিণ-পশ্চিমাঞ্চলের জেলাগুলো ঢাকা এবং দেশের উত্তর ও পূর্বাঞ্চলের সঙ্গে সরাসরি যুক্ত হয়েছে।

সেতুটি ২০২২ সালের ২৫ জুন উদ্বোধন করা হয় এবং পরদিন সড়ক চলাচলের জন্য খুলে দেওয়া হয়। এটি প্রায় ৬.১৫ কিলোমিটার দীর্ঘ এবং দুই স্তরবিশিষ্ট: ওপরের স্তরে চার লেনের সড়ক এবং নিচের স্তরে একক রেললাইন। ৪২টি পিলারের ওপর ৪১টি ইস্পাতের স্প্যান বসানো হয়েছে। সেতুটি ফেরির ওপর নির্ভরতা কমিয়েছে এবং যাতায়াতকে আরও নির্ভরযোগ্য করেছে। কৃষকরা দ্রুত পণ্য বাজারে পাঠাতে পারে এবং সংযুক্ত জেলার মানুষ হাসপাতাল, স্কুল ও কর্মস্থলে সহজে পৌঁছাতে পারে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Remember the key facts: 6.15 kilometres long, 41 spans, 42 piers, inaugurated 25 June 2022, opened to traffic 26 June 2022.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — what it connects, its size, and its benefits — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words first.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
