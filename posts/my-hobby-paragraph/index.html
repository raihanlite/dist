My Hobby Paragraph

April 16, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find a My Hobby paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

My Hobby Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

My favourite hobby is gardening. I grow flowers, herbs, and a few vegetables in pots and in a small patch beside our home. I loosen the soil, sow seeds, water the plants, and remove weeds. Watching a seed become a living plant teaches me patience and care.

Gardening refreshes me after study and gives me gentle exercise. Green plants make the surroundings cooler and more attractive, while herbs and vegetables can provide fresh food. I reuse kitchen containers as planters and avoid wasting water. Sometimes I share seedlings with my neighbours. This hobby has made me more interested in nature and more aware that healthy surroundings depend on daily responsibility. I also keep a small diary where I write the names of new plants and note when I sowed their seeds. This diary helps me remember what worked and what did not.

My Hobby Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

My favourite hobby is gardening. I grow flowers, herbs, and a few vegetables in pots and in a small patch beside our home. I loosen the soil, sow seeds, water the plants, and remove weeds. Watching a seed become a living plant teaches me patience and care, because a plant does not grow in a single day. I have learned the names of many plants and flowers, and I enjoy seeing bees and butterflies visit my garden.

Gardening refreshes me after study and gives me gentle exercise. It takes my mind away from books and mobile screens for a while, and the fresh air makes me feel calm. Green plants make the surroundings cooler and more attractive, while herbs and vegetables can provide fresh food for my family. I reuse kitchen containers as planters, collect rainwater when I can, and avoid wasting water. Sometimes I share seedlings with my neighbours, and we exchange advice about plants. This hobby has made me more interested in nature and more aware that healthy surroundings depend on daily responsibility. A small garden can teach us that good results need regular work, not only big plans. On weekends, I sometimes visit a nursery to learn about new plants and to buy seeds for the next season. The nursery workers often give useful advice about soil, water, and sunlight, and I try to follow their suggestions at home. Gardening has also taught me the value of patience, because a garden rewards those who wait and work steadily.

My Hobby Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

My favourite hobby is gardening. I grow flowers, herbs, and a few vegetables in pots and in a small patch beside our home. I loosen the soil, sow seeds, water the plants, and remove weeds. Watching a seed become a living plant teaches me patience and care, because a plant does not grow in a single day. I have learned the names of many plants and flowers, and I enjoy seeing bees and butterflies visit my garden. When a plant I planted with my own hands begins to flower, I feel a quiet joy that no bought thing can give.

Gardening refreshes me after study and gives me gentle exercise. It takes my mind away from books and mobile screens for a while, and the fresh air makes me feel calm and cheerful. Green plants make the surroundings cooler and more attractive, while herbs and vegetables can provide fresh food for my family. A garden also helps the environment, because plants give oxygen and keep the air clean. I reuse kitchen containers as planters, collect rainwater when I can, and avoid wasting water. Sometimes I share seedlings with my neighbours, and we exchange advice about plants and seeds.

This hobby has made me more interested in nature and more aware that healthy surroundings depend on daily responsibility. It has taught me that good results need regular work, not only big plans. A hobby should give both pleasure and learning, and gardening gives me both. I hope to make my garden bigger one day and to teach my younger brother how to plant and care for trees. When friends visit my home, I show them my garden first, and many of them have started small gardens of their own. That is the best reward of a hobby.

Bangla Meaning of My Hobby Paragraph

আমার শখ অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

আমার প্রিয় শখ হলো বাগান করা। আমি টবে এবং বাড়ির পাশের ছোট জায়গায় ফুল, ভেষজ গাছ ও কিছু শাকসবজি চাষ করি। মাটি আলগা করে বীজ বপন করি, গাছে পানি দিই এবং আগাছা পরিষ্কার করি। একটি বীজ থেকে জীবন্ত গাছ হতে দেখা আমাকে ধৈর্য ও যত্ন শেখায়।

বাগান করা পড়াশোনার পর আমাকে সতেজ করে এবং হালকা ব্যায়াম হয়। সবুজ গাছপালা চারপাশ ঠান্ডা ও সুন্দর করে, আর ভেষজ ও শাকসবজি থেকে তাজা খাবার পাওয়া যায়। আমি রান্নাঘরের পুরোনো পাত্র গাছের টব হিসেবে ব্যবহার করি এবং পানি অপচয় করি না। মাঝে মাঝে প্রতিবেশীদের সঙ্গে চারা ভাগ করে নিই। এই শখ আমাকে প্রকৃতির প্রতি আরও আগ্রহী করেছে এবং শিখিয়েছে যে সুস্থ পরিবেশ প্রতিদিনের যত্নের ওপর নির্ভর করে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • If your hobby is different, use the same structure — what it is, what you do, how it helps you, and what you learned — and write about your own hobby.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points and write them in your own sentences.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words first.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
