A Book Fair Paragraph for Class 6, 7, 8, SSC, HSC

October 12, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find a book fair paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

A Book Fair Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A book fair is a temporary marketplace where publishers, booksellers, writers and readers meet. Rows of colourful stalls in one place display storybooks, novels, science books, biographies, dictionaries and works by local authors. Visitors can look at new books, compare prices and meet writers.

The fair is enjoyable, but its greatest value is educational. It introduces children to many kinds of reading and encourages families to make books part of everyday life. A child who loves books becomes a thoughtful person. Publishers also use the fair to present new ideas and support young writers.

In Bangladesh, the Amar Ekushey Book Fair is held in Dhaka in February by Bangla Academy. It honours the martyrs of the Language Movement of 1952. When I visit a fair, I make a list before entering so that excitement does not make me waste money. A well-organised book fair combines recreation with learning.

A Book Fair Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A book fair is a temporary marketplace where publishers, booksellers, writers and readers meet. Rows of colourful stalls in one place display storybooks, novels, science books, biographies, dictionaries and works by local authors. Visitors can look at new books, compare prices and meet writers at discussions or signing sessions. It is a festival of books, and for many families it is the happiest day of the year.

The fair is enjoyable, but its greatest value is educational. It introduces children to many kinds of reading and encourages families to make books part of everyday life. A child who loves books becomes a thoughtful person. Publishers also use the fair to present new ideas and support young writers. Writers sign books, answer questions and inspire young readers to write their own stories. A fair may include lectures, quizzes, exhibitions and cultural programmes as well.

In Bangladesh, the Amar Ekushey Book Fair is held in Dhaka in February by Bangla Academy. It honours the martyrs of the Language Movement of 1952, who gave their lives for the Bangla language. Thousands of people visit the fair every day, and new books appear by the thousand. When I visit a fair, I make a list before entering so that excitement does not make me waste money. I choose a few useful books and spend time reading the displays. A well-organised book fair therefore combines recreation with learning and helps build a thoughtful, informed community for all.

A Book Fair Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A book fair is a temporary marketplace where publishers, booksellers, writers and readers meet. Rows of colourful stalls in one place display storybooks, novels, science books, biographies, dictionaries and works by local authors. Visitors can look at new books, compare prices and meet writers at discussions or signing sessions. It is a festival of books, and for many families it is the happiest day of the year. Children crowd around picture books, students look for textbooks, and parents search for books their children will love.

The fair is enjoyable, but its greatest value is educational. It introduces children to many kinds of reading and encourages families to make books part of everyday life. A child who loves books becomes a thoughtful person. Publishers also use the fair to present new ideas and support young writers. Writers sign books, answer questions and inspire young readers to write their own stories. A fair may include lectures, quizzes, exhibitions and cultural programmes as well. A book fair also helps the book trade, because publishers can see which books readers want.

In Bangladesh, the Amar Ekushey Book Fair is held in Dhaka in February by Bangla Academy. It honours the martyrs of the Language Movement of 1952, who gave their lives for the Bangla language. Thousands of people visit the fair every day, and new books appear by the thousand. When I visit a fair, I make a list before entering so that excitement does not make me waste money. I choose a few useful books and spend time reading the displays. A well-organised book fair therefore combines recreation with learning and helps build a thoughtful, informed community for all. Books are the best friends of a student, and a book fair is the best place to meet them.

Bangla Meaning of A Book Fair Paragraph

বইমেলা অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

বইমেলা হলো একটি অস্থায়ী বাজার, যেখানে প্রকাশক, বই বিক্রেতা, লেখক ও পাঠক মিলিত হন। সারি সারি রঙিন স্টলে গল্পের বই, উপন্যাস, বিজ্ঞানের বই ও জীবনী সাজানো থাকে।

বইমেলা আনন্দের, তবে এর সবচেয়ে বড় মূল্য শিক্ষামূলক। এটি শিশুদের নানা ধরনের বইয়ের সঙ্গে পরিচয় করিয়ে দেয় এবং পরিবারকে বইকে জীবনের অংশ করতে উৎসাহ দেয়।

বাংলাদেশে অমর একুশে বইমেলা ফেব্রুয়ারি মাসে ঢাকায় বাংলা একাডেমি আয়োজন করে। এটি ১৯৫২ সালের ভাষা আন্দোলনের শহীদদের স্মরণে অনুষ্ঠিত হয়। বইমেলায় যাওয়ার আগে তালিকা করে নিলে অযথা অর্থ নষ্ট হয় না। সুসংগঠিত বইমেলা বিনোদন ও শিক্ষাকে একসঙ্গে মেলায়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — what a book fair is, its value for readers, and the Ekushey Book Fair — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Mention the Amar Ekushey Book Fair by name: Bangla Academy, Dhaka, February, in honour of the Language Movement martyrs of 1952.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
