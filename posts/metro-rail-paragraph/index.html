Dhaka Metro Rail paragraph for Class 6,7,8, SSC, HSC

December 15, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find a Metro Rail paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Metro Rail Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Metro rail is a rapid urban transport system in which electric trains travel along dedicated tracks, often above busy roads. It can carry large numbers of passengers quickly and reduce pressure on crowded streets. In a growing city, a dependable metro service saves commuting time, lowers fuel use, and can reduce air pollution and traffic noise.

Dhaka's first metro line, MRT Line 6, runs from Uttara North to Motijheel. Its first section, Uttara North to Agargaon, opened on 28 December 2022, and the Agargaon to Motijheel section opened on 4 November 2023. Passengers should queue, buy the correct ticket, keep stations clean, and offer priority seats to those who need them. With responsible use and careful expansion, metro rail can make urban travel cleaner and more efficient. The line is being extended to Kamalapur, the city's main railway station, and more metro lines are planned for the future.

Metro Rail Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Metro rail is a rapid urban transport system in which electric trains travel along dedicated tracks, often above busy roads or underground. It can carry large numbers of passengers quickly and reduce pressure on crowded streets. In a growing city, a dependable metro service saves commuting time, lowers fuel use, and can reduce air pollution and traffic noise. Because the trains run on electricity and do not stop at traffic signals, journeys become faster and more predictable.

Dhaka's first metro line, MRT Line 6, runs from Uttara North to Motijheel, passing through Mirpur, Agargaon, Farmgate, and Shahbagh. Its first section, Uttara North to Agargaon, opened on 28 December 2022, and the Agargaon to Motijheel section opened on 4 November 2023. The line is about 20 kilometres long with 16 stations, and it is operated by Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited. The line is being extended to Kamalapur, the city's main railway station.

Passengers should queue, buy the correct ticket, keep stations clean, and offer priority seats to those who need them. Safety rules must be followed near platforms and doors. Metro rail alone cannot solve every transport problem; buses, walking routes, cycling facilities, and good planning are also necessary. With responsible use and careful expansion, it can make urban travel cleaner and more efficient. Students and office workers now reach their destinations on time, and many families have moved closer to metro stations to enjoy this benefit. Clean stations and polite behaviour make the journey pleasant for all.

Metro Rail Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Metro rail is a rapid urban transport system in which electric trains travel along dedicated tracks, often above busy roads or underground. It can carry large numbers of passengers quickly and reduce pressure on crowded streets. In a growing city, a dependable metro service saves commuting time, lowers fuel use, and can reduce air pollution and traffic noise. Because the trains run on electricity and do not stop at traffic signals, journeys become faster and more predictable, and people can plan their day with confidence.

Dhaka's first metro line, MRT Line 6, runs from Uttara North to Motijheel, passing through Mirpur, Agargaon, Farmgate, and Shahbagh. Its first section, Uttara North to Agargaon, opened on 28 December 2022, and the Agargaon to Motijheel section opened on 4 November 2023. The line is about 20 kilometres long with 16 stations, and it is operated by Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited. The line is being extended to Kamalapur, the city's main railway station, and more metro lines are planned for the future. For many office workers and students, the metro has changed a journey that once took hours into a journey of less than an hour.

Passengers should queue, buy the correct ticket, keep stations clean, and offer priority seats to those who need them. Safety rules must be followed near platforms and doors, and people should not push or block the doors. Metro rail alone cannot solve every transport problem; buses, walking routes, cycling facilities, and good planning are also necessary. With responsible use and careful expansion, metro rail can make urban travel cleaner, safer, and more efficient for everyone. Other cities of Bangladesh may also get metro rail in the future, but Dhaka's experience shows that such projects need careful planning, honest management, and patient passengers. A good metro is a shared treasure.

Bangla Meaning of Metro Rail Paragraph

মেট্রোরেল অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

মেট্রোরেল একটি দ্রুতগতির নগর পরিবহন ব্যবস্থা, যেখানে বৈদ্যুতিক ট্রেন নির্দিষ্ট লাইনে চলে, প্রায়ই ব্যস্ত রাস্তার ওপরে। এটি অল্প সময়ে অনেক যাত্রী বহন করতে পারে এবং ব্যস্ত রাস্তার চাপ কমায়। নির্ভরযোগ্য মেট্রো সেবা যাতায়াতের সময় বাঁচায়, জ্বালানির ব্যবহার কমায় এবং বায়ুদূষণ ও যানজট কমাতে সাহায্য করে।

ঢাকার প্রথম মেট্রো লাইন এমআরটি লাইন ৬ উত্তরা উত্তর থেকে মতিঝিল পর্যন্ত চলে। এর প্রথম অংশ উত্তরা উত্তর থেকে আগারগাঁও ২৮ ডিসেম্বর ২০২২ সালে চালু হয় এবং আগারগাঁও থেকে মতিঝিল অংশ ৪ নভেম্বর ২০২৩ সালে চালু হয়। যাত্রীদের সারিতে দাঁড়ানো, সঠিক টিকিট কেনা, স্টেশন পরিষ্কার রাখা এবং প্রয়োজনীয় ব্যক্তিদের অগ্রাধিকার আসন ছেড়ে দেওয়া উচিত। দায়িত্বশীল ব্যবহার ও সতর্ক সম্প্রসারণের মাধ্যমে মেট্রোরেল নগর যাতায়াতকে আরও পরিচ্ছন্ন ও কার্যকর করতে পারে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Remember the two opening dates: 28 December 2022 (Uttara North to Agargaon) and 4 November 2023 (Agargaon to Motijheel).
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — what metro rail is, the Dhaka line, and passenger duties — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words first.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
