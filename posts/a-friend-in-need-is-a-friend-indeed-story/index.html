A Friend in Need is a Friend Indeed Completing story for all classes

February 17, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 5 min read

This is the completing story of a friend in need, written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The given beginning is: "Two friends were travelling through a forest. Suddenly, they heard a bear approaching and one of them climbed a tree." Read the complete story below, then learn the moral, the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end.

A Friend in Need is a Friend Indeed (Complete Story)

Two friends were travelling through a forest. They had been walking since morning, talking and laughing together. The forest was quiet, and the path was narrow. Suddenly, they heard a bear approaching, and one of them quickly climbed a tree. He did not think of his friend at all. He only thought of his own safety.

The other friend was left alone on the ground. He had no time to climb a tree, and he knew he could not run faster than a bear. He remembered that a bear is unlikely to touch a person who appears lifeless, so he held his breath and lay flat on the ground without moving.

The bear came close and examined him carefully. It sniffed his face and his ears, and it pushed his body gently with its paw. The man did not move and did not make a sound. At last, the bear decided that the man was dead and moved away into the forest.

The man on the ground lay still for a long time even after the bear had gone. His heart was beating fast, and his hands were shaking. At last he stood up slowly and looked around to make sure the forest was safe. He felt angry and sad at the same time, because the friend he trusted had left him alone in danger.

When the danger had passed, the man in the tree climbed down. He was smiling, as if nothing serious had happened. He jokingly asked his friend what the bear had whispered in his ear.

His companion stood up, dusted his clothes, and replied calmly that the animal had advised him never to trust a person who abandons a friend in danger. The words were calm, but the lesson was serious.

The man in the tree felt ashamed. He understood that he had failed the test of friendship. Friendship is not measured by cheerful conversations or shared meals. It is revealed when help is inconvenient and fear is real. A dependable friend offers support instead of searching for personal safety first.

The two friends continued their journey, but the friendship was never the same again. The man who had been left alone learned to choose his friends more carefully, and the other learned that a true friend stands beside us during difficulty.

Moral

A true friend stands beside us during difficulty. A friend in need is a friend indeed.

Bangla Meaning of the Story

গল্পটির বাংলা অর্থ

দুই বন্ধু বনের মধ্যে দিয়ে যাচ্ছিল। হঠাৎ তারা একটি ভালুক আসছে শুনতে পেল। একজন দ্রুত গাছে উঠে গেল, আর অন্যজন মাটিতে পড়ে রইল। সে জানত, ভালুক মৃত মনে করা মানুষকে স্পর্শ করে না। তাই সে নিঃশ্বাস বন্ধ করে নিশ্চল হয়ে শুয়ে রইল।

ভালুক কাছে এসে তাকে ভালোভাবে পরীক্ষা করল, তার মুখ ও কান শুঁকল। শেষে তাকে মৃত মনে করে চলে গেল। বিপদ কেটে গেলে গাছের বন্ধুটি নেমে এসে মজা করে জিজ্ঞেস করল, ভালুক তার কানে কী বলেছে। বন্ধুটি শান্তভাবে উত্তর দিল, ভালুক তাকে পরামর্শ দিয়েছে বিপদে বন্ধুকে ফেলে পালিয়ে যাওয়া মানুষকে কখনো বিশ্বাস না করতে। বন্ধুত্বের আসল পরীক্ষা হয় বিপদের সময়েই।

ভালুক চলে যাওয়ার পরও লোকটি অনেকক্ষণ নিশ্চল হয়ে শুয়ে রইল। তার বুক ধড়ফড় করছিল এবং হাত কাঁপছিল। অবশেষে সে ধীরে ধীরে উঠে দাঁড়াল এবং চারদিকে তাকিয়ে দেখল বন নিরাপদ কি না। একই সঙ্গে তার রাগ ও দুঃখ হচ্ছিল, কারণ যাকে সে বিশ্বাস করত সেই বন্ধুই তাকে বিপদে একা ফেলে গিয়েছিল।

Exam Tips

  • Begin the story with the given beginning exactly as it is written in the question, then continue it in your own words.
  • Keep the events in order: the bear appears, one friend climbs the tree, the other pretends to be dead, the bear leaves, and the friend gives his reply.
  • Write in the past tense, because the story happened in the past.
  • Do not change the main idea of the story. The friend on the ground must survive by his own presence of mind, and the moral must come from his reply.
  • End the story with the moral in a separate short paragraph, as shown above.
  • Write the friend's reply as direct speech. The reply carries the moral, so it should be clear and complete.
