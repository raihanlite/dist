The Pied Piper of Hamelin Completing Story

August 21, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

This is the completing story of the Pied Piper of Hamelin, written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The given beginning is: "Long ago, the town of Hamelin was full of rats. They entered houses, spoiled food, and frightened the people." Read the complete story below, then learn the moral, the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end. The story teaches us that a promise must be kept.

The Pied Piper of Hamelin (Complete Story)

Long ago, the town of Hamelin was full of rats. They entered houses, spoiled food, and frightened the people. The rats were so many that the people could not eat or sleep in peace. They chewed clothes, bit babies in their cradles, and ran through the streets day and night. The people went to the mayor and asked him to save the town.

The mayor promised a large reward to anyone who could rid the town of the pests. Many people tried, but no one succeeded. Some set traps, some used poison, and some kept cats in their houses, but the rats were too many and too clever. Then a travelling piper arrived at the town gate. He wore a bright coat and carried a long pipe. He said that his music could lead the rats away.

The mayor agreed to pay him a thousand gold coins if he could free the town. The piper stood in the market place and played a strange, bright tune. At once, every rat in the town came out of its hiding place. The rats followed the piper through the streets and out to the river. He guided them safely away, and the town was free at last.

The piper returned to claim the promised payment. But the mayor had expected the piper to work for less, so he refused to pay the full reward. He offered only a small sum and told the piper to be glad of it.

The piper warned him that a promise made publicly must be honoured, but the mayor dismissed him with laughter. The piper left the town hall quietly and stood in the street. Then he played another tune. This time the tune was sweet and sad, and every child in Hamelin followed him beyond the gates.

When the mayor and the citizens realised what was happening, they ran after the piper and begged him to return the children. The piper stopped and said that he would bring them back only after the town paid what it had promised and apologised for breaking its word. The mayor agreed at once. The piper played his pipe again, and the children came back to their homes. From then on, the leaders of Hamelin kept their promises.

Moral

A promise is a responsibility, not an opportunity for dishonest bargaining.

Bangla Meaning of the Story

গল্পটির বাংলা অর্থ

বহু আগে হ্যামেলিন শহর ইঁদুরে ভরে গিয়েছিল। ইঁদুরগুলো ঘরে ঢুকে খাবার নষ্ট করত এবং মানুষকে ভয় দেখাত। মেয়র ঘোষণা করলেন, যে শহরকে ইঁদুরমুক্ত করতে পারবে তাকে বড় পুরস্কার দেওয়া হবে।

একদিন এক ভ্রমণকারী বংশীবাদক এলো। সে তার বাঁশির সুরে সব ইঁদুরকে শহরের বাইরে নদীর দিকে নিয়ে গেল। কিন্তু কাজ শেষ হলে মেয়র পুরো পুরস্কার দিতে অস্বীকার করলেন। তখন বংশীবাদক আবার বাঁশি বাজাল, আর এবার শহরের সব শিশু তার পিছু পিছু চলে গেল। মেয়র ও নগরবাসী ক্ষমা চেয়ে প্রতিশ্রুত পুরস্কার দিলে বংশীবাদক শিশুদের ফিরিয়ে আনল। এরপর থেকে হ্যামেলিনের নেতারা প্রতিশ্রুতি রক্ষা করতেন।

অনেকেই চেষ্টা করেছিল — কেউ ফাঁদ পেতেছিল, কেউ বিষ ব্যবহার করেছিল, আবার কেউ বাড়িতে বিড়াল পুষেছিল। কিন্তু ইঁদুরের সংখ্যা এত বেশি ছিল যে কিছুই কাজ করল না। তখনই বংশীবাদক এসে তার সুরের জাদুতে সব ইঁদুরকে নদীর দিকে নিয়ে গেল। মেয়রের প্রতিশ্রুতি ভঙ্গের পরিণাম শহরবাসীকে শিখিয়েছিল, কথা রাখা কতটা জরুরি।

Exam Tips

  • Begin the story with the given beginning exactly as it is written in the question, then continue it in your own words.
  • Keep the events in order: the rats trouble the town, the piper leads them away, the mayor breaks his promise, the piper leads the children away, and the town pays and apologises.
  • Write in the past tense, because the story happened in the past.
  • Do not change the main idea of the story. The mayor must break his promise first, and the piper must bring the children back only after the promise is kept.
  • End the story with the moral in a separate short paragraph, as shown above.
  • Do not add a year or other details you are not sure about. The story is a legend, so "long ago" is enough.
