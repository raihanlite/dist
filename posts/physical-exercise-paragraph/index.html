Physical Exercise Paragraph

February 15, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find a physical exercise paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version suits junior classes, while the longer versions suit SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Physical Exercise Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Physical exercise means moving the body in a planned and healthy way. Walking, running, swimming, cycling, gardening and playing games are all good forms of exercise. It makes our muscles strong, improves blood circulation, and helps us keep a healthy weight. It also keeps the mind fresh and cheerful.

A student who exercises regularly can study with better concentration. Exercise teaches discipline, patience and teamwork. Games teach us to follow rules and to accept both victory and defeat gracefully.

We should exercise every day, even if only for a short time. A morning walk or an evening game is enough for most students. We should warm up before exercise, drink enough water, and stop if we feel unwell. Good food and enough sleep are also necessary for good health.

In short, physical exercise keeps the body fit and the mind active. It is a habit that brings health, happiness and success in life.

Physical Exercise Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Physical exercise means moving the body in a planned and healthy way. Walking, running, swimming, cycling, gardening and playing outdoor games are all good forms of exercise. It makes our muscles strong, improves blood circulation, and helps us keep a healthy weight. It also keeps the mind fresh, cheerful and ready to learn. A healthy body is the foundation of a happy and useful life.

A student who exercises regularly can study with better concentration. Exercise teaches discipline, patience and teamwork. In games we learn to follow rules, to cooperate with others, and to accept both victory and defeat gracefully. These lessons are as valuable in life as they are on the playground. Regular exercise also improves sleep and reduces stress, which helps students perform better in examinations.

We should exercise every day, even if only for a short time. A morning walk, a short run or an evening game is enough for most students. We should warm up before exercise, drink enough water, and stop if we feel unwell. People who are old or weak should begin with gentle walking and stretching. Good food and enough sleep are also necessary for good health. Many forms of exercise are free, so lack of money need not prevent a healthy routine.

In short, physical exercise keeps the body fit and the mind active. It protects us from many diseases and fills life with energy. We should avoid sitting for long hours and make movement part of everyday life. A person who exercises regularly can work, study and enjoy life much better.

Physical Exercise Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Physical exercise means moving the body in a planned and healthy way. Walking, running, swimming, cycling, gardening and playing outdoor games are all good forms of exercise. It makes our muscles strong, improves blood circulation, and helps us keep a healthy weight. It also keeps the mind fresh, cheerful and ready to learn. A healthy body is the foundation of a happy and useful life, and exercise builds it.

A student who exercises regularly can study with better concentration. Exercise teaches discipline, patience and teamwork. In games we learn to follow rules, to cooperate with others, and to accept both victory and defeat gracefully. These lessons are as valuable in life as they are on the playground. Regular exercise also improves sleep, reduces stress and refreshes the mind, which helps students perform better in examinations. A student who plays outdoors often returns to study with a clearer mind.

We should exercise every day, even if only for a short time. A morning walk, a short run or an evening game is enough for most students. We should warm up before exercise, drink enough water, and stop if we feel unwell. People who are old or weak should begin with gentle walking and stretching. Good food and enough sleep are also necessary for good health. Many forms of exercise are free, so lack of money need not prevent a healthy routine.

In short, physical exercise keeps the body fit and the mind active. It protects us from many diseases, gives us energy for work and study, and fills life with joy. We should avoid sitting for long hours and make movement part of everyday life. A person who exercises regularly can work, study and enjoy life much better than one who lives a lazy life. So let us make exercise a daily habit from today.

Bangla Meaning of Physical Exercise Paragraph

শারীরিক ব্যায়ামের বাংলা অর্থ

শারীরিক ব্যায়াম মানে পরিকল্পিত ও স্বাস্থ্যকর উপায়ে শরীর নাড়াচাড়া করা। হাঁটা, দৌড়ানো, সাঁতার কাটা, সাইকেল চালানো, বাগান করা ও খেলাধুলা — সবই ভালো ব্যায়াম। ব্যায়াম পেশি শক্ত করে, রক্ত চলাচল ভালো করে এবং ওজন ঠিক রাখতে সাহায্য করে। এতে মনও সতেজ ও প্রফুল্ল থাকে।

যে ছাত্র নিয়মিত ব্যায়াম করে, সে পড়ায় বেশি মনোযোগ দিতে পারে। ব্যায়াম শৃঙ্খলা, ধৈর্য ও দলগত কাজ শেখায়। খেলায় আমরা নিয়ম মানতে, অন্যদের সঙ্গে মিলেমিশে চলতে এবং জয়-পরাজয় মেনে নিতে শিখি।

প্রতিদিন অল্প সময় হলেও ব্যায়াম করা উচিত। সকালে হাঁটা বা বিকেলে খেলা বেশিরভাগ ছাত্রের জন্য যথেষ্ট। ব্যায়ামের আগে শরীর গরম করে নেওয়া, পর্যাপ্ত পানি পান করা এবং অসুস্থ বোধ করলে থেমে যাওয়া উচিত। ভালো খাবার ও পর্যাপ্ত ঘুমও সুস্থতার জন্য দরকারি।

সংক্ষেপে, ব্যায়াম শরীর সুস্থ ও মন সক্রিয় রাখে। তাই ব্যায়ামকে আমাদের দৈনন্দিন অভ্যাস করা উচিত।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — benefits, discipline, daily routine — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block. Do not break it into many small paragraphs unless the question asks for it.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
