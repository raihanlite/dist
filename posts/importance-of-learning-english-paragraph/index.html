Importance of Learning English paragraph

October 15, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find importance of learning English paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Importance of Learning English Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

English is an international language. It is used in education, science, business, travel, technology and communication all over the world. It is the main language of the internet, international meetings and most scientific books. A student who knows English can read more books, follow online lessons and talk with people from many countries.

English is also useful for higher study and for many careers. Students can apply for scholarships, read research papers and work with people from other countries. In Bangladesh, good English helps in jobs, examinations and interviews. Many good books and courses are available only in English.

Learning English takes regular practice. Students should read simple texts, listen carefully, speak without fear and write a little every day. Mistakes are a natural part of learning. English should not replace our mother tongue; it should add another useful way to express ourselves.

Importance of Learning English Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

English is an international language. It is used in education, science, business, travel, technology and communication all over the world. It is the main language of the internet, international meetings and most scientific books. A student who knows English can read more books, follow online lessons and talk with people from many countries. When we know English, the whole world becomes a classroom.

English is also useful for higher study and for many careers. Students can apply for scholarships, read research papers and work with people from other countries. In Bangladesh, good English helps in jobs, examinations and interviews. Many good books and courses are available only in English, so a learner who knows English can keep learning throughout life. Employers often look for English skills, and many government and private jobs require them. It opens doors that stay closed for others.

Learning English takes regular practice rather than memorising a few difficult words. Students should read simple texts, listen carefully, speak without fear and write a little every day. Mistakes are a natural part of learning, and they should not stop us from trying. Reading English newspapers and storybooks, watching English programmes with subtitles, and speaking with classmates are simple habits that build skill day by day. English should not replace our mother tongue; it should add another useful way to express ourselves. With patience and meaningful practice, learners can use English confidently while respecting their own language and culture.

Importance of Learning English Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

English is an international language. It is used in education, science, business, travel, technology and communication all over the world. It is the main language of the internet, international meetings and most scientific books. A student who knows English can read more books, follow online lessons and talk with people from many countries. When we know English, the whole world becomes a classroom. A traveller who knows English can ask for help, read signs and understand people in almost any country.

English is also useful for higher study and for many careers. Students can apply for scholarships, read research papers and work with people from other countries. In Bangladesh, good English helps in jobs, examinations and interviews. Many good books and courses are available only in English, so a learner who knows English can keep learning throughout life. Employers often look for English skills, and many government and private jobs require them. It opens doors that stay closed for others. Scientists, doctors and engineers share their work in English, so a student who reads English can learn from the best minds in the world.

Learning English takes regular practice rather than memorising a few difficult words. Students should read simple texts, listen carefully, speak without fear and write a little every day. Mistakes are a natural part of learning, and they should not stop us from trying. Reading English newspapers and storybooks, watching English programmes with subtitles, and speaking with classmates are simple habits that build skill day by day. English should not replace our mother tongue; it should add another useful way to express ourselves. With patience and meaningful practice, learners can use English confidently while respecting their own language and culture. In the end, English is not a difficult subject to fear but a useful skill to practise every day.

Bangla Meaning of Importance of Learning English Paragraph

ইংরেজি শেখার গুরুত্বের বাংলা অর্থ

ইংরেজি একটি আন্তর্জাতিক ভাষা। শিক্ষা, বিজ্ঞান, ব্যবসা, ভ্রমণ ও প্রযুক্তিতে সারা বিশ্বে এটি ব্যবহৃত হয়। ইংরেজি জানলে বেশি বই পড়া যায়, অনলাইন পাঠ অনুসরণ করা যায় এবং নানা দেশের মানুষের সঙ্গে কথা বলা যায়।

উচ্চশিক্ষা ও চাকরির ক্ষেত্রেও ইংরেজি কাজে লাগে। নিয়মিত চর্চা করলে ইংরেজি শেখা যায়—সহজ লেখা পড়া, মনোযোগ দিয়ে শোনা, ভয় না পেয়ে বলা এবং প্রতিদিন একটু লেখা। ভুল করা শেখারই অংশ; ভুলের ভয়ে কথা বলা বন্ধ করা উচিত নয়।

ইংরেজি আমাদের মাতৃভাষার জায়গা নেবে না; এটি আমাদের আরেকটি দরকারি প্রকাশের মাধ্যম দেবে। ধৈর্য ও নিয়মিত চর্চায় ইংরেজি আত্মবিশ্বাসের সঙ্গে ব্যবহার করা যায়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — where English is used, its value for study and jobs, and how to learn it — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Mention that English should add to our mother tongue, not replace it. Examiners like this balanced point.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
