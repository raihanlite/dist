Price Hike Paragraph for Class 6,7,8, SSC,HSC

December 22, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 1 min read

On this page you will find a price hike paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version suits junior classes, while the longer versions suit SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Price Hike Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Price hike means a sustained rise in the prices of everyday goods and services. When rice, vegetables, fuel, medicine, rent or transport becomes more expensive, families must spend more money for the same standard of living. The burden is heaviest for poor people and workers whose incomes do not rise with the market.

Prices may rise because of inflation, higher fuel and production costs, floods or other disasters, shortages, transport problems, hoarding and excessive profit-taking. A disruption in one part of the supply chain can raise the final price in the shop. The result can be anxiety, less nutritious food, postponed treatment and greater poverty.

The government can respond by ensuring adequate supply, improving storage and transport, monitoring markets, taking action against illegal hoarding, and helping families in genuine difficulty. Consumers should compare prices, avoid waste, and not create artificial shortages by panic buying. A coordinated effort can protect both producers and buyers while the economy stabilises.

Price Hike Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Price hike means a sustained rise in the prices of everyday goods and services. When rice, vegetables, fuel, medicine, rent or transport becomes more expensive, families must spend more money for the same standard of living. The burden is heaviest for poor people and workers whose incomes do not rise with the market. Even a small rise in the price of rice or cooking oil can upset the monthly budget of a poor family.

Prices may rise because of inflation, higher fuel and production costs, floods or other disasters, shortages, transport problems, hoarding and excessive profit-taking. A disruption in one part of the supply chain can raise the final price in the shop. For example, when floods damage crops, the supply of vegetables falls and prices climb. When fuel becomes dearer, the cost of carrying goods also rises.

The result of a price hike is painful for ordinary people. Families cut down on nutritious food, delay medical treatment, and reduce spending on education. Children may suffer from poor nutrition, and the elderly may skip their medicines. Anxiety grows in every household, and poverty increases. The gap between the rich and the poor becomes wider.

The government can respond by ensuring adequate supply, improving storage and transport, monitoring markets, taking action against illegal hoarding, and helping families in genuine difficulty. Consumers should compare prices, avoid waste, and not create artificial shortages by panic buying. A coordinated effort can protect both producers and buyers while the economy stabilises.

Price Hike Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Price hike means a sustained rise in the prices of everyday goods and services. When rice, vegetables, fuel, medicine, rent or transport becomes more expensive, families must spend more money for the same standard of living. The burden is heaviest for poor people and workers whose incomes do not rise with the market. Even a small rise in the price of rice or cooking oil can upset the monthly budget of a poor family. A family that once lived comfortably on its income may suddenly find it hard to buy the same amount of food.

Prices may rise because of inflation, higher fuel and production costs, floods or other disasters, shortages, transport problems, hoarding and excessive profit-taking. A disruption in one part of the supply chain can raise the final price in the shop. For example, when floods damage crops, the supply of vegetables falls and prices climb. When fuel becomes dearer, the cost of carrying goods also rises.

The result of a price hike is painful for ordinary people. Families cut down on nutritious food, delay medical treatment, and reduce spending on education. Children may suffer from poor nutrition, and the elderly may skip their medicines. Anxiety grows in every household, and poverty increases. The gap between the rich and the poor becomes wider, which harms the whole society.

The government can respond by ensuring adequate supply, improving storage and transport, monitoring markets, taking action against illegal hoarding, and helping families in genuine difficulty. Farmers and small traders also need fair access to finance and markets so that they can sell at reasonable prices. Consumers should compare prices, avoid waste, and not create artificial shortages by panic buying. A coordinated effort can protect both producers and buyers while the economy stabilises.

Bangla Meaning of Price Hike Paragraph

মূল্যবৃদ্ধির বাংলা অর্থ

মূল্যবৃদ্ধি মানে নিত্যপ্রয়োজনীয় জিনিসপত্র ও সেবার দামের টানা বৃদ্ধি। চাল, সবজি, জ্বালানি, ওষুধ, ভাড়া বা পরিবহনের খরচ বাড়লে পরিবারকে একই জীবনযাত্রার জন্য বেশি টাকা খরচ করতে হয়। গরিব মানুষ ও যাদের আয় বাজারের সঙ্গে বাড়ে না, তাদের কষ্ট সবচেয়ে বেশি।

দাম বাড়তে পারে মূল্যস্ফীতি, জ্বালানি ও উৎপাদন খরচ বৃদ্ধি, বন্যা বা অন্য দুর্যোগ, ঘাটতি, পরিবহন সমস্যা, মজুতদারি ও অতিরিক্ত মুনাফার কারণে। এর ফলে পরিবার পুষ্টিকর খাবার কম খায়, চিকিৎসা পিছিয়ে দেয় এবং দারিদ্র্য বাড়ে।

সরকার পর্যাপ্ত সরবরাহ নিশ্চিত করে, বাজার তদারকি করে ও অবৈধ মজুতদারির বিরুদ্ধে ব্যবস্থা নিয়ে পরিস্থিতি সামাল দিতে পারে। ভোক্তাদের উচিত দাম যাচাই করা, অপচয় এড়ানো এবং আতঙ্কে কিনে কৃত্রিম ঘাটতি তৈরি না করা।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — causes, effects on families, and remedies — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block. Do not break it into many small paragraphs unless the question asks for it.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
