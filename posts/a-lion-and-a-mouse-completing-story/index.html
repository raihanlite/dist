A Lion and a Mouse Completing Story for Class 6,7,8, SSC, HSC

December 01, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 5 min read

This is the completing story of a lion and a mouse, written for Class 6 to 10, SSC and HSC students. The given beginning is: "Once, a lion was sleeping under a tree in a forest. A little mouse came out of its hole and began to run over the lion's body." Read the complete story below, then learn the moral, the Bangla meaning and the important words at the end.

A Lion and a Mouse (Complete Story)

Once, a lion was sleeping under a tree in a forest. A little mouse came out of its hole and began to run over the lion's body. The mouse was playing happily and did not notice that the lion had woken up. Suddenly, the lion caught the little creature with his strong paw and prepared to punish it.

The mouse was very frightened. It begged for freedom in a trembling voice. "Please let me go, O King," it said. "I am very small, and I am not good food for you. If you spare my life, I may be able to help you someday."

The lion laughed at the unlikely idea. How could such a small creature help the king of the forest? But the mouse looked so helpless that the lion felt pity. He opened his paw and let the mouse go. "Run away," he said, "and do not disturb my sleep again."

The mouse thanked the lion again and again and ran back to its hole. The lion soon forgot the whole matter and went back to sleep.

Days passed, and the forest returned to its usual calm. The lion hunted, rested, and walked through his territory without fear. He did not remember the small mouse at all. But the mouse remembered the lion's kindness and often told its family how the king of the forest had spared its life.

Some time later, hunters came to the forest and set a strong net to catch the lion. That night, the lion walked into the trap. The ropes held him fast, and he could not free himself. He roared loudly in anger and fear, and his roar was heard far away.

The little mouse heard the roar and hurried to the place. It saw the lion caught in the net and remembered the lion's kindness. Without wasting time, it began to gnaw through the ropes with its sharp teeth, one strand at a time. The work was slow, but the mouse did not stop. At last, the net was cut, and the lion was free.

The lion thanked the mouse warmly. He understood that size does not determine usefulness. Everyone has abilities that may become valuable in an unexpected situation. Kindness also creates bonds that return when help is needed. From that day, the lion and the mouse became good friends.

Moral

Never despise a small friend; kindness is rarely wasted.

Bangla Meaning of the Story

গল্পটির বাংলা অর্থ

একদিন একটি সিংহ বনের একটি গাছের নিচে ঘুমাচ্ছিল। একটি ছোট ইঁদুর তার গর্ত থেকে বের হয়ে সিংহের শরীরের উপর দৌড়াতে লাগল। সিংহ জেগে উঠে ইঁদুরটিকে ধরে ফেলল। ইঁদুর ভয়ে কাঁপতে কাঁপতে প্রাণভিক্ষা চাইল এবং বলল, একদিন হয়তো সে সিংহের উপকারে আসতে পারবে।

সিংহ হাসলেও দয়া করে ইঁদুরটিকে ছেড়ে দিল। কিছুদিন পর শিকারিরা সিংহকে শক্ত জালে আটকে ফেলল। সিংহের গর্জন শুনে ইঁদুর ছুটে এলো এবং তার ধারালো দাঁত দিয়ে একটু একটু করে জালের দড়ি কেটে সিংহকে মুক্ত করল। তখন সিংহ বুঝল, ছোট বলে কাউকে তুচ্ছ করা উচিত নয়। দয়া কখনো বৃথা যায় না।

এরপর বনে আবার শান্তি ফিরে এলো। সিংহ শিকার করত, বিশ্রাম নিত এবং নির্ভয়ে ঘুরে বেড়াত। সে ছোট ইঁদুরের কথা ভুলেই গিয়েছিল। কিন্তু ইঁদুর সিংহের দয়া ভোলেনি এবং প্রায়ই তার পরিবারকে বলত, বনের রাজা কীভাবে তার প্রাণ রক্ষা করেছিলেন।

Exam Tips

  • Begin the story with the given beginning exactly as it is written in the question, then continue it in your own words.
  • Keep the events in order: the lion catches the mouse, the mouse begs for freedom, the lion frees it, the hunters trap the lion, and the mouse cuts the net.
  • Write in the past tense, because the story happened in the past.
  • Do not change the main idea of the story. The mouse must save the lion by its own small ability.
  • End the story with the moral in a separate short paragraph, as shown above.
  • Use the lion's and the mouse's feelings in your sentences. Words like frightened, pity and grateful make the story lively.
