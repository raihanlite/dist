Tree Plantation paragraph

May 08, 2026 by Study Koro · 1 min read

On this page you will find a tree plantation paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Tree Plantation Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

Tree plantation means planting trees in suitable places and looking after them until they grow. Trees give us oxygen, shade, fruit and timber. They also keep the soil firm with their roots, cool the air with their leaves, and give birds and animals a home. Trees keep our air clean and our weather balanced.

We can plant trees beside roads, around schools and ponds, and on unused land. Fruit trees give food and income, while big shade trees give comfort. We should choose healthy saplings, prepare the soil well, water them regularly and protect them from goats. Planting a tree is easy, but caring for it is the real work.

We must stop needless cutting and plant new trees in their place. Schools can hold a tree plantation programme, but the saplings need care all year, not only on that day. If every family plants a few trees, our country will be greener and healthier.

Tree Plantation Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

Tree plantation means planting trees in suitable places and caring for them until they grow into strong, useful trees. It is very important for Bangladesh because trees give us oxygen, shade, fruit, timber, fuel and medicine. Their roots hold the soil firm and stop it from washing away in rain and floods. Their leaves cool the air and give shelter to birds and other animals. Without enough trees, the air becomes hot and dry.

We can plant trees in many places: beside roads, around schools, colleges and ponds, in home gardens and on unused land. Fruit trees like mango, jackfruit and guava give food and extra income to a family. Big shade trees give comfort to travellers and workers in hot weather. Timber trees give wood for houses and furniture. Before planting, we should choose healthy saplings, prepare the soil well, and plant them at the right time, usually in the rainy season when water is easy to get.

Planting is only the first step. A young tree needs water, fencing and protection from goats and cattle. Many trees die because nobody looks after them after the planting day. So schools, clubs and local people should take responsibility for the trees they plant and check them regularly. We must also stop cutting trees without need and plant new ones in place of the trees we cut. If every family plants and protects a few trees, our villages and towns will become greener, cooler, cleaner and healthier for everyone.

Tree Plantation Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

Tree plantation means planting trees in suitable places and looking after them until they grow into strong and useful trees. It is one of the most important duties of the people of Bangladesh. Trees give us oxygen, shade, fruit, timber, fuel and medicine. Their roots hold the soil together and protect it from erosion. Their leaves take in carbon dioxide, give out oxygen and cool the air around them. Birds, bees and many other animals live on trees, so trees also protect the natural balance of our country.

There are many suitable places for planting trees. We can plant them beside roads, around schools and ponds, in home gardens, on river banks and on unused land. Fruit trees such as mango, jackfruit, litchi and guava give food and income to families. Timber trees such as teak, mahogany and rain tree give valuable wood. Medicinal plants like neem and tulsi are also useful. Before planting, we should select healthy saplings, prepare the soil well, and plant them at the proper time. The rainy season is the best time because young trees get water easily and grow fast.

However, planting a tree is not enough. A sapling needs regular water, protection from goats and cattle, and care for several years. Many trees die because people forget them after the planting ceremony. The government has also set a target of planting 25 crore trees in five years, which shows how seriously the country now takes this work. But no programme can succeed if saplings are left uncared for. Every school, office and family should take responsibility for the trees it plants. We must stop cutting trees without need, and plant two more whenever one is cut. If we all do this, Bangladesh will be greener and healthier, and future generations will thank us.

Bangla Meaning of Tree Plantation Paragraph

বৃক্ষরোপণের বাংলা অর্থ

বৃক্ষরোপণ মানে উপযুক্ত জায়গায় গাছ লাগানো এবং বড় না হওয়া পর্যন্ত তার যত্ন নেওয়া। গাছ আমাদের অক্সিজেন, ছায়া, ফল, কাঠ ও ওষুধ দেয়। গাছের শিকড় মাটি ধরে রাখে, পাতা বাতাস ঠান্ডা করে এবং পাখি ও অন্যান্য প্রাণীর আশ্রয় হয়। রাস্তার পাশে, স্কুলের চারপাশে, পুকুরের ধারে, বাড়ির বাগানে ও পতিত জমিতে গাছ লাগানো যায়। ফলের গাছ খাবার ও আয় দেয়, আর বড় ছায়াদার গাছ গরমে আরাম দেয়। সুস্থ চারা বেছে নিয়ে মাটি তৈরি করে নিয়মিত পানি দিতে হয় এবং ছাগল-গরু থেকে রক্ষা করতে হয়। গাছ লাগানো সহজ, কিন্তু তার যত্ন নেওয়াই আসল কাজ। অপ্রয়োজনে গাছ কাটা বন্ধ করে কাটা গাছের বদলে নতুন গাছ লাগাতে হবে। প্রতিটি পরিবার কয়েকটি গাছ লাগিয়ে যত্ন নিলে আমাদের দেশ আরও সবুজ, ঠান্ডা ও স্বাস্থ্যকর হবে।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Learn the main points — benefits of trees, where to plant, how to care — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word; practise writing it once from memory.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Keep the paragraph in one block and revise it once the night before the exam.
