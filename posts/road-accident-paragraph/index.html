Road Accident Paragraph

September 03, 2025 by Mohasin Islam · 6 min read

On this page you will find road accident paragraph in 150, 250 and 300 words for Class 6 to 12, SSC and HSC. The short version is best for junior classes, and the longer versions are best for SSC and HSC. A Bangla meaning, important word meanings and exam tips are given at the end.

Road Accident Paragraph in 150 Words

Best for Class 6–7.

A road accident is a collision or other harmful incident involving vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists or roadside objects. It may happen on a highway, street, bridge or village road. Every day, road accidents take lives and injure people in our country. Speeding, reckless overtaking, driving while tired, using a phone and ignoring signals are common causes.

Poor lighting, damaged roads, untrained drivers and careless pedestrians add to the danger. Accidents cause death, injury, disability, emotional pain and financial loss. A single crash can affect an entire family and delay traffic for thousands of people. No punishment after a crash can bring back a lost life.

Safety begins with behaviour. Drivers should obey speed limits, wear seat belts or helmets, avoid alcohol and phones, and never drive when exhausted. Pedestrians should use footpaths and crossings and look carefully before crossing. Authorities must also enforce licensing rules, repair roads and improve signs and lighting. Road safety is a shared responsibility.

Road Accident Paragraph in 250 Words

Best for Class 8–10 and SSC.

A road accident is a collision or other harmful incident involving vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists or roadside objects. It may happen on a highway, street, bridge or village road. Every day, road accidents take lives and injure people in our country. Most accidents do not happen by chance; they happen because someone breaks a rule. Speeding, reckless overtaking, driving while tired, using a phone and ignoring signals are common causes. Driving an unfit vehicle also causes many crashes.

Poor lighting, damaged roads, untrained drivers and careless pedestrians add to the danger. Accidents cause death, injury, disability, emotional pain and financial loss. A single crash can affect an entire family and delay traffic for thousands of people. No punishment after a crash can bring back a lost life. Children crossing roads near schools are in special danger, so drivers must slow down there. A family that loses its earning member may fall into poverty for years.

Safety begins with behaviour. Drivers should obey speed limits, wear seat belts or helmets, avoid alcohol and phones, and never drive when exhausted. Pedestrians should use footpaths and crossings and look carefully before crossing. Authorities must also enforce licensing rules, inspect vehicles, repair roads, improve signs and lighting, and educate the public. Emergency services should reach crash sites quickly. Schools and parents should teach children road rules from an early age. Road safety is a shared responsibility, and patience can save lives. A careful driver is a good citizen.

Road Accident Paragraph in 300 Words

Best for SSC and HSC.

A road accident is a collision or other harmful incident involving vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists or roadside objects. It may happen on a highway, street, bridge or village road. Every day, road accidents take lives and injure people in our country. Most accidents do not happen by chance; they happen because someone breaks a rule. Speeding, reckless overtaking, driving while tired, using a phone and ignoring signals are common causes. Driving an unfit vehicle also causes many crashes. A driver who respects the speed limit respects the lives of others.

Poor lighting, damaged roads, untrained drivers and careless pedestrians add to the danger. Accidents cause death, injury, disability, emotional pain and financial loss. A single crash can affect an entire family and delay traffic for thousands of people. No punishment after a crash can bring back a lost life. Children crossing roads near schools are in special danger, so drivers must slow down there. A family that loses its earning member may fall into poverty for years. Prevention is always better than regret. After an accident, the injured need quick help, and bystanders should call for an ambulance at once.

Safety begins with behaviour. Drivers should obey speed limits, wear seat belts or helmets, avoid alcohol and phones, and never drive when exhausted. Pedestrians should use footpaths and crossings and look carefully before crossing. Authorities must also enforce licensing rules, inspect vehicles, repair roads, improve signs and lighting, and educate the public. Emergency services should reach crash sites quickly. Schools and parents should teach children road rules from an early age. Road safety is a shared responsibility, and patience can save lives. A careful driver is a good citizen. If drivers, pedestrians and authorities each do their part, our roads can become much safer.

Bangla Meaning of Road Accident Paragraph

সড়ক দুর্ঘটনা অনুচ্ছেদের বাংলা অর্থ

সড়ক দুর্ঘটনা মানে যানবাহন, পথচারী বা রাস্তার পাশের বস্তুর সঙ্গে সংঘর্ষ বা ক্ষতিকর ঘটনা। অতিরিক্ত গতি, বেপরোয়া ওভারটেকিং, ক্লান্ত অবস্থায় গাড়ি চালানো ও মোবাইল ব্যবহার এর সাধারণ কারণ। খারাপ আলো, ভাঙা রাস্তা ও অপ্রশিক্ষিত চালক বিপদ আরও বাড়ায়।

দুর্ঘটনায় মৃত্যু, আঘাত, পঙ্গুত্ব, মানসিক কষ্ট ও আর্থিক ক্ষতি হয়। একটি দুর্ঘটনা পুরো পরিবারকে ক্ষতিগ্রস্ত করতে পারে।

চালকদের গতি সীমা মানা, সিটবেল্ট বা হেলমেট পরা এবং ক্লান্ত অবস্থায় গাড়ি না চালানো উচিত। পথচারীদের ফুটপাত ও জেব্রা ক্রসিং ব্যবহার করা উচিত। সড়ক নিরাপত্তা সবার যৌথ দায়িত্ব। ধৈর্য ও নিয়ম মানলে জীবন বাঁচানো যায়।

Exam Tips

  • Choose the version that matches your class: 150 words for Class 6–7, 250 words for Class 8–10 and SSC, 300 words for SSC and HSC.
  • Do not memorise the whole paragraph word by word. Learn the main points — causes of accidents, their effects, and what drivers, pedestrians and authorities should do — and write them in your own sentences.
  • Cover all three sides of the solution: careful drivers, careful pedestrians, and strict enforcement by authorities.
  • Use the Bangla meaning to understand difficult words before you memorise the English version.
  • Revise the paragraph once the night before the exam instead of reading it for the first time in the hall.
