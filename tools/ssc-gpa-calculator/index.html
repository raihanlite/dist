SSC GPA Calculator

Calculate your SSC GPA for Science, Commerce, and Arts across all Bangladesh education boards — using the board's own formula, including the 4th-subject bonus. Enter a letter grade or marks for each subject.

Core subjects (9)

4th subject (bonus)

Only the grade points above C (2.00) in your 4th subject are added as a bonus. A low grade there never lowers your GPA.

Note: This calculator provides an estimate based on the Bangladesh SSC grading system. Your official GPA is the result shown on the marksheet issued by the relevant education board.

How SSC GPA is calculated

SSC uses a 5.00-point scale. Each subject's marks become a letter grade and a grade point:

MarksLetter GradeGrade Point
80–100A+5.00
70–79A4.00
60–69A−3.50
50–59B3.00
40–49C2.00
33–39D1.00
0–32F0.00

The formula is: GPA = (grade points of the 9 core subjects + 4th-subject bonus) ÷ 9, capped at 5.00. The 4th subject is never in the divisor — only its bonus counts.

Worked example

A Science student with these core grades — Bangla A+ (5), English A (4), Math A+ (5), Religion A+ (5), ICT A (4), Bangladesh & Global Studies A+ (5), Physics A (4), Chemistry A+ (5), Biology A (4) — has a core total of 41. Without a 4th subject: 41 ÷ 9 = 4.56.

With an A+ in Higher Math as the 4th subject, the bonus is 5.00 − 2.00 = 3.00, so (41 + 3.00) ÷ 9 = 4.89.

The 4th-subject bonus

Only the part of your 4th subject's grade point above 2.00 counts as a bonus:

  • A+ (5.00) → +3.00 bonus
  • A (4.00) → +2.00 bonus
  • A− (3.50) → +1.50 bonus
  • B (3.00) → +1.00 bonus
  • C (2.00) or below → no bonus

The 4th subject can only help. A low grade there never lowers your GPA, and an F in it does not fail you — it simply adds no bonus.

If you get an F

An F is a grade point of 0.00, and an F in any core subject makes the overall GPA 0.00. Your official result and the board's rules determine what happens next — check your official SSC result rather than relying on a GPA calculation alone.

Is this calculator official?

No. This is an educational calculator for estimating your GPA from your marks or grades. It is not an official Bangladesh Education Board result service. Your official SSC GPA is the result issued by your relevant education board and shown on your official marksheet — if the two differ, the board result is authoritative.
