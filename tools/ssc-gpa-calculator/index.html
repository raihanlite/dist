SSC GPA Calculator for All Education Boards in Bangladesh

Calculate your SSC GPA for Science, Commerce, and Arts across all Bangladesh education boards, using the board's own formula, including the 4th-subject bonus. Enter a letter grade or marks for each subject. See how your SSC GPA translates abroad in the GPA conversion guide.

How to Use the SSC GPA Calculator

  1. Select your group: Science, Commerce or Arts. The subject list changes to match your group.
  2. Enter a grade or marks for each of the nine core subjects. Marks are out of 100 for every subject.
  3. Add your 4th subject and its grade or marks, or leave it empty if you have none.
  4. Press Calculate GPA. Your final GPA, pass or fail status, total grade points and 4th subject bonus appear below.

SSC Grading System

SSC uses a 5.00-point scale. Every SSC subject is marked out of 100, so one table covers all subjects. Find your marks in the table to get the letter grade and grade point:

How SSC GPA is calculated

The formula is: GPA = (grade points of the 9 core subjects + 4th-subject bonus) ÷ 9, capped at 5.00. The 4th subject is never in the divisor, only its bonus counts.

Worked example

A Science student with these core grades: Bangla A+ (5), English A (4), Math A+ (5), Religion A+ (5), ICT A (4), Bangladesh & Global Studies A+ (5), Physics A (4), Chemistry A+ (5), Biology A (4), has a core total of 41. Without a 4th subject: 41 ÷ 9 = 4.56.

With an A+ in Higher Math as the 4th subject, the bonus is 5.00 − 2.00 = 3.00, so (41 + 3.00) ÷ 9 = 4.89.

Worked example 2: GPA with and without the 4th subject

Take a student whose nine core grade points are 5, 4, 5, 4, 5, 4, 5, 4 and 4, a total of 40. Without a 4th subject: 40 ÷ 9 = 4.44. With an A in the 4th subject, the bonus is 4.00 − 2.00 = 2.00, so (40 + 2.00) ÷ 9 = 4.67.

The 4th-subject bonus

Only the part of your 4th subject's grade point above 2.00 counts as a bonus:

  • A+ (5.00) → +3.00 bonus
  • A (4.00) → +2.00 bonus
  • A− (3.50) → +1.50 bonus
  • B (3.00) → +1.00 bonus
  • C (2.00) or below → no bonus

The 4th subject can only help. A low grade there never lowers your GPA, and an F in it does not fail you, it simply adds no bonus.

If you get an F

An F is a grade point of 0.00, and an F in any core subject makes the overall GPA 0.00. Your official result and the board's rules determine what happens next, so check your official SSC result rather than relying on a GPA calculation alone.

Combined SSC and HSC GPA

Many university admission circulars add your SSC GPA and your HSC GPA into a combined GPA out of 10.00. With SSC 4.80 and HSC 4.50, the combined GPA is 9.30. Each university sets its own minimum and its own weighting, and some do not count the 4th subject, so read the circular of the institution you apply to. Work out your HSC GPA with the HSC GPA Calculator.

Check your official result

The education boards publish official SSC results on educationboardresults.gov.bd. Use this calculator to check the GPA on your marksheet or to estimate it before you open it. If the two numbers differ, check the grade or marks you entered and whether any paper, MCQ, theory or practical part shows F. The board result is final. If you believe your marksheet has an error, the boards accept rescrutiny applications (board challenge) after the result, and each board announces its own dates and rules.

Is this calculator official?

No. This is an educational calculator for estimating your GPA from your marks or grades. It is not an official Bangladesh Education Board result service. Your official SSC GPA is the result issued by your relevant education board and shown on your official marksheet, if the two differ, the board result is authoritative.

Frequently asked questions

How is SSC GPA calculated in Bangladesh?

Subject results are converted into letter grades and grade points using the Bangladesh grading scale. The grade points from the 9 core subjects are averaged, with the 4th-subject bonus included when appropriate.

How does the 4th subject affect GPA?

If the optional subject's grade point is above 2.00, the amount above 2.00 is added as a bonus. For example, an A+ (5.00) in the 4th subject adds 3.00.

Can I calculate SSC GPA from marks?

Yes. Enter your marks out of 100 and the calculator converts them to the corresponding grade points.

Is SSC GPA divided by 9 or 10?

It is divided by 9. The 4th subject adds a bonus to the total and does not count as a tenth subject.

What marks do I need for A+ in SSC?

You need 80 out of 100 in the subject, subject to the pass requirements of each paper or component on your marksheet.

What happens if I fail one core subject?

Your overall result is Fail. Good grades in other subjects and the 4th subject bonus cannot cancel it. The calculator names the failed subject and shows no GPA.

Does failing the 4th subject fail me in SSC?

No. An F in the 4th subject adds no bonus, and your result stays a pass if all nine core subjects pass.

Can I get GPA 5.00 without A+ in every subject?

Yes. The 4th subject bonus can lift the total to 45.00 even when some core subjects are A or B. With a core total of 42.00 (for example, seven A+ grades, one A and one B: 35 + 4 + 3 = 42) and an A+ in the 4th subject, the total is 45.00 and the GPA is 5.00.

Which education boards does this calculator cover?

It supports Science, Commerce and Arts for the nine general education boards. Alim and technical boards may use different subject structures, so check your own marksheet and board rules.

What is the combined SSC and HSC GPA?

It is your SSC GPA plus your HSC GPA, out of 10.00. For example, 4.80 plus 4.50 gives 9.30. Each university or admission test sets its own minimum and weighting, so follow the circular of the institution you apply to.
