Tools

Free tools for planning your study abroad — IELTS, GPA and costs.

  • IELTS Band Score Calculator — Convert Listening and Reading raw scores to IELTS band scores.
  • CGPA Calculator — Calculate your CGPA for Bangladeshi universities.
  • SSC GPA Calculator — Calculate your SSC GPA for all boards.
  • HSC GPA Calculator — Calculate your HSC GPA, including the 4th subject bonus.
  • Study Abroad Cost Estimator — Estimate your first-year cost of studying abroad — tuition, living, visa, insurance and flights.

Calculator results are estimates. Always confirm official figures with the university, scholarship body or embassy before you pay anything.
