      How CGPA is calculated

      Bangladeshi universities use the University Grants Commission (UGC) uniform 4.00 scale:

      Marks (%)Letter GradeGrade Point
      80–100A+4.00
      75–79A3.75
      70–74A−3.50
      65–69B+3.25
      60–64B3.00
      55–59B−2.75
      50–54C+2.50
      45–49C2.25
      40–44D2.00
      Below 40F0.00

      Semester GPA = Σ (credit hours × grade point) ÷ Σ credit hours. Your CGPA is the same calculation over all your credits, including previous semesters.

      Worked example

      Three 3-credit courses: CSE 101 A+ (4.00), MAT 101 A (3.75), PHY 101 B+ (3.25). Grade points: 3 × 4.00 + 3 × 3.75 + 3 × 3.25 = 12 + 11.25 + 9.75 = 33. GPA = 33 ÷ 9 = 3.67.

      What class is my CGPA?

      On the commonly published UGC cut-offs: 3.00–4.00 is First Class, 2.25–2.99 is Second Class, 2.00–2.24 is Third Class. Your university may use slightly different lines — check your own syllabus, but 3.00 is the line that matters for most government jobs and graduate applications.

      Is the UGC scale the same everywhere?

      Most public universities, National University, and many private universities follow this scale. A few private universities publish their own table (for example, one where A is 4.00 and A− is 3.70). If your result looks different from your transcript, check your university's grading policy — your transcript legend is what counts.
