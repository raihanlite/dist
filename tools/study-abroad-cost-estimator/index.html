Study Abroad Cost Estimator

Estimate your first-year cost of studying abroad. Pick a country — the fields fill with typical figures — then replace them with your university's actual fees and see the total in the original currency and in approximate BDT.

What the defaults mean

  • Defaults are typical starting points, not quotes — your offer letter is the real tuition figure.
  • UK: the visa field includes the £558 application fee + one year of the Immigration Health Surcharge (£776). Maintenance money is proof, not a fee.
  • Germany: health insurance is inside the €992-a-month living figure, so the insurance field is 0. You must also hold €11,904 in a blocked account.
  • Japan, China, Malaysia: the visa field starts at 0 — check the embassy and type in the real fee.
  • BDT totals use mid-market rates of 19 Nov 2026 (USD ৳124.50, GBP ৳163.50, CAD ৳87.00, AUD ৳83.50, EUR ৳136.80, JPY ৳0.78, CNY ৳18.70, MYR ৳30.25). Rates move daily. Full comparison: Cost of Studying Abroad Compared.
