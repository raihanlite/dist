How HSC GPA is calculated

Bangladesh's HSC grading system uses a 5.00-point scale. Each subject's marks become a letter grade and a grade point:

MarksLetter GradeGrade Point
80–100A+5.00
70–79A4.00
60–69A−3.50
50–59B3.00
40–49C2.00
33–39D1.00
0–32F0.00

The GPA is the average of your main subjects' grade points, plus the 4th-subject bonus if you have one.

Worked example

SubjectGradeGrade Point
BanglaA+5.00
EnglishA4.00
ICTA+5.00
PhysicsA+5.00
ChemistryA4.00
BiologyA+5.00

Main subjects total 28 ÷ 6 = 4.67. With an A+ in the 4th subject, the bonus is (5.00 − 2.00) ÷ 2 = 1.50, so (28 + 1.50) ÷ 6 = 4.92.

The 4th-subject bonus

The optional (4th) subject is treated differently. If its grade point is above 2.00, half of the excess counts as a bonus:

  • A+ (5.00) → +1.50 bonus
  • A (4.00) → +1.00 bonus
  • A− (3.50) → +0.75 bonus
  • B (3.00) → +0.50 bonus
  • C (2.00) or below → no bonus

The calculator applies the bonus automatically — enter your optional subject separately and it is included.

If you get an F

An F is a grade point of 0.00, and under the board rules an F in any subject makes the overall GPA 0.00. Your official result and the board's rules determine what happens next — check your official HSC result rather than relying on a GPA calculation alone.

GPA is not a percentage

HSC results are reported as GPA, not as a percentage. There is no official formula that converts GPA to a percentage, so do not treat GPA × 20 as an exact percentage. If an institution asks for a percentage, check that institution's own requirement.

Is this calculator official?

No. This is an educational calculator for estimating your GPA from your marks or grades. It is not an official Bangladesh Education Board result service. Your official HSC GPA is the result issued by your relevant education board and shown on your official marksheet — if the two differ, the board result is authoritative.

Frequently asked questions

How is HSC GPA calculated in Bangladesh?

Subject results are converted into letter grades and grade points using the Bangladesh grading scale. The grade points from the main subjects are averaged, with the applicable 4th-subject bonus included when appropriate.

How does the 4th subject affect GPA?

If the optional subject's grade point is above 2.00, half of the amount above 2.00 is added as a bonus. For example, an A+ (5.00) in the 4th subject adds 1.50.

Can I calculate HSC GPA from marks?

Yes. Enter your marks and the calculator converts them to the corresponding grade points. Enter the total out of 200 for two-paper subjects, and out of 100 for ICT.

Is 5.00 the same as Golden A+?

Not exactly. 5.00 is the maximum GPA. "Golden A+" is an informal expression students use for receiving A+ grades across the relevant subjects, including the optional subject. It is not a separate GPA value.
