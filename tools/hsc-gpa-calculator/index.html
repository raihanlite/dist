How to Use the HSC GPA Calculator

  1. Pick your group tab: Science, Business Studies or Humanities. The subject list changes to match your group.
  2. Pick Grade or Marks at the top. Only the method you pick is shown, so the form stays short. Switching keeps what you already entered.
  3. Fill in each of the six main subjects. For marks, enter the combined total of both papers (200), or 100 for ICT. The code beside each subject is its board code, so you can match it to your marksheet.
  4. Add your 4th subject and its grade or marks, or leave it empty if you have none.
  5. Press Calculate GPA. Your final GPA, pass or fail status, total grade points and 4th subject bonus appear below.

HSC Grading System

Bangladesh's HSC grading system uses a 5.00-point scale. Most subjects are two papers (200 marks); ICT is one paper (100 marks). Find your marks in the table to get the letter grade and grade point:

How HSC GPA is calculated

The formula is: GPA = (grade points of the 6 main subjects + 4th-subject bonus) ÷ 6, capped at 5.00. The 4th subject is never in the divisor, only its bonus counts.

Worked example

SubjectGradeGrade Point
BanglaA+5.00
EnglishA4.00
ICTA+5.00
PhysicsA+5.00
ChemistryA4.00
BiologyA+5.00

Main subjects total 28 ÷ 6 = 4.67. With an A+ in the 4th subject, the bonus is 5.00 − 2.00 = 3.00, so (28 + 3.00) ÷ 6 = 5.17, capped at 5.00.

Worked example 2: GPA with and without the 4th subject

Take a student with A+ in Bangla, English and ICT, A in Physics and Chemistry, and A− in Biology. The main total is 5 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 3.5 = 26.50, so the GPA without a 4th subject is 26.50 ÷ 6 = 4.42. With an A in the 4th subject, the bonus is 4.00 − 2.00 = 2.00, so (26.50 + 2.00) ÷ 6 = 4.75.

The 4th-subject bonus

Only the part of your 4th subject's grade point above 2.00 counts as a bonus:

  • A+ (5.00) → +3.00 bonus
  • A (4.00) → +2.00 bonus
  • A− (3.50) → +1.50 bonus
  • B (3.00) → +1.00 bonus
  • C (2.00) or below → no bonus

The 4th subject can only help. A low grade there never lowers your GPA, and an F in it does not fail you, it simply adds no bonus.

If you get an F

An F is a grade point of 0.00, and under the board rules an F in any main subject makes the overall GPA 0.00. Your official result and the board's rules determine what happens next, so check your official HSC result rather than relying on a GPA calculation alone.

Combined SSC and HSC GPA

Many university admission circulars add your SSC GPA and your HSC GPA into a combined GPA out of 10.00. With SSC 4.80 and HSC 4.50, the combined GPA is 9.30. Each university sets its own minimum and its own weighting, and some do not count the 4th subject, so read the circular of the institution you apply to. Work out your SSC GPA with the SSC GPA Calculator.

GPA is not a percentage

HSC results are reported as GPA, not as a percentage. There is no official formula that converts GPA to a percentage, so do not treat GPA × 20 as an exact percentage. If an institution asks for a percentage, check that institution's own requirement.

Check your official result

The education boards publish official HSC results on educationboardresults.gov.bd. Use this calculator to check the GPA on your marksheet or to estimate it before you open it. If the two numbers differ, check the grade or marks you entered, the full marks (100 or 200) of each subject, and whether any paper, MCQ, theory or practical part shows F. The board result is final. If you believe your marksheet has an error, the boards accept rescrutiny applications (board challenge) after the result, and each board announces its own dates and rules.

Is this calculator official?

No. This is an educational calculator for estimating your GPA from your marks or grades. It is not an official Bangladesh Education Board result service. Your official HSC GPA is the result issued by your relevant education board and shown on your official marksheet, if the two differ, the board result is authoritative.

Frequently asked questions

How is HSC GPA calculated in Bangladesh?

Subject results are converted into letter grades and grade points using the Bangladesh grading scale. The grade points from the 6 main subjects are averaged, with the applicable 4th-subject bonus included when appropriate.

How does the 4th subject affect GPA?

If the optional subject's grade point is above 2.00, the amount above 2.00 is added as a bonus. For example, an A+ (5.00) in the 4th subject adds 3.00.

Can I calculate HSC GPA from marks?

Yes. Pick Marks at the top and enter your marks. Enter the total out of 200 for two-paper subjects, and out of 100 for ICT. The calculator converts each one to a percentage first, then to a grade point.

Should I use grades or marks?

Either works, the result is the same. Use grades if you only know the letter grades from your marksheet. Use marks if you know the numbers. Pick one method at the top; the other hides so the form stays short. Switching keeps what you already entered.

What are the subject codes beside each subject?

They are the board codes printed on your marksheet and admit card, for example Physics 174/175 and ICT 275. Two-paper subjects show both paper codes. Use them to check you picked the right subject, especially when two subjects have similar names.

Why is Finance, Banking and Insurance a choice, not fixed?

Business Studies has three group subjects, but the third is either Finance, Banking and Insurance or Production Management and Marketing. Pick the one on your marksheet as a main subject; the other one can be your 4th subject.

Is 5.00 the same as Golden A+?

Not exactly. 5.00 is the maximum GPA. "Golden A+" is an informal expression students use for receiving A+ grades across the relevant subjects, including the optional subject. It is not a separate GPA value.

Is HSC GPA divided by 6 or 7?

It is divided by 6. The 4th subject adds a bonus to the total and does not count as a seventh subject.

What marks do I need for A+ in HSC?

You need 80 out of 100 or 160 out of 200 in the subject, subject to the pass requirements of each paper or component on your marksheet.

What happens if I fail one main subject?

Your overall result is Fail. Good grades in other subjects and the 4th subject bonus cannot cancel it. The calculator names the failed subject and shows 0.00.

Does failing the 4th subject fail me in HSC?

No. An F in the 4th subject adds no bonus, and your result stays a pass if all six main subjects pass.

Can I get GPA 5.00 without A+ in every subject?

Yes. The 4th subject bonus can lift the total to 30.00 even when some main subjects are A or A−. With main grade points of 5, 4, 5, 4, 5 and 4 (27.00) and an A+ in the 4th subject, the GPA is 5.00.

Which education boards does this calculator cover?

It supports Science, Humanities and Business Studies for the nine general education boards. Alim and technical boards may use different subject structures, so check your own marksheet and board rules.

How do I enter a subject with two papers?

Add both paper totals and enter the combined subject mark. Select 200 as the full marks if both papers are out of 100. Use the full marks printed on your marksheet.

What is the combined SSC and HSC GPA?

It is your SSC GPA plus your HSC GPA, out of 10.00. For example, 4.80 plus 4.50 gives 9.30. Each university or admission test sets its own minimum and weighting, so follow the circular of the institution you apply to.
