How the overall band is calculated

Your overall band is the average of the four module bands, rounded by the official IELTS rule: an average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, and .75 rounds up to the next whole band.

For example: Listening 30 correct (7.0), Reading 30 correct in Academic (7.0), Writing 6.5, Speaking 6.5. Average = (7.0 + 7.0 + 6.5 + 6.5) ÷ 4 = 6.75, which rounds up to 7.0.
