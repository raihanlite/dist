Attendance Calculator

Find out how many classes you can skip while maintaining your target attendance percentage, or how many classes you need to attend to reach your target percentage.

Mode

How to use it

  1. Pick a mode. "Classes held so far" is for tracking what's happened up to today. "Full term" is for when you already know how many classes the whole course runs for.

  2. Set your target percentage. Most universities set this at 75%, but the dropdown covers 60% to 90%.

  3. Fill in your numbers and hit Calculate. After that, the result updates automatically as you edit any field.
