UK Student Visa from Bangladesh — CAS, Fees, IHS and the 28-Day Rule

19 Nov 2026 · 8 min read · Last checked 19 Nov 2026

The UK Student visa is for students aged 16 or over who have an unconditional offer from a licensed UK university or college. For a Bangladeshi applicant, the route is: get your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS), prepare your maintenance money, apply online on GOV.UK, pay the fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge, give biometrics at a Visa Application Centre, and wait for a decision — usually within three weeks.

The steps, in order

1. Get your CAS

Once you accept an unconditional offer and meet the university's conditions (including paying any deposit it asks for), the university issues your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies — a CAS reference number, not a paper document. The CAS states your course, its fees, and the documents the university used to assess you. You cannot apply for the visa without it, and the CAS is normally valid for six months.

2. Prove your English

For degree-level study you must show English at CEFR level B2 — roughly IELTS 5.5–6.0 in each component, though most universities ask for more (usually 6.0–6.5 overall). The test must be a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider if the university asks for one; many universities accept IELTS Academic taken at any centre. Check what your CAS says — it lists the English evidence the university relied on.

3. Hold your maintenance money for 28 days

You must show you can pay your first year's course fees (as stated on the CAS, minus any amount already paid) plus maintenance: £1,529 a month if your course is in London, or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to nine months. The money must be in your account — or your parent's or partner's account — for 28 consecutive days, and the 28th day must be no more than 31 days before you apply. A single day below the required amount restarts the count. This is the rule that causes the most refusals: count the days carefully.

4. Apply online and pay

Apply on GOV.UK from outside the UK. You pay the Immigration Health Surcharge first (£776 per year of your visa — £1,164 for a typical 12-month master's), then the £558 application fee. Both are paid online as part of the application. Each dependant pays the same fee and surcharge.

5. Give biometrics in Dhaka

After submitting online, you book an appointment at the Visa Application Centre in Dhaka (run by VFS Global) to give fingerprints and a photograph and hand in your passport and documents. You can also upload documents online before the appointment. A TB test certificate from an approved clinic is required for applicants from Bangladesh.

6. Wait for the decision

Most applications from outside the UK are decided within three weeks of biometrics. You can pay for priority service if it is available, but it does not guarantee approval. Your passport is returned with a vignette (sticker) valid for 90 days; you collect your Biometric Residence Permit (now an eVisa) after arriving in the UK.

The maintenance money is not a fee — it is your own money, and you spend it on living costs once you arrive. But you must be able to show it, untouched, for 28 days. If your course is longer than a year, you still only show up to nine months of maintenance plus the first year's fees.

Maintenance rises on 30 November 2026. Under Statement of Changes HC 584, the maintenance requirement rises to £1,570 a month in London and £1,203 a month elsewhere for applications made on or after 30 November 2026. Applications made before that date are decided under the current £1,529/£1,171 figures. If your 28-day period crosses the change, check which figure applies to your application date on GOV.UK before you apply.

Financial proof: what counts

  • Your own bank account — statements showing the full amount held for 28 consecutive days. The bank must be one UKVI accepts (most Bangladeshi banks are, but check the list).
  • A parent's account — allowed, but you must also provide your birth certificate and a signed letter from your parent confirming the money is for your studies.
  • An official scholarship or sponsorship letter — from a government, university, or recognised body, stating the amount covered.
  • An education loan — a loan letter from a regulated bank showing the amount available to you.
  • Money already paid to the university — shown on your CAS, this reduces the amount you need to show.

Do not use money that will be withdrawn the day after you apply. UKVI checks the balance on the closing date of your statements, and a shortfall of even £1 means refusal.

Common refusal reasons

ReasonHow to avoid it
Maintenance below the required amountHold the full amount for 28 days; add a buffer above the minimum
28-day rule brokenCount 28 consecutive days with no dip; apply within 31 days of the last statement date
Wrong or missing documentsUse the document checklist on your CAS; upload everything the application asks for
Credibility interviewBe ready to explain your course, university, finances and plans after study — in your own words
Previous immigration historyDeclare all past UK visa applications and travel honestly

Some applicants are called for a credibility interview by phone or video. It is not a test of your English — it is a check that your study plans are genuine. Know your course content, why you chose the university, and what you plan to do afterwards.

Working and staying after your course

Student visa holders may work up to 20 hours a week in term time and full time during holidays. After completing an eligible degree, you can apply for the Graduate visa from inside the UK — no sponsor or job offer needed. It costs £937 plus the Immigration Health Surcharge at the standard adult rate of £1,035 a year. The grant is two years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027; doctoral graduates still get three years. The Graduate visa cannot be extended — you must switch to another route, such as Skilled Worker, before it expires.

Documents checklist

  • Valid passport
  • CAS reference number from your university
  • Proof of funds held for 28 days (bank statements, sponsor letter, or loan letter)
  • English test result, if stated on your CAS
  • TB test certificate from an approved clinic
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (SSC, HSC, bachelor's if applicable)
  • ATAS certificate, if your course requires it (some science and engineering subjects)
  • Parental consent and birth certificate, if you are under 18 or using a parent's funds
  • Translations of any document not in English or Welsh
  • IHS payment confirmation and visa fee receipt
