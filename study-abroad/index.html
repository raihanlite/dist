Study Abroad from Bangladesh — the Complete Guide

Every year, thousands of Bangladeshi students go abroad to study — for a bachelor's degree, a master's, or a PhD. This guide explains the whole journey in order: choosing a country, finding scholarships, preparing your application, getting a visa, and budgeting the cost. No agent needed.

The 6 steps, in order

1. Choose your country and level

Start with your budget, not a ranking list. A bachelor's degree takes 3–4 years; a master's 1–2. Use the cost estimator to compare first-year totals, then read the country guides.

2. Check scholarships first

Full scholarships (MEXT, DAAD, Chevening, Australia Awards, CSC, Commonwealth) cover tuition and living costs — but their deadlines fall 8–12 months before the course starts. Check the scholarship guides before you shortlist universities.

3. Prepare your tests and documents

Most universities need an English test (IELTS 6.0–7.0 overall, depending on level and country), transcripts, a statement of purpose, and recommendation letters. See admissions for the IELTS vs TOEFL comparison, the SOP guide, and the full timeline.

4. Apply to universities

Apply to 4–6 universities: two ambitious, two realistic, two safe. Watch each university's own deadline — they differ, and late applications are usually not accepted.

5. Apply for your visa

Only after you have an offer and, where required, paid a deposit. Visa guides are in visas: fees, financial proof, documents and interviews. Budget extra time — interviews and processing can take weeks.

6. Plan money and travel

Tuition, living costs, visa fees, insurance, flights and the first month's expenses. The costs guides and the estimator give you a first-year total before you commit.

Documents checklist

  • Valid passport (at least 6 months beyond your planned stay)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (SSC, HSC, bachelor's if applicable)
  • English test result (IELTS / TOEFL / PTE, as the university requires)
  • Statement of purpose and recommendation letters
  • Offer letter from the university
  • Financial proof (bank statement, blocked account, GIC, or scholarship letter)
  • Health insurance (required in Germany, Australia and others)
  • Visa application form, fee receipt and photos

These are starting points, not quotes — your university's fees and your city decide the real number. Run your own numbers in the cost estimator.

Watch out: fees and immigration rules change often. The US added a $250 Visa Integrity Fee in 2025 that is still being phased in; the UK raised its maintenance requirement from 30 November 2026; Australia raised its student visa fee to AUD $2,500 in July 2026. Always confirm the current figure on the official source before you pay anything.
